# DLY Module - Delay Effects

## Pure - Digital Delay Effect
- Mix: Controls the wet/dry signal ration (1-100)
- Time: Controls the delay time (20ms - 1000ms) 
- F.Back: Controls the amount of feedback (1-100)
- Trail: Trail effect on/off when bypassed (On - Off)
