# RVB Module - Reverb effects

## Air - Airy reverb effect
- Mix: Controls the Wet/Dry signal ratio (1-100)
- Decay: Controls the duration of reverb time (1-100)
- Damp: Dampens the effect high frequency amount (1-100)
- Trail: Trail effect on/off when bypassed (On - Off)
