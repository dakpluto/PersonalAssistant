# Pre Module - Compression, Boost, Wah, and pitch effects

## COMP - Based on the Legendary Ross Compressor 
- Sustain: Controls the compression amount (1-100)
- VOL: Controls the effect output (1-100)

## COMP4 - Based on the Keeley C4 4-knob Compressor
- Sustain: Controls the compression amount (1-100)
- Attack: Controls how soon the compressor starts to process the signal (1-100)
- Clipping: Controls the Input Sesitivity (1-100)
- VOL: Controls the effect output (1-100)

## Boost - Based on famous Xotic EP Booster pedal
- Gain: controls the effect output/boost amount (1-100)
- +3dB: Selects the minimum boost amount from 0dB (off) to +3dB (on)
- Bright: Selects the sound character from vintage (off) to flat (on)

## MicroBoost - Based on the legendary MXR M133 Micro Amp2 Pedal
- Gain: controls the gain amount (1-100) 

## B-Boost - Based on the Xotic BB Preamp Overdrive Pedal
- Gain: control the distortion amount (1-100)
- VOL: Controls the effect output (1-100)
- Bass: Controls the Bass tone (1-100)
- Treble: Controls the Treble tone (1-100)

## Toucher - Wide Range Touch Sensative Envelope Filter designed for Guitar and Bass
- Sense: Controls the effect sensitivity (1-100)
- Range: Controls the frequency range of the filter (1-100)
- Q: Control the sharpness of the filter (1-100)
- Mix: Controls the wet/dry signal ratio (1-100)
- Mode: Guitar or Bass

## Crier - Auto Wah effect for both Guitar and Bass
- Depth: Controls the effect depth (1-100)
- Rate: Controls the effect speed (0.10Hz - 10.00Hz)
- VOL: Controls the effect output (1-100)
- Low: Controls the bottom point of center frequency (1-100)
- Q: Control the sharpness of the filter (1-100)
- High: Controls the top point of center frequency (1-100)

## Octa - PProvides polyphonic octave effect
- Low: Controls the volume of lower octave (1-100)
- High: Controls the volume of higher octave (1-100)
- Dry: Controls the dry signal level (1-100) 

## Pitch - Polyphonic pitch shifter/harmonizer
- High: Controls the high pitch shifting range by semitones (0 - 24)
- Low: Controls the low pitch shifting range by semitones (-24 - 0)
- Dry: Controls the dry signal level (1-100) 
- H-VOL: Controls the high pitch Volume (1-100)
- L-VOL: Controls the low pitch Volume (1-100)
