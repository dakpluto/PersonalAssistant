## AC Pre1 - Acoustic - Based on AER Colourizer 2 (Channel 1)
- VOL: Controls the output volume (1-100)
- Tone: Controls the brightness (1-100)
- Balance: Control the tone control balance.  Set to 0 to disable tone control (0-100)
- EQ Freq: Controls the EQ Center Frequency from 90Hz to 1.6kHz (1-100)
- EQ Q: Controls the EQ bandwidth (1-100)
- EQ Gain: Controls the EQ boost/cut amount.  Set to 50 to keep neutral (1-100)
