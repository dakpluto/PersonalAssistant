## Pairing notes

- **IR:** a Fender Deluxe 1x12 (Jensen C12N or Oxford). Nothing in `IRs/ir.md` is a direct match yet. The closest documented options are the Origin Effects "Brown Deluxe" 1x12 (1961 brownface Deluxe, Oxford 12K5) and "Tweed Combo" 1x12 (5E3, Jensen P12Q), both in the same small-Fender-1x12 family. A proper blackface Deluxe 1x12 IR is still the #1 guitar cab to find.
- **Covers:** the Deluxe Reverb slot, which is the #1 guitar amp on the priority list at 10 songs (the Brooks & Dunn parts, Heads Carolina, Believe, Red Dirt Road, American Pie).
- **Starting points:** Nashville clean → CLEANEST or CLEAN. Chicken-pickin' snap with a little hair → EDGY. Gritty country-rock rhythm (Hard Workin' Man, Red Dirt Road) → RYTHM or ROCKING.
