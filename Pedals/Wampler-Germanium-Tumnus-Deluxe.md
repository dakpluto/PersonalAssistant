# Wampler Germanium Tumnus Deluxe

Confirmed product name is "Germanium Tumnus Deluxe" (not a separately-branded product) — the expanded/deluxe version of Wampler's Germanium Tumnus, itself a germanium-diode variant of the Tumnus (Wampler's Klon Centaur-style transparent overdrive). The Deluxe adds a full 3-band EQ and a gain-boost switch on top of the base Germanium Tumnus's simpler 3-knob layout.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Amount of overdrive/drive.
- **Level**: Output volume.
- **Bass**: Low-frequency EQ control.
- **Mids**: Midrange EQ control.
- **Treble**: High-frequency EQ control.
- **Normal/Hot toggle switch**: Switches between the classic Tumnus gain range (Normal) and a hotter, more driven gain range (Hot) — described by Wampler as pushing more output and drive than a typical "Klone."
- **Buffer/True Bypass toggle switch** (side-mounted): Selects whether the pedal runs buffered bypass (adds a touch of extra sparkle/high-end when off) or true bypass (fully uncolored signal path when off).

## Notes for patch-building
- A transparent, germanium-flavored overdrive/boost — sits as a front-end gain/boost stage, typically ahead of an amp or a higher-gain distortion, adding warmth and low-end push rather than its own distinct distortion character (a "Klon-style" transparent boost/OD).
- The Normal/Hot switch effectively gives two overdrive voicings in one pedal (light boost vs. more overtly driven), similar in spirit to a two-channel drive pedal but switch-selected rather than footswitch-selected — it can't be toggled live via footswitch, so pick a setting per song/patch.
- The user-selectable buffer/true-bypass toggle is a real quirk worth knowing when placing it on a board with other bypass-sensitive gear: it can be set to either behavior depending on where it sits in the chain.
- Top-mounted input/output jacks; runs on 9V (battery or standard 9V DC).
