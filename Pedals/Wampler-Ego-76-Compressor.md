# Wampler Ego 76 Compressor

Studio-style compressor pedal modeled after classic FET studio compressors (in the vein of the Urei/UA 1176), bringing "console" compression controls — Attack, Release, and a parallel Blend — to a stompbox format.

## Controls
- **Compress**: Overall amount of compression applied (functions like a Ratio control — higher = more squash).
- **Attack**: How quickly the compression kicks in once a signal peak is detected.
- **Release**: How quickly the compression lets go after the signal drops.
- **Blend**: Parallel-compression control — mixes the fully compressed signal back with the original uncompressed (clean) signal, letting the initial transient/pick attack through while the compressed signal fills in sustain.
- **Tone**: Adds high-end sparkle/presence; fully counter-clockwise leaves the tone unaffected, turning clockwise brightens the signal.
- **Level**: Overall output volume.

## Notes for patch-building
- A front-of-chain dynamics/sustain tool — typically placed early in the signal path (before drive/distortion stages) to even out picking dynamics and add sustain, similar in role to a compressor stage on other boards but with studio-grade Attack/Release/Blend control the GP-5 doesn't expose on a single comp block.
- The Blend knob is the standout feature: dialing in full wet gives classic "squashed" studio compression, while a partial blend preserves pick attack/dynamics for players who find full compression too sterile.
- **True bypass** (3PDT switching) — safe to leave fully out of the signal path with no coloration when off.
