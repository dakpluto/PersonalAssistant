# Strymon Deco V2

Dual-purpose pedal combining a tape-saturation/texture stage modeled on reel-to-reel and cassette machines with an independent "doubletracker" delay effect (two short, modulatable "tape decks" blended together to simulate the classic double-tracking studio trick), each with its own footswitch.

## Controls
- **Tone**: Overall saturation tone, dark to bright
- **Volume**: Output level of the tape-saturation stage
- **Saturation**: Amount of tape drive/dynamic compression/distortion
- **Blend**: Mix between the doubletracker's two virtual tape decks; 12 o'clock = 50/50
- **Lag Time**: Delay offset between the two doubletracker decks
- **Wobble**: Random modulation applied to the Lag Time setting, for tape-style pitch wander

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Voice** (mini-toggle, 2-position): Classic (2-track mastering reel-to-reel response) or Cassette (auto-level-control process for a compressed, fatter tone)
- **Blend Type** (mini-toggle, 3-position): Sum (decks in phase), Invert (lag deck phase-inverted), or Bounce (right/lag deck bounced to the left channel for stereo ping-pong or mono double-echo)
- **Input Select** (rear-panel toggle, 2-position): Mono or Stereo, matching the input signal
- **Tape Saturation footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the saturation stage
- **Doubletracker footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the doubletracker delay stage; hold to engage Auto-Flange

## Notes for patch-building
- Two effects in one pedal that don't map to a single GP-5 slot: the Tape Saturation side is a texture/drive stage (fits ahead of AMP, similar role to a low-gain drive or an always-on "glue" stage), while the Doubletracker side is a short modulated delay (fits near the GP-5's DLY or MOD slot depending on the sound wanted).
- Both stages can run together or independently since they have separate footswitches — a natural CTL pairing (saturation always on, doubletracker footswitched for choruses/hooks).
- Stereo in/out via TRS input (Class A JFET preamp) and two independent TS outputs; has an EXP/MIDI jack and USB-C.
- Power: 9VDC center-negative, 300mA minimum (supply not included).
