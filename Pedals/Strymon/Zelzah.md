# Strymon Zelzah

Dual-channel "multidimensional" phaser combining an independent 4-stage phaser (classic phaser/vibrato territory) and 6-stage phaser (phaser through flanger through chorus territory) in one stereo pedal, each with its own footswitch so both can run simultaneously or independently.

## Controls
- **4-Stage Speed**: LFO rate for the 4-stage phaser
- **4-Stage Depth**: LFO range/intensity for the 4-stage phaser
- **4-Stage Mix**: Balances dry and phase-shifted signal for the 4-stage side; strongest classic phasing sits around 12 o'clock, fully clockwise gives 100% wet (which turns the 4-stage into a vibrato)
- **6-Stage Speed**: LFO rate for the 6-stage phaser
- **6-Stage Depth**: LFO range/intensity for the 6-stage phaser
- **6-Stage Voice**: Sweeps the 6-stage circuit's character from phasing through flanging to chorus tones, adjusting roughly 30 interacting internal parameters at once
- **6-Stage Mix**: Balances dry and processed signal for the 6-stage side

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Sweep** (4-stage mini-toggle, 3-position): Modulation shape for the 4-stage phaser — Classic (rising/falling LFO), Barber (continuously rising or falling "barber pole" sweep), or Envelope (touch/dynamics-controlled sweep)
- **Resonance** (6-stage mini-toggle, 3-position): Off, Mild, or Strong — feeds signal back into the 6-stage phaser's input for a more pronounced, vowel-like intensity
- **4-Stage footswitch**: Independently engages/bypasses the 4-stage phaser
- **6-Stage footswitch**: Independently engages/bypasses the 6-stage phaser

## Notes for patch-building
- Because the two phaser engines are fully independent (own knobs, own footswitch), Zelzah effectively covers two distinct GP-5 MOD-slot voicings in one pedal — e.g. a subtle always-on 4-stage vibrato/phase plus a switchable 6-stage flange or chorus for choruses/solos.
- 4-stage Mix fully clockwise (100% wet) is the vibrato setting — useful as a distinct third voicing beyond phasing/flanging/chorus.
- Stereo in/out via TRS input (Class A JFET preamp) and L/R TS outputs; has an EXP/MIDI jack and USB-C for expression/MIDI control.
- Power: 9VDC center-negative, 300mA minimum (supply not included).
