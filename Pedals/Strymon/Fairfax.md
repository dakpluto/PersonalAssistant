# Strymon Fairfax

Class A output-stage drive pedal, voiced to emulate the power-amp sag and saturation character of a Class A tube amp being pushed hard, rather than a preamp-gain overdrive circuit.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Amount of drive and saturation.
- **Level**: Output level when engaged.
- **Sag**: Variable power-rail sag control; at maximum settings introduces gating and sputtering artifacts characteristic of a starved Class A power section under heavy load.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Bright switch** (2-position): Off cuts high frequencies for a darker tone; On gives a flat, full-frequency response.
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the pedal (true bypass); lit amber LED indicates engagement. No mode switches beyond Bright and bypass.

## Notes for patch-building
- Voiced as a power-amp-style drive rather than a preamp overdrive — the Sag control is the distinctive feature, letting the drive character range from tight and controlled to spongy, gated, and sputtering as if a small Class A power amp is being starved.
- High-impedance JFET-buffered input, low-impedance mono output; positions well either as the DST-stage analog before a GP-5/amp-sim signal path, or after a clean amp model to add power-amp-style push.
- Mono in/out only, no stereo, no MIDI, no presets — a straightforward analog drive stomp. 9VDC center-negative, 500mA minimum.
