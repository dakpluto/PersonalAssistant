# Strymon DIG V2

Compact dual digital delay offering three classic "digital delay era" voicings (an early rack-style ADM delay, a lo-fi 12-bit delay, and a pristine modern 24-bit/96kHz delay) with two independently mixed delay lines running off one master time control.

## Controls
- **Time 1**: Master delay time for Delay 1 (also settable via the Tap Tempo footswitch); Delay 2's time is derived from it via Subdivision
- **Subdivision**: Rhythmic relationship between Delay 2's time and Delay 1's master time
- **Repeats**: Feedback amount, applied to both delay lines
- **Tone**: Filters the feedback path — counterclockwise for high cut, clockwise for low cut, flat at 12 o'clock
- **Mix 1**: Wet/dry balance for Delay 1; 3 o'clock = 50/50
- **Mix 2**: Wet/dry balance for Delay 2; 3 o'clock = 50/50

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Voice** (mini-toggle, 3-position): ADM (early rack-delay character), 12 Bit (lo-fi/gritty), or 24/96 (clean, modern digital)
- **Modulation** (mini-toggle, 3-position): Off, Light, or Deep modulation depth on the repeats
- **Input Select** (rear-panel toggle, 2-position): Mono or Stereo
- **Tap Tempo / Circular Repeats footswitch**: Tap to set Delay 1's master time; hold to engage circular repeats (routes repeats between the two delay lines)
- **On/Off footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the effect (red LED)

## Notes for patch-building
- Effectively two synced/related delay voices from one control set — useful where a GP-5 DLY slot alone can't cover both a short slapback-style repeat and a longer subdivided echo without a CTL-conditional setting change (which the GP-5 doesn't allow within one module).
- Stereo in/out via TRS input (Class A JFET preamp) and two independent TS outputs; has an EXP/MIDI jack and USB-C.
- Power: 9VDC center-negative, 300mA minimum (supply not included).
