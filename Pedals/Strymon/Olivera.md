# Strymon Olivera

Vintage oil can echo emulation — models the multi-head rotating-disc "oil can" delay units (e.g. the Tel-Ray/Morley Echo Chamber design) prized for their dark, warbly, modulated repeats, with selectable virtual playback-head configurations.

## Controls
- **Time**: Adjusts the virtual disc rotation speed / delay time, roughly 72ms–800ms depending on the selected head configuration.
- **Mix**: Balances dry and wet signal; 50/50 at the 3 o'clock position.
- **Rate**: Speed of the modulation applied to the echo repeats.
- **Regen**: Amount of delay signal fed back from the selected head(s) into the input (feedback/repeats amount).
- **Intensity**: Depth of the modulation; off at minimum.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Heads toggle** (3-way): Selects the virtual playback-head configuration — Long, Short, or Both.
- **I/O Mode selector** (rear panel, 3-position): Mono in/Mono out, Mono in/Stereo out, or Stereo in/Stereo out.
- **Footswitch**: Engages/disengages the effect; red LED indicates active status.

## Notes for patch-building
- Fills the GP-5's DLY role with a specifically dark, wobbly, lo-fi character distinct from the GP-5's tape/analog/digital delay models — good for surf, dub, and vintage-ambient textures where the modulation is baked into the repeats rather than a separate MOD effect.
- Class A JFET stereo preamp on the input; EXP/MIDI multifunction jack for expression pedal or MIDI control, plus USB for MIDI I/O and firmware updates.
- No onboard preset system (knobs-and-switches pedal, not a menu-diving workstation like TimeLine); the Heads switch is the primary voicing choice and isn't footswitch-toggleable mid-song.
