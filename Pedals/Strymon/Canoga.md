# Strymon Canoga

Vintage silicon fuzz — a minimalist, purely analog two-knob fuzz built around unbuffered, low-impedance input and high-impedance output to preserve the pickup-loading interaction and output-impedance character of vintage 1960s silicon fuzz circuits.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Amount of distortion, from a light edge-of-breakup grind through moderate crunch to massively saturated fuzz.
- **Level**: Output level when engaged; a substantial gain increase is available for use as a lead boost or to drive other pedals/the amp harder.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the pedal (true bypass); lit amber LED indicates engagement. No mode switches — Canoga is deliberately just Drive, Level, and bypass.

## Notes for patch-building
- Unbuffered, low-impedance input is a deliberate design choice — Strymon recommends placing Canoga first in the signal chain (directly after the guitar) for the most authentic vintage-fuzz interaction with pickups; a buffer or other pedal ahead of it will change/tame the fuzz character.
- High-impedance output likewise preserves classic fuzz behavior when feeding into the next stage — it's voiced to sound and react like a vintage fuzz box, not a modern low-noise unity-impedance pedal.
- Mono in/out only, no stereo, no MIDI, no presets — this is a simple analog stomp despite sharing shelf space with Strymon's workstation pedals. 9VDC center-negative, 50mA minimum.
