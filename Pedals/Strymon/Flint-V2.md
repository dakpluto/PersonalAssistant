# Strymon Flint V2

Combination tremolo and spring reverb pedal modeling three distinct vintage tremolo circuits and three distinct reverb eras, run as two independently footswitchable effects (tremolo and reverb) in series.

## Controls
- **Speed**: Tremolo LFO rate, extending down to nearly 1Hz at the slowest setting
- **Depth**: Tremolo effect intensity
- **Reverb Time (Decay)**: Reverb tail length; the '70s and '80s reverb types approach near-infinite decay at maximum
- **Reverb Tone (Color)**: Reverb tone, darker at low settings and brighter at higher settings
- **Mix**: Reverb dry/wet balance; 3 o'clock = 50/50

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Tremolo Era** (mini-toggle, 3-position): '61 Harmonic, '63 Tube, or '65 Photocell tremolo circuit
- **Reverb Era** (mini-toggle, 3-position): '60s Spring Tank, '70s Electronic Plate, or '80s Hall Rack
- **Tremolo footswitch**: Engages/bypasses tremolo (LED pulses with Speed)
- **Reverb footswitch**: Engages/bypasses reverb
- Secondary Live Edit functions (via footswitch-hold): Effect Order (tremolo-then-reverb or reverb-then-tremolo), Tap Division, Pre-Delay, and independent ±3dB Boost/Cut for the tremolo and reverb sections

## Notes for patch-building
- Covers two GP-5 module roles (MOD-slot tremolo and RVB-slot reverb) in a single pedal with independent footswitches — useful as a Full Board addition when a patch wants both an always-on vintage-voiced tremolo and a separate switchable spring/plate/hall reverb without spending two GP-5 module slots on them.
- Tremolo Era and Reverb Era are fixed toggles, not footswitch-conditional — settle on one circuit per era per static patch (Effect Order is similarly a fixed Live Edit setting, not something CTL can flip live).
- Stereo-capable I/O: TRS input (mono/stereo selectable via a rear switch) and two independent TS outputs; has an EXP/MIDI jack and USB-C.
- Power: 9VDC center-negative, 300mA minimum (supply not included).
