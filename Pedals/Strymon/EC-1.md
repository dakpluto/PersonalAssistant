# Strymon EC-1

Single-head dTape echo — a compact, single-algorithm distillation of Strymon's digital tape-echo modeling (the same dTape technology used as one machine within TimeLine), voicing one classic single-head tape delay rather than offering multiple delay types.

## Controls
- **Time**: Delay time, roughly 50ms to 1 second, controlling the virtual record-head position.
- **Mix**: Balances dry and wet signal, 100% dry to 100% wet.
- **Tape Age**: Controls simulated tape bandwidth/wear characteristics.
- **Mechanics**: Adjusts simulated mechanical speed fluctuations and tape-transport irregularities (wow/flutter-style artifacts).
- **Repeats**: Varies delay repeats from a single echo to continuous oscillation.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Rec Level switch** (3-way toggle): Low (unity gain, cleanest repeats) / Medium (6dB boost, slight saturation) / High (12dB boost, heavy saturation) — models the input-gain-driven saturation of overdriving a real tape echo's record head.
- **I/O Mode selector** (rear panel, 3-position): Mono (mono in/out, true bypass), Mono→Stereo (mono in, stereo out, buffered bypass), or Stereo (stereo in/out, buffered bypass).
- **Footswitch**: Engages/disengages the effect; also used to enter/exit Tap Mode and Save Mode; red LED indicates engagement.

## Notes for patch-building
- A focused single-voice alternative to the GP-5's DLY module for a specifically warm, saturating tape-delay character — the Rec Level switch is the standout feature, letting the repeats themselves saturate and darken the way overdriving a real tape echo's input would.
- Class A JFET stereo preamp on the input; EXP/MIDI multifunction jack for expression pedal or MIDI control, plus USB for MIDI I/O and firmware updates.
- No onboard preset bank (unlike TimeLine) — it's a single always-loaded voice with a small number of saveable "tap"/settings states rather than a full preset system.
