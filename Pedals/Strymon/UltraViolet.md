# Strymon UltraViolet

Vintage vibe pedal — models classic pitch-vibrato/uni-vibe-style circuits, selectable between chorus, blended, and pure vibrato voicings with an adjustable LFO bias point.

## Controls
- **Speed**: Rate of the LFO sweep.
- **Volume**: Output level, up to a 4dB boost at maximum.
- **Intensity**: LFO sweep amplitude (modulation depth).

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Mode switch** (3-position toggle): Chorus (left, 50/50 dry/wet mix) / Blend (middle, 70/30 dry/wet mix) / Vibrato (right, 100% wet signal).
- **Bias switch** (3-position): Low / Mid / High — sets the LFO's center frequency point, shifting where in the sweep the vibe effect is centered.
- **TRS switch** (rear panel, 3-position): Mono in/Mono out, Mono in/Stereo out, or Stereo in/Stereo out.
- **Footswitch**: Engages/disengages the effect; red LED indicates engagement.

## Notes for patch-building
- Fills the GP-5's MOD role with a specifically vintage pitch-vibrato/uni-vibe character (throbbing, rotating-speaker-adjacent but not a true rotary sim) rather than chorus/flanger/phaser — the Mode switch is the key voicing choice, since it sets the dry/wet ratio structurally rather than via the Mix control most modulation pedals use.
- Class A-adjacent high-impedance TRS input, low-impedance stereo TRS output; EXP/MIDI multifunction jack usable for expression pedal, MIDI, favorites recall, or tap tempo control of Speed.
- No onboard preset bank — a knobs-and-switches pedal, its "favorites"/MIDI capability is for external recall of the front-panel state rather than a menu-based preset system like TimeLine/BigSky/Mobius.
