# Strymon Riverside

Single-channel drive-to-distortion pedal built around a continuously variable gain circuit — the Drive knob doesn't just add level, it retunes the interstage circuitry as it's turned, so a single knob sweeps from clean edge-of-breakup through saturated distortion while staying optimally tuned throughout its range.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Continuously variable gain; retunes the interstage circuit values as it turns, so different points on the sweep are each individually optimized rather than just "more gain"
- **Level**: Output volume when engaged
- **Bass**: Active shelving low-frequency control
- **Middle**: Parametric mid boost/cut centered at a frequency tuned for guitar
- **Treble**: Wide-range active shelving high-frequency control

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Gain** (mini-toggle, 2-position): Low (smoother overdrive voicing) or High (more saturated distortion voicing)
- **Push** (mini-toggle, 2-position): Norm (transparent) or Mid (adds a mid-range push/enhancement)
- **Presence** (rear-panel toggle, 3-position): Minus (–, controlled/clear top end, good with already-bright amps), Enhanced (center, extended top end/sizzle), Plus (+, extra high-end detail, good for dark amps)
- **On footswitch**: Engages/bypasses via an electromechanical relay (true bypass)
- **Favorite footswitch**: Recalls a saved preset; press and hold to save the current knob state as the new Favorite

## Notes for patch-building
- A single flexible drive stage rather than Sunset's two-circuit approach — Gain and Push toggles give four base voicings (Low/Norm, Low/Mid, High/Norm, High/Mid) before the Drive knob's own clean-to-saturated sweep, so it covers everything from a light boost to a fairly heavy distortion from one circuit.
- Can also be used as a preamp/DI-style tone-shaping stage ahead of a return/mixer, per Strymon's own "using Riverside as a preamp" guidance, thanks to the active 3-band EQ.
- Mono in/out; rear-panel jack for an external footswitch giving a ±6dB boost, plus an expression pedal input (continuous knob control or dedicated Volume mode).
- Power: 9V DC center-negative, 250mA minimum (supply not included).
