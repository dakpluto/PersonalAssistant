# Strymon Cloudburst

Ambient reverb plus ensemble (chorus/vibrato-style modulation) pedal in Strymon's compact enclosure format — a single reverb algorithm with a built-in orchestral ensemble effect layered on top, rather than the multi-algorithm depth of BigSky.

## Controls
- **Pre-Delay**: Time gap before the reverberated signal appears.
- **Mod**: Dual-function knob — first half of its range controls modulation depth, second half controls modulation speed.
- **Decay**: Duration of the reverb tail's decay.
- **Mix**: Balances dry and wet reverb signal.
- **Tone**: High-frequency content of the reverb.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Ensemble switch** (3-position): Off (no ensemble effect) / mp (subtle, medium enhancement) / Forte (maximum orchestral ensemble effect).
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the effect; red LED indicates engagement.
- **TRS switch** (rear panel, 3-position): Mono in/out, Mono in/Stereo out, or Stereo in/out.

## Notes for patch-building
- Fills the GP-5's RVB module role but adds a built-in ensemble/chorus layer to the reverb tail that no onboard GP-5 reverb algorithm offers — good for lush, swelling ambient pads or shoegaze-style textures without needing a separate MOD pedal after it.
- EXP/MIDI jack for expression pedal or MIDI control; USB (rear panel) for MIDI and firmware updates — lighter MIDI implementation than the BigSky/Mobius/TimeLine workstation line (no onboard preset system; it's a knobs-and-switches pedal, not a menu-diving one).
- Class A JFET stereo preamp on the input; stereo TRS I/O with a mono-compatible routing option via the rear TRS switch.
