# Strymon BigSky

Original flagship multi-algorithm reverb workstation offering 12 reverb "machines" (Room, Hall, Plate, Spring, Swell, Bloom, Cloud, Chorale, Shimmer, Magneto, Nonlinear, and Reflections/other variants), 300 nameable presets, and a three-footswitch layout with a dedicated Infinite/freeze switch — still current alongside the newer dual-engine BigSky MX.

## Controls
- **Type**: Rotary encoder that selects the reverb machine. Press toggles the display between Bank and Time readout; press and hold saves the current preset.
- **Value**: Rotary encoder for preset navigation and decay-time fine-tuning; also edits global settings.
- **Decay**: Reverb tail length (range depends on the selected machine).
- **Pre-Delay**: Gap between the dry signal and the onset of reverb (0–1.5s); on Nonlinear/Magneto machines, controls feedback instead.
- **Tone**: High-frequency content of the reverb — darker/warmer at lower settings, brighter/crisper at higher.
- **Mod**: Adds modulation/movement to the reverb tail, subtle at low settings and stronger at high.
- **Param 1 / Param 2**: Algorithm-specific menu parameters, assignable per preset (meaning changes with the selected Type).
- **Mix**: Balances dry and wet signal, 100% dry to 100% wet; 50/50 at the 3 o'clock position.
- **Display**: Shows decay time, preset name, bank number, or parameter values during editing.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Footswitch A**: Engages/bypasses the preset; press and hold engages Infinite Sustain/freeze; A+B together banks down through presets.
- **Footswitch B**: Engages/bypasses the preset; press and hold engages Infinite Sustain/freeze; B+C together banks up through presets.
- **Footswitch C**: Engages/bypasses the preset; press and hold engages Infinite Sustain/freeze.
- **Cab Filter switch** (rear panel): Applies a speaker-response curve for direct/PA use.
- **I/O**: Left/Right In and Out — mono when only the left jacks are used, stereo when both are used.

## Notes for patch-building
- Same structural role as the GP-5's RVB module but a dramatically deeper substitute: 12 reverb algorithms with per-algorithm parameter depth (shimmer, chorale, magneto tape-style, etc.) and an Infinite footswitch that freezes the current tail regardless of what preset is loaded — useful for swells and drones a single onboard reverb block can't produce.
- 300 user-saveable presets, full MIDI I/O, expression pedal jack (controls any knob or combination, saveable per preset), and a Kill Dry mode for use in a parallel effects loop.
- Selectable true bypass or analog buffered bypass, with Spillover and Reverb Persist modes so the tail carries through a preset change; ±3dB global boost/cut.
- 24-bit/96kHz conversion with an analog dry path (zero added latency) — a strong choice as the dedicated ambient/end-of-chain unit on a board with room for a full workstation pedal.
