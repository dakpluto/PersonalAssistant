# Strymon Iridium

Amp modeler and cabinet/IR pedal — three amp models paired with a bank of nine stereo speaker-cabinet impulse responses (user-replaceable), plus adjustable room ambience, designed as a full amp-in-a-box front end for DI/recording/silent-stage use or as a pedalboard amp stage.

## Amp Models (3)
1. **Round Amp** — based on a Fender Deluxe Reverb: clean, bright, mid-scooped.
2. **Chime Amp** — based on the Brilliant channel of a Vox AC30TB: jangly, chimey.
3. **Punch Amp** — based on a Marshall Plexi (Super Lead, model 1959): high-gain, midrange-forward.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Amount of gain applied to the incoming signal.
- **Bass**: Low-frequency content of the tone stack.
- **Middle**: Midrange content; functions as a tone-cut control specifically on the Chime amp model.
- **Treble**: High-frequency content.
- **Level**: Output volume for all outputs; secondary (push/hold) function sets headphone output trim.
- **Room**: Level of natural room ambience added; secondary function toggles among small/medium/large room sizes.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Amp switch**: Selects one of the three amp types.
- **Cab switch**: Selects one of three complementary cabinet IRs paired to the selected amp type.
- **Audio Input Configuration selector** (rear panel, 3-position): MONO (guitar input, stereo output), STEREO (stereo input, stereo output), or SUM (stereo input summed to mono via the OUT L jack).
- **ON footswitch**: Engages amp/cab/room processing; red LED indicator.
- **FAV (Favorite) footswitch**: Recalls a stored favorite setting; press and hold saves the current settings as the favorite.

## Cabinet Impulse Responses (9, factory-loaded, user-replaceable)
1. Deluxe Reverb 1x12" (OwnHammer)
2. Blues Junior 1x12" (CabIR)
3. Vibrolux 2x10" (CabIR)
4. AC30 2x12" AlNiCo (OwnHammer)
5. 1x12" AlNiCo (Celestion)
6. Mesa 4x12" (Valhallir)
7. GNR 4x12" (OwnHammer)
8. 2x12" Vintage 30 (Celestion)
9. Marshall 8x12" AlNiCo (CabIR)

All delivered at 24-bit/96kHz, 500ms stereo resolution; any of the nine slots can be replaced with a custom IR via the free Strymon Impulse Manager software over USB.

## Notes for patch-building
- Functionally overlaps the GP-5's AMP+CAB pair, similar to how a NAM capture is used in this workflow — but Iridium is a physical always-on pedal rather than a `NAMs/` reference file, so if it's ever added to a board it would occupy the AMP/CAB position in the chain directly rather than being documented as a NAM substitution.
- EXP/MIDI jack supports expression-pedal volume control (pre- or post-amp/cab/room, logarithmic taper) or "morph between knob positions" mode; full MIDI with up to 300 presets (requires the MIDI EXP cable accessory) and MultiSwitch Plus compatibility for up to 4 presets at the feet.
- Stereo 1/8" headphone output for silent practice/recording; line-level or instrument-level input selectable.
- 9VDC center-negative, 500mA minimum.
