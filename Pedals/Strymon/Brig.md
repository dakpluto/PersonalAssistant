# Strymon Brig

dBucket delay — a bucket-brigade-style analog-voiced delay (digitally modeled "dBucket" circuit, the same delay family used as one machine within TimeLine/TimeLine MX) offered as a focused standalone pedal with three selectable voicings.

## Controls
- **Filter**: Adjusts EQ filtering, noise character, and bucket-loss coloration of the repeats; behavior depends on the selected Voice.
- **Mod**: Adds LFO modulation to the delay time; off at minimum, modulation speed increases clockwise.
- **Time**: Delay time; continuously variable, usable for pitch-bend-style effects when swept while repeating.
- **Mix**: Balances dry and wet signal; 50/50 at roughly the 3 o'clock position.
- **Repeats**: Varies repeats from a single echo to runaway self-oscillation; approximately 3 o'clock gives infinite sustain.
- **Voice**: Selector for one of three delay voicings (see below).

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Voice switch** (3-position): **3205** (30–300ms range, gritty bucket-brigade repeats), **3005** (100ms–1sec range, dreamier/smoother quality), **Multi** (cross-coupled dual delay lines for more complex rhythmic patterns).
- **TRS switch** (rear panel, 3-position): Mono in/Mono out, Mono in/Stereo out, or Stereo in/Stereo out.
- **Footswitch**: Engages/disengages the effect; press and hold for 1 second enters tap-tempo mode. LED is red when engaged, flashes red/blue during tap tempo entry.

## Notes for patch-building
- Fills the GP-5's DLY role with a specifically gritty, lo-fi analog bucket-brigade character (distinct from Olivera's oil-can warble and EC-1's tape saturation) — the Voice switch is the primary voicing choice between a short/gritty range, a longer/dreamier range, and a dual cross-coupled delay-line mode.
- Class A JFET stereo preamp on the input; EXP/MIDI multifunction jack with 5 configurable modes (expression pedal or MIDI control); USB-C for MIDI I/O and firmware updates.
- No onboard preset bank — a knobs-and-switches pedal rather than a menu-diving workstation.
