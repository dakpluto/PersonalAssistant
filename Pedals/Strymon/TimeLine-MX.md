# Strymon TimeLine MX

Second-generation flagship delay workstation built on the same 12-delay-machine foundation as the original TimeLine, adding a true dual-engine architecture that can run two delays simultaneously in Series, Parallel, or Split routing, 300 presets, a 5-minute stereo looper, USB-C, and an OLED display.

## Controls
- **Type**: Rotary encoder that selects the delay machine for the active engine. Press toggles between editing Delay Engine 1, Engine 2, Tap Division 1/2, or Dual Mode routing; press and hold opens the SAVE screen to name and store a preset.
- **Value**: Rotary encoder for selecting among 300 preset locations. Press enters the PARAM menu; hold 1 second returns to the Home screen; hold 3 seconds opens the GLOBAL menu.
- **Time**: Sets delay time across the selected machine's full range; in a Reverb-type machine, controls Predelay instead.
- **Repeats**: Sets echo count from a single repeat to sustained regenerating oscillation; on Reverb-type machines, controls Decay time instead.
- **Filter**: Shapes the repeats' filtering — tape age on dTape, low-pass cutoff on dBucket, LFO center frequency on the Filter engine (algorithm-dependent).
- **Grit**: Progressively adds distortion/lo-fi artifacts; controls tape bias on dTape.
- **Speed**: Delay-modulation LFO speed; controls tape crinkle rate on dTape.
- **Depth**: Modulation intensity; controls wow & flutter depth on dTape.
- **Mix**: Balances dry and wet signal, 100% dry to 100% wet; 50/50 at the 3 o'clock position.
- **Display**: OLED screen for type, parameter, and preset navigation, readable in bright light.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Footswitch A**: Engages/bypasses the current bank preset (LED green when active, amber if edited, off when bypassed); A+B together selects the bank below.
- **Footswitch B**: Engages/bypasses the current bank preset; A+B together selects the bank below; B+TAP together selects the bank above.
- **TAP footswitch**: Taps in tempo (blinks at the current delay-type tempo); press and hold enters Looper mode; supports "1 Button Looper" control without formally entering Looper mode. B+TAP together selects the bank above.
- **Optional FS MODE (global setting)**: Can be set to DUAL so the two footswitches independently engage/bypass the two delay engines rather than switching presets together.
- **I/O**: Left/Right In and Out, all TRS stereo, with switchable I/O configurations — normal, mono/stereo effects loop, wet/dry, or wet/dry/wet.

## Notes for patch-building
- Same structural role as the GP-5's DLY module, but the dual-engine routing (Series/Parallel/Split, independent per-engine pan) lets a single preset cover, e.g., a subtle always-on slapback plus a swell-able ambient repeat — something a single GP-5 DLY slot can't do.
- 5-minute stereo looper (up from the original TimeLine's 30 seconds), placeable pre- or post-delay; USB-C for MIDI I/O, firmware updates, and (per Strymon) an upcoming Nixie 2 editor.
- Full MIDI (DIN and USB-C) with Program Change preset recall and CC control of any parameter; expression/MIDI jack compatible with Strymon's MultiSwitch and MultiSwitch Plus accessories.
- Selectable true bypass or buffered bypass; analog dry path stays undigitized for zero added latency.
