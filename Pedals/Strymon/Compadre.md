# Strymon Compadre

Dual-voice compressor and boost in one enclosure — independently footswitchable compressor and boost sections that can be used separately or stacked, with fixed attack/decay/threshold/makeup-gain compression (only Compression amount and dry blend are user-adjustable) for a simple, musical compressor rather than a fully parametric one.

## Controls
- **Compression**: Amount of compression applied to the signal (attack, decay, threshold, and makeup gain are internally fixed per Comp Type).
- **Dry**: Blends uncompressed dry signal back in with the compressed signal, parallel-compression style.
- **Level**: Compressor-section output level, -6dB to +6dB, unity at noon.
- **Boost**: Amount of boost, up to roughly +14dB of clean gain at a flat EQ setting.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Comp Type switch** (2-position): Studio / Squeeze — two distinct compression voicings/curves.
- **Boost Type switch** (2-position, rear panel): Clean / Dirty — selects between a clean boost circuit and a dirty (mildly overdriving) boost circuit.
- **Boost EQ switch** (3-position): Treble / Mid / Flat — shapes the frequency content of the boost.
- **Comp footswitch**: Engages/disengages the compressor section independently.
- **Boost footswitch**: Engages/disengages the boost section (applied post-compression) independently.

## Notes for patch-building
- Two effects in one box that map to two different roles in a GP-5-based chain: the Comp section substitutes for a dedicated compressor pedal ahead of DST/AMP, while the Boost section can function as a clean/dirty volume kick for solos, placed after compression.
- Rear-panel Volume jack accepts a TRS expression pedal for real-time volume-pedal-style control; Fav/MIDI jack accepts a Strymon MiniSwitch, MultiSwitch Plus, or MIDI EXP Cable — full MIDI implementation supports recalling up to 300 presets, with each section's bypass state saved independently per preset.
- Mono in/out only (no stereo I/O); high-impedance Class A JFET input, low-impedance mono output. 9VDC center-negative.
