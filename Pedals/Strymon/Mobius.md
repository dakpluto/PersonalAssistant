# Strymon Mobius

Multi-modulation workstation offering 12 modulation "machines" — Chorus, Flanger, Rotary, Vibe, Phaser, Filter, Formant, Vintage Trem, Pattern Trem, Autoswell, Destroyer, and Quadrature — several of which have their own internal sub-mode selections, with 200 nameable presets and full MIDI.

## Controls
- **Type**: Rotary encoder that selects the modulation machine. Press toggles the display between Bank and BPM; press and hold saves the current preset.
- **Value**: Rotary encoder for preset navigation and fine-tuning LFO speed; also edits global settings.
- **Speed**: Coarse LFO speed.
- **Depth**: Modulation intensity/depth.
- **Level**: Output level trim, -3dB to +3dB, unity at 12 o'clock.
- **Param 1 / Param 2**: User-assignable menu parameters, saveable per preset (function depends on the selected machine — e.g. Chorus/Flanger sub-mode, Phaser stage count, Filter type).
- **Display**: Shows LFO speed (BPM), preset name, bank number, or extended parameters.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Footswitch A**: Engages/bypasses the preset; A+B together banks down through presets.
- **Footswitch B**: Engages/bypasses the preset; B+TAP together banks up through presets.
- **TAP footswitch**: Taps in LFO speed; retriggers the LFO phase on Phaser, Filter, Formant, Pattern Trem, and Quadrature machines; assignable to speed selection on Rotary.
- **Signal Routing switch** (rear panel): Selects stereo operation or Pre/Post Mode, which lets Mobius sit in two different points of a mono signal chain via its second input/output pair.
- **I/O**: Left/Right In and Out — mono (or Input 2/Output 2 in Pre/Post Mode) when only the left jacks are used.

## 12 Modulation Machines
1. **Chorus** — 5 internal modes: dBucket, Multi, Vibrato, Digital, Detune
2. **Flanger** — 6 internal modes: Silver, Grey, Black+, Black-, Zero+, Zero-
3. **Rotary** — rotating speaker simulation
4. **Vibe** — vintage pitch-vibrato/uni-vibe style
5. **Phaser** — selectable 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, or 16-stage
6. **Filter** — Low Pass, Wah, and High Pass modes with resonance
7. **Formant** — vowel-controlled modulation
8. **Vintage Trem** — Harmonic, Tube, and Photo (opto) modes
9. **Pattern Trem** — 8-beat step-sequenced tremolo patterns
10. **Autoswell** — 4 swell curve types, blendable with chorus/vibrato
11. **Destroyer** — bit-crushing, sample-rate reduction, lo-fi filtering, vinyl-style artifacts
12. **Quadrature** — AM, FM, and frequency-shifting modes

## Notes for patch-building
- Plays the same structural role as the GP-5's MOD module, but with 12 distinct effect families (several with their own internal voicing sub-modes) versus a single MOD slot — a strong pick when a patch calls for a modulation type the GP-5's onboard MOD models don't cover well (e.g. Formant, Pattern Trem, Destroyer).
- 200 user-saveable presets, full MIDI implementation (clock sync, CC control of any parameter, preset recall), and an expression pedal jack that can control any knob or combination, saveable per preset.
- Pre/Post Mode is notable for board design: it lets a single Mobius sit in two chain positions at once (e.g. before drive and after delay) in a mono rig without a second unit.
- Selectable true bypass or analog buffered bypass; 24-bit/96kHz conversion, 32-bit floating-point processing.
