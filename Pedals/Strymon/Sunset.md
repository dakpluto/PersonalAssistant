# Strymon Sunset

Dual-channel overdrive pedal pairing two independently voiced drive circuits (Channel A and Channel B, each a 3-way selectable circuit) that can be run singly, stacked in series (either order), or blended in parallel, with custom-calibrated knob ranges per circuit for maximal usable range on each.

## Controls
- **Channel A Drive**: Gain amount for whichever Channel A circuit is selected
- **Channel A Tone**: Treble/brightness for the selected Channel A circuit
- **Channel A Level**: Output volume for Channel A when engaged
- **Channel B Drive**: Gain amount for whichever Channel B circuit is selected
- **Channel B Tone**: Treble/brightness for the selected Channel B circuit
- **Channel B Level**: Output volume for Channel B when engaged

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Channel A circuit selector** (3-position): Ge (germanium-diode clipping with a dry parallel path), Texas (single-stage soft-clipper with a mid bump), or Treble (clean boost that strips low end)
- **Channel B circuit selector** (3-position): 2 Stage (soft-clipping into hard-clipping), Hard (high-gain single-stage hard-clipper), or JFET (clean boost)
- **Config switch** (rear panel, 3-position): How the two channels combine when both are on — series (A into B), reverse series (B into A), or parallel
- **Bright switch** (rear panel, 3-position): High-frequency trim, roughly –/neutral/+
- **Footswitch A**: Engages/bypasses Channel A (red LED)
- **Footswitch B**: Engages/bypasses Channel B (red LED)

## Notes for patch-building
- Effectively two independently-voiced overdrives in one box; a natural fit ahead of the GP-5's DST/AMP block as a rhythm/lead drive-stacking pair — e.g. Channel A (Ge or Texas) as an always-on low-gain push, Channel B (Hard or 2 Stage) footswitched in for leads, with the Config switch set to series.
- Because Config and circuit-selector positions are set by rear/front switches (not footswitch-toggleable), pick the combination that matches the patch's needs when building a static patch — a true two-voicing CTL toggle needs footswitch A/B to do the switching, not the Config switch.
- Mono in/out; has an expression pedal input (for morphing knob values) and a jack for an external footswitch for Favorite-preset recall.
- Power: 9V DC center-negative, 250mA minimum (supply not included).
