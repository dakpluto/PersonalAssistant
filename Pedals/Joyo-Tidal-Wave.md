# Joyo Tidal Wave (R-30)

Bass preamp/overdrive pedal with 3-band EQ, DI output, and noise reduction. Bass-only — this is the drive/preamp slot on the Bass Full Board (where guitar builds use the Donner Stylish Fuzz + King of Kings instead).

## Knobs
- **Drive**: Overdrive/preamp gain amount
- **Blend**: Wet/dry mix between the clean signal and the driven signal
- **Presence**: High-frequency gain/edge on the driven signal
- **Level**: Overall output volume
- **Treble**: 3-band EQ high control
- **Middle**: 3-band EQ mid control
- **Bass**: 3-band EQ low control

## Toggle switches
- **Mid-Frequency select**: 500Hz / 1000Hz center frequency for the Middle EQ band
- **Bass-Shift select**: 40Hz / 80Hz center frequency for the Bass EQ band
- **Cab-Sim (DI out)**: Engages cabinet simulation on the DI output only (main instrument output is unaffected)
- **Ground Lift**: Reduces hum/noise on the DI output when engaged

## I/O
- Standard 1/4" in/out plus a balanced DI output (XLR) with its own cab-sim and ground-lift switches — the DI path is independent of what's being sent to the amp/GP-5.

## Notes for patch-building
- Drive/Blend together control how much overdrive character stacks with the GP-5's own PRE/DST/AMP chain — for a full board build, keep Blend moderate unless the song specifically wants an obviously dirty bass tone, since bass tone usually needs the fundamental to stay intact.
- Bass-Shift 40Hz keeps more low end in the drive signal (fuller/muddier); 80Hz tightens it up (more articulate, better for high-gain or busy mixes).
- Mid-Frequency 500Hz vs 1000Hz picks where the EQ's mid push/cut lands — 500Hz is a classic "bass body" frequency, 1000Hz is more "pick/finger attack and cut-through."
