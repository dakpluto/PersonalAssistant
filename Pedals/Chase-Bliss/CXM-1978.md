# Chase Bliss CXM 1978

Studio reverb pedal built with Meris, modeling the classic Lexicon 224 studio reverb unit (a fixture in recording studios starting in 1978). Uses six motorized faders and arcade-style buttons instead of conventional knobs/switches, so recalled presets physically move the faders to the saved positions in real time.

## Controls
- **Bass** (fader): Decay behavior for frequencies below the Cross point
- **Mids** (fader): Decay behavior for frequencies above the Cross point
- **Cross** (fader): Sets the crossover frequency splitting Bass/Mids decay (0 Hz–1.7 kHz)
- **Treble** (fader): Brightness of the reverb tail; interacts with the Bass/Mids decay split
- **Mix** (fader): Wet/dry blend
- **Pre-Dly** (fader): Pre-delay time before the reverb tail begins (range depends on Clock setting)
- **Type** (arcade button): Selects reverb algorithm — Room, Plate, or Hall
- **Diffusion** (arcade button): Amount of smearing applied to the early reflections/attack (low/medium/high); at low settings with high Diffusion it interacts with Pre-Dly for flanging/dense textures, at higher settings for more distinct echoes
- **Tank Mod** (arcade button): Modulation depth/rate applied to the virtual reverb tank's internal feedback and delays — from subtle chorus/vibrato up to fast, rotary-speaker-like movement
- **Clock** (arcade button): Sets sample rate/bit depth/pre-delay ceiling — Standard (24kHz/16-bit, up to 168ms pre-delay), Hifi (48kHz/32-bit, up to 42ms pre-delay), Lofi (degraded, variable 2.4–48kHz, up to 1.7s pre-delay)

## Footswitches / Switches
- Bypass/Save footswitch: engages/bypasses the effect; holding it saves the current fader/arcade-button state (and expression assignments) to the active preset
- Preset/Bank footswitch: steps through 10 presets per bank (0–9); holding it switches between the pedal's 3 banks (30 total preset slots)
- Jump button: quick-recalls a favorite preset (0 or 5)
- Holding the Preset footswitch at power-up toggles "Dry Kill" (whether the dry signal is cut entirely, useful for wet-only effects-loop or parallel-mix use)

## Notes for patch-building
- A pure reverb block — sits in the RVB position of a signal chain (after drive/amp/cab and delay), or in an effects loop/parallel mix path if the amp or board supports one, especially useful with Dry Kill engaged.
- The motorized-fader preset recall makes it well suited to songs needing multiple distinct reverb settings switched instantly (e.g. tight room verb for verses, huge hall for a bridge) without CTL-style compromise, since each preset is a full fader snapshot rather than a single toggled parameter.
- Balanced stereo TRS I/O; bypass is via the dedicated footswitch (not stated as true bypass — treat as buffered/DSP unless confirmed otherwise). Supports MIDI In/Thru, CV, and expression control of every fader/parameter, with real-time motorized visual feedback when a preset or external controller changes settings — depth beyond just twisting the six faders by hand.
