# Electro-Harmonix Lizard King

Bass-focused octave fuzz pedal (the bass-optimized successor to EHX's guitar-centric Lizard Queen, which EHX discontinued in 2025). Combines a fixed-voicing fuzz with a blendable sub-octave and a two-flavor tone toggle, aimed at players who want fuzz without losing low-end punch and note definition.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Overall output level.
- **Blend**: Mixes clean and fuzz signal, from 100% clean up to 100% fuzz — lets low end and pick attack stay present even with fuzz dialed in, instead of an all-or-nothing fuzz blend.
- **Tone**: Lowpass filter that rolls off high end, smoothing out the fuzz's top end.
- **Octave**: Controls the blend level of a retro-voiced octave-down layered under the dry/fuzz signal.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch: true bypass on/off.
- **SUN/SHADOW toggle**: Switches the fuzz voicing character — SHADOW is a darker, smoother, more sustaining fuzz; SUN is brighter, raspier, and more biting.

## Notes for patch-building
- Front-end drive stage for bass patches — functions like a substitute for (or addition to) the GP-5's DST module specifically for bass, where the Blend and Octave controls let you keep low-end integrity that a straight fuzz would otherwise smear.
- The Octave control effectively gives this pedal double duty as an octave/synth-ish texture generator, not just a fuzz — useful in patches that want a sub-octave layer without a dedicated octave pedal.
- True bypass. Powered by 9V battery or a 9VDC center-negative adapter (8mA draw, do not exceed 12VDC).
- Don't confuse with the older EHX Lizard Queen (guitar-oriented, simpler Volume/Octave/Balance control set, discontinued 2025) — the Lizard King is the current bass-specific model with the added Blend and Tone knobs.
