# Donner Ultimate Comp

Optical compressor pedal. True bypass.

## Knobs
- **COMP**: Compression intensity — how hard the signal gets squashed/sustained
- **TONE**: Brightness of the compressed signal (dark to bright)
- **LEVEL**: Output volume

## Mode switch
- **NORMAL**: Neutral optical compressor character
- **TREBLE**: Brighter optical compressor character, more bite/attack up top

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits early in the chain (after any octave pedal, before fuzz/drive) — squashes dynamics before they hit the gain stages downstream.
- Light COMP settings add sustain and evens out pick attack without an audible "squeeze"; heavy COMP flattens dynamics for a glassy, always-on-level tone (good for chimey clean/arpeggiated parts).
- TREBLE mode is useful when the compressor is feeding a darker amp/cab pairing downstream and needs to keep pick attack audible.
