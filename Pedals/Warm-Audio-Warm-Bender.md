# Warm Audio Warm Bender

Tone Bender-style fuzz pedal offering three selectable vintage fuzz circuits in one box — two germanium (NOS transistor) voicings plus a modern silicon voicing — with a voltage-starving SAG control for extra touch-sensitivity and compression.

## Controls
- **Attack**: Gain/fuzz amount. Clockwise increases the amount of fuzz applied to the signal.
- **Level**: Output volume.
- **Version (3-way rotary/chicken-head switch)**: Selects the fuzz circuit:
  - **NOS 76**: Based on the original 2-transistor "MkI" Tone Bender circuit.
  - **NOS 75**: Based on the 3-transistor "Mark II Professional" Tone Bender circuit.
  - **Silicon**: Modern 3-transistor silicon topology — smoother, more distortion-like character than the germanium modes.
- **SAG switch**: Starves the circuit's voltage from 9V down to 6V, producing a compressed, touch-sensitive "dying battery" fuzz tone.

## Notes for patch-building
- Front-end drive/fuzz stage — sits early in the chain (before other gain stages), the same role as a dedicated fuzz pedal ahead of the GP-5's own DST/AMP.
- The three-circuit selector makes this a good single-pedal stand-in for "which vintage fuzz does this song want" — NOS 76 for the earliest, gated/spitty Tone Bender character, NOS 75 for smoother/more sustained fuzz, Silicon for a tighter modern-distortion feel.
- True bypass. 9V center-negative, 6mA draw; runs on a 9V battery or external 9V regulated DC adapter.
- Single footswitch, no CTL/mode-switching mid-song — the Version and SAG settings are hands-on-pedal only, not footswitchable.
