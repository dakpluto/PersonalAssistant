# JHS Colour Box 10

Studio-grade preamp, 3-band shifting EQ, and distortion generator in a pedal enclosure, built as a multi-instrument utility (guitar, bass, vocals, drums, keys) as much as a guitar-pedalboard tone shaper — the 10th-anniversary edition of the original Colour Box, effectively a Colour Box V2 with combo XLR/1/4" input and 48V phantom power for mic use.

## Controls
**Gain section (red):**
- **Master**: Overall unit output volume
- **Pre-Vol**: Gain between the pedal's two internal preamp stages — acts as a drive/gain control, adding grit, distortion, or fuzz as it's increased
- **Step**: 5-position gain-stage selector, roughly +18dB / +23dB / +28dB / +33dB / +39dB per step

**EQ section (blue), each band paired with a shift control:**
- **Bass**: ±15dB cut/boost, flat at noon
- **Bass Shift**: Sets the Bass band's center frequency, 20Hz–440Hz
- **Middle**: ±10dB cut/boost, flat at noon
- **Middle Shift**: Sets the Middle band's center frequency, 150Hz–2.4kHz
- **Treble**: ±15dB cut/boost, flat at noon
- **Treble Shift**: Sets the Treble band's center frequency, 2kHz–30kHz

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Hi/Lo headroom switch**: Lo gives cleaner headroom; Hi makes distortion easier to reach
- **Input selector (INST/XLR)**: Chooses between 1/4" instrument input and XLR mic/line input
- **-20dB Pad switch**: XLR-input-only, pads hot microphone signals
- **Hi-Pass Filter toggle**: Engages an adjustable 12dB/octave high-pass filter, sweepable roughly 160Hz–650Hz
- Silent footswitch (no switch-pop)

## Notes for patch-building
- Functions as a preamp/EQ/drive utility rather than a single-voice overdrive — the Step + Pre-Vol combination alone can go from clean preamp boost to full distortion, so treat it as a flexible AMP/PRE substitute or an always-on tone-shaping utility ahead of the rest of the chain.
- The combo XLR input, phantom power, and -20dB pad are studio/DI features (mic preamp use) not relevant to a guitar-only GP-5 chain, but worth noting if Michael also uses it for vocal or DI recording off the board.
- Typical placement: front of the chain as a preamp/gain-staging tool, or in an effects loop as a studio-style EQ/drive utility.
- 9V DC center negative only (no higher-voltage option).
