# JHS The Violet

Lari Basilio signature distortion/overdrive built around a three-band EQ with an unconventional layout: Middle sits *before* the distortion/clipping stage (shaping what gets driven), while Bass and Treble sit in the traditional post-distortion position — giving pre- and post-gain tone shaping in one pedal rather than a single flat EQ block.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Drive amount. Counterclockwise reduces drive, clockwise maximizes it.
- **Mid Freq**: Selects which midrange frequency band the Middle knob affects (pre-distortion).
- **Middle**: Midrange level, applied before the distortion/clipping stage. Noon is flat, left scoops, right boosts.
- **Bass**: Low-frequency level, applied after distortion. Noon is neutral, left cuts, right boosts.
- **Treble**: Treble level, applied after distortion. Noon is neutral, left cuts, right boosts.
- **Volume**: Overall output level. Left is less, right is more.

## Notes for patch-building
- The pre-distortion Middle/Mid Freq pair is the key character control here — scooping or boosting mids *before* the clipping stage changes the flavor of the distortion itself (tighter/scooped vs. thicker/pushed), not just its tonal color after the fact, which is a different tool than a standard post-gain 3-band EQ.
- Sits in the distortion/overdrive family (not a pure fuzz), voiced for lead and rhythm tones with real EQ shaping range.
- Typical placement: main drive stage of the chain, ahead of the amp.
- Silent soft-touch switching, buffered bypass. Mono in/out (1/4" jacks top-mounted), 9V DC center-negative only, 55mA draw.
