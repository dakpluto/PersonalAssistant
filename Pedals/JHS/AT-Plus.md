# JHS AT+

Andy Timmons signature overdrive, the "+" revision of JHS's original AT (2016) that adds an independent footswitchable boost stage. The base drive channel is a FET-based overdrive voiced for Andy Timmons's touch-sensitive, amp-like feel; the boost is a slightly dirty pre-boost that can push the drive channel harder or run on its own.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level of the main drive channel
- **Drive**: Gain range from high-gain blues breakup to heavier distortion
- **EQ**: Standard bright/dark tone control
- **Air**: Presence-style control, adds top-end brightness/articulation
- **Boost**: Level of the independent boost stage

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Wattage toggle (3-position)**: Simulates the compression/breakup character of a 25W, 50W, or 100W tube amp (up = 25W, most compression/saturation; middle = 100W, max volume and open crunch; down = 50W, balanced compression and openness)
- **Dual footswitches**: Independent bypass (drive channel) and boost engage, so the boost can stack onto the drive channel or run standalone

## Notes for patch-building
- Two usable gain stages in one pedal: dial the drive channel as the main overdrive voice, and use the boost footswitch as a lead-boost or to push the drive channel into heavier saturation without a second pedal.
- The wattage toggle is a static, non-footswitchable voicing choice — pick the position that matches the song before building a static patch, same as any other internal-style toggle.
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (safe at 9V or 18V), 15mA current draw.
