# JHS 3 Series Glitch Delay

Digital delay with randomized time-based glitching in JHS's $99 3 Series line, inspired by the Line 6 DL4 and the ambient, smearing delay work of Bill Frisell — warps and stutters repeats within the feedback loop rather than repeating cleanly.

## Controls
- **Glitch**: Probability of a glitch event occurring, 0–50%
- **Time**: Delay time, 20ms to 980ms
- **Repeats**: Number of repeats; also adds modulation to the repeats as it's increased
- **Mix toggle**: Selects delay level — down is roughly 35% wet, up is roughly 80% wet

An LED flashes in sync with glitch events.

## Notes for patch-building
- Glitch knob is the defining control — low settings give occasional stutter/pitch artifacts on top of a normal delay, higher settings make the glitching the dominant character
- Good pick for an ambient/experimental DLY slot distinct from a clean utility delay elsewhere on the board
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (65mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
