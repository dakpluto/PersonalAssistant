# JHS 3 Series Phaser

Six-stage phaser in JHS's $99 3 Series line, inspired by classic 1970s phaser circuits, with a Blend control and Feedback toggle for a wider range than a typical fixed-mix vintage phaser.

## Controls
- **Speed**: Rate of the phaser's LFO sweep (left slower, right faster)
- **Blend**: Wet/dry mix (left fully dry, right fully wet)
- **Width**: Depth/width of the LFO sweep (left mild, right extreme)
- **Feedback toggle**: Up feeds the output back into the input, adding resonance and a more intense, emphasized phase sweep

## Notes for patch-building
- Blend control lets this sit as a subtle vintage-style phase (low blend) or a full-on swirl (high blend) from the same pedal
- Feedback toggle up pushes toward more resonant, almost vocal/wah-adjacent phasing — good for a more aggressive MOD voicing
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (70mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
