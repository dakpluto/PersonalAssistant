# JHS Haunting Mids

Sweepable-mids EQ preamp — a dedicated parametric-style midrange tool rather than a drive or boost pedal in its own right, meant to scoop or push midrange frequencies to reshape an amp's or another pedal's voicing.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Standard output level — set at unity or boost the signal
- **Mids**: Boosts or cuts the selected midrange frequency; noon (12 o'clock) is neutral/flat, counterclockwise scoops, clockwise boosts
- **Sweep**: Selects which midrange frequency the Mids knob affects — left targets low-mids, right targets high-mids (range spans roughly 400Hz–7.5kHz)

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Lo/Hi toggle**: Acts as a "Q" control for the mid peak — Lo gives a softer, wider peak for subtler shaping, Hi gives a sharper, steeper peak for a more pointed, defined effect
- **Footswitch**: On/off

## Notes for patch-building
- Boost/cut range is ±15dB, so it can go from a gentle mid nudge to a fairly aggressive scoop or honk depending on Mids/Sweep/Lo-Hi settings.
- Typical placement: after drive/distortion, before or in the amp's effects loop — works well as a static "voicing" tool (e.g., permanent mid scoop for a metal rhythm tone, or a mid push for a lead) rather than something toggled mid-song, though it can also function as a solo-boost EQ.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V DC, 13mA current draw.
