# JHS Morning Glory (V4)

Transparent overdrive pedal, JHS's flagship drive designed to preserve the amp's own character rather than impose its own tonal stamp, now in its fourth revision with roughly double the headroom of V3 and an added boost mode for a second, higher-gain voice.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Sweeps from completely clean to full rock-and-roll drive; treble content increases slightly as Gain is turned up
- **Tone**: Basic high-pass filter — clockwise brighter, counterclockwise darker
- **Volume**: Master output level

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Gain toggle switch**: Activates a boost mode that is more powerful and low-end-heavy, giving higher-gain tones than any prior Morning Glory when Gain is set high; indicated by the status LED switching from blue to red
- **Remote Gain jack**: 1/4" jack on the upper right side accepting a JHS Red Remote footswitch for hands-free, mid-song switching between the standard and boost voicings
- **Side-mounted bright-cut switch**: Tames high end for brighter amp/guitar combinations

## Notes for patch-building
- Two selectable voicings (standard / boost) via the front toggle or an external Red Remote — if the patch needs both voicings mid-song, note that on the GP-5 the pedal's own toggle is a fixed setting per patch unless a Red Remote is present on the actual board to switch it live (this pedal isn't currently on Michael's fixed board).
- Genuinely transparent — good default low/medium-gain drive stage that lets the chosen AMP model's or NAM's own character come through.
- Typical placement: drive stage of the chain, ahead of AMP/CAB or a NAM.
- Standard 9V DC center negative, 43mA.
