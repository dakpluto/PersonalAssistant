# JHS 424 Gain Stage

Preamp/overdrive/distortion pedal modeled on the mic-preamp gain stages of a vintage TASCAM 424 four-track cassette recorder, with two cascading gain stages, active bass/treble EQ, and a balanced XLR direct-out — built as much for direct-to-interface recording as for a pedalboard.

## Controls
- **Gain 1 (Trim)**: Preamplification amount applied to the input signal (first gain stage)
- **Gain 2 (Channel)**: Output/drive level of the second gain stage
- **Volume**: Overall output level (corresponds to the source 424 mixer's Master control)
- **Bass**: Cuts/boosts low frequencies
- **Treble**: Cuts/boosts high frequencies

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Ground Lift dip switch**: Side-mounted switch that lifts the ground on the balanced XLR output if a hum/buzz appears when running into a mixer or interface
- Single main footswitch, soft-touch switching with buffered bypass

## Notes for patch-building
- Two cascading gain stages (Trim into Channel) mean the pedal can cover clean-preamp-boost through cassette-recorder-style saturation into full drive/distortion depending on how the two Gain knobs are staged relative to each other.
- The XLR balanced output is a direct-recording/DI feature, not relevant to the GP-5 signal chain itself, but worth noting on the patch write-up if Michael is tracking direct alongside the board.
- Typical placement: drive/preamp stage of the chain.
- Mono 1/4" in/out plus XLR out, 9V DC center negative, 50mA.
