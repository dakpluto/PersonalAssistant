# JHS Cheese Ball

Distortion/fuzz pedal — a faithful, ground-up recreation of the Lovetone Big Cheese, a rare boutique fuzz from the English company Lovetone (founded mid-1990s), used historically by players like The Edge, Jimmy Page, Kevin Shields, and Gary Moore. JHS built it by directly copying an original Big Cheese circuit.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level
- **Gain**: Distortion/fuzz intensity
- **Tone**: Frequency shaping (disengaged entirely in the mode selector's "Off" position)

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Mode selector (4-position rotary)**: Off (bright, trashy fuzz/distortion voicing, Tone disengaged) / 1 (mid-scooped, rounder tone) / 2 (mid-boosted, cuts through a mix) / 3 (biting, gated fuzz with a spitting character)
- **Footswitch**: On/off

## Notes for patch-building
- The Mode selector does most of the heavy lifting tonally — it's a fixed (non-footswitchable) choice, so pick the mode that fits the song when building a static patch.
- Ranges from vintage-fuzz trashiness through scooped/boosted mid voicings to a gated, spitty high-gain fuzz — versatile within the fuzz/distortion space but not a low-gain drive pedal.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V DC center-negative, 9mA current draw.
