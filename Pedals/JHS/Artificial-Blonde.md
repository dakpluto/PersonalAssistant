# JHS Artificial Blonde

**Not a Fender-style clean boost/overdrive** — the Artificial Blonde is Madison Cunningham's signature true pitch-vibrato pedal, built as an "always-on" modulation tool with dual switchable presets and true stereo output for a wide "3D" soundscape.

## Controls
- **Speed**: Vibrato rate. Left is slower, right is faster.
- **Depth**: Vibrato intensity. Left is less, right is more.
- **EQ**: Active tilt EQ. Noon is flat; left boosts lows/cuts highs, right boosts highs/cuts lows.
- **Volume**: Overall output level. Left is less, right is more.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Left footswitch**: Bypass, effect on/off. When off, buffered bypass — both outputs pass the dry signal.
- **Right footswitch**: Preset select, toggles between Preset A and Preset B (default on power-up is Preset B). Each preset stores its own Speed/Depth combination within the same range, letting two distinct vibrato settings be switched between live.

## Notes for patch-building
- True pitch vibrato (pitch modulation), not a chorus or amplitude tremolo, and not any kind of drive/boost — reclassify if it was filed under overdrive in planning notes.
- Stereo-capable: has both a mono output and a dedicated stereo output for a widened "3D" effect when run into two amps/inputs; mono output alone gives standard vibrato.
- The two footswitch-selectable presets (A/B) are a genuine two-voicing feature within a single pedal instance — useful for a rhythm/lead vibrato-depth split without needing two pedal slots, unlike most single-footswitch modulation pedals.
- Typical placement: modulation section of the chain, usually after drive/distortion, before or after delay depending on desired interaction.
- Buffered bypass. 9V DC center-negative (max 9V, do not exceed), 138mA draw.
