# JHS 3 Series Flanger

Compact analog-style flanger in JHS's $99 3 Series line, inspired by classic (and expensive) analog flangers like the Electro-Harmonix Electric Mistress, the Tycobrahe Pedalflanger, and the MXR M117 Flanger.

## Controls
- **Blend**: Mix of the flanged signal against the dry signal
- **Rate**: Speed of the flange sweep (clockwise = faster)
- **Intensity**: Amount/depth of the flange effect (left = mild, right = extreme)
- **Tape toggle**: Engages a mode replicating hardware tape flanging

## Notes for patch-building
- Ranges from gentle, subtle swirl to full jet-plane sweep; Intensity is the main "how extreme" control
- Tape mode gives a smoother, more organic flange character versus the standard analog voicing
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (70mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
