# EHX by JHS – Big Muff 2

Fuzz pedal co-produced by Electro-Harmonix and JHS Pedals, built from a hand-drawn schematic labeled "BIG MUFF USING (2 DUAL OP AMPS)" found in EHX designer Bob Myer's archives — a dual-op-amp Big Muff variant distinct from the classic transistor-based Big Muff Pi circuit (that standalone EHX pedal is documented separately elsewhere in this repo). The op-amp architecture pushes the voicing toward a more aggressive, cutting fuzz with emphasized low end and midrange, rather than the smoother, violin-like sustain of the original Big Muff.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level — left quieter, right louder
- **Sustain**: Fuzz amount/sustain length — left less, right more
- **Tone**: Overall tonal character — left darker, right brighter

## Notes for patch-building
- Distinct circuit from a standard EHX Big Muff Pi — expect a more aggressive, mid-forward, cutting fuzz rather than the classic scooped/sustaining Muff voicing; don't substitute one for the other when a patch specifically calls for this pedal.
- Classic 3-knob Big Muff-style control set (Volume/Sustain/Tone), housed in the traditional Big Muff big-box wedge enclosure.
- Typical placement: fuzz/distortion stage of the chain.
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center negative, 18mA.
