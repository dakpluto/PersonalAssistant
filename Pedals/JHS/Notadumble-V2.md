# JHS Notadümblë V2

Dual-channel DIY-kit pedal (Howard Dumble-style boutique amp voicing) combining a Dumble-inspired overdrive/preamp channel with a separate clean-boost channel that includes its own effects loop, released as a corrected/expanded second edition of JHS's original Notadümblë kit. Each channel has its own footswitch and can run independently or together, with a front-panel switch setting which channel comes first in the signal path when both are engaged.

## Controls
**Overdrive channel:**
- **Volume**: Overall output level of the overdrive channel
- **Drive**: Saturation/gain amount
- **EQ**: Low-pass filter — turning left cuts more high frequency
- **Presence**: Upper-mid and treble content in the gain stage

**Clean/boost channel:**
- **Output**: Level sent out of the channel's effects-loop send
- **Input**: Input impedance/gain-staging control at the channel's front end — affects how bright, dark, tight, or full the incoming signal feels (not a standard gain knob)

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Left (Red) footswitch**: Overdrive channel on/off
- **Right (Green) footswitch**: Clean/boost channel on/off
- **Order switch**: Front slide switch selecting which channel comes first in the chain when both channels are engaged together

## Notes for patch-building
- Two independently footswitchable voicings in one box — Dumble-style boutique overdrive plus a separate clean boost/utility channel — closer to two pedals than one, so it can cover both a rhythm/clean-boost role and a lead-drive role from a single slot if the board has room for both footswitches.
- The clean channel's onboard 1/4" TRS effects loop (via a TRS-to-TS/TS Y-cable) lets other pedals be inserted only into that channel's path — useful for isolating an external drive/modulation so it only engages with the clean channel active.
- Typical placement: drive section of the chain, ahead of AMP; works well feeding into an already-lower-gain amp voicing since the overdrive channel supplies its own boutique-amp-style breakup.
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center negative, 25mA, do not exceed 9V.
