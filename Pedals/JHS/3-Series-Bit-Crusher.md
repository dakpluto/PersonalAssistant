# JHS 3 Series Bit Crusher

Digital lo-fi/degradation pedal in JHS's compact $99 3 Series line, built around a "zero-hole filter" circuit that lets the Crush and Sample Rate controls dig into harsh, aliased, digital-decimation textures rather than just a single crush effect.

## Controls
- **Crush**: Bit-depth reduction — counterclockwise is clean 24-bit resolution, clockwise crushes down to 1-bit
- **Sample Rate**: Sample-rate reduction — clockwise is a clean 32.768kHz, counterclockwise drops as low as 2.5Hz
- **Filter**: Shapes the crushed signal — fully clockwise is unfiltered, counterclockwise sweeps the filter's corner frequency down
- **Type toggle**: Down engages a second-order low-pass filter (Oberheim-inspired); up applies high-pass then low-pass filtering in succession

## Notes for patch-building
- Extreme end of both Crush and Sample Rate gets to full digital breakup/noise territory fast; keep both low-to-mid for usable "old sampler" grit
- Works well after DST/AMP as a texture effect, or run pre-drive for gated/glitchy input harmonics
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (65mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
