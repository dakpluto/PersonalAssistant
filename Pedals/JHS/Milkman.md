# JHS Milkman

A collaboration between JHS and Milkman Sound (Tim Marcus) combining a slap/echo delay and a clean boost in one dual-footswitch enclosure — not an overdrive pedal (correcting an assumption before research: the boost side is a clean, discrete-amplifier-based boost, not a gain/distortion circuit). Aimed at country, Americana, slide, and rockabilly players who want slapback delay and an always-available clean push in one box.

## Controls
**Delay side (left):**
- **Slap**: Delay time, roughly 0ms (doubling/thickening effect) up to 250ms
- **Mix**: Echo/repeat volume relative to dry signal
- **Repeat**: Number of delay repeats
- **EQ**: Brightness/darkness of the repeats, from darker/ambient to brighter/cutting

**Boost side (right):**
- **Volume**: Discrete-amplifier-based clean boost level — unity gain sits around the 10 o'clock position; below that it acts as a slight attenuator, above it boosts cleanly, and pushed further it can drive a tube amp into natural overdrive

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Two independent footswitches**: One engages/bypasses the slap delay, the other engages/bypasses the boost — either can run alone or both together

## Notes for patch-building
- The boost can double as an always-on tone enhancer (set near/at unity to fatten and clarify the signal) or as a solo/lead boost when turned up past unity.
- Typical placement: boost side works well either up front (pushing everything downstream) or at the end of the chain into the amp; delay side belongs in the time-based/ambient part of the chain as usual for a slapback delay.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V DC negative, 27mA current draw.
