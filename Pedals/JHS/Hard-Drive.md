# JHS Hard Drive

High-gain, '90s-modern-voiced distortion built around cascaded gain stages feeding a hard-limiting clipping section, followed by a class-AB amp stage driving a sweepable-midrange EQ plus Baxandall bass/treble — a more aggressive, more heavily EQ'd counterpart to JHS's lower-gain drive pedals.

## Controls
- **Drive (Distortion)**: Amount of drive/distortion. Left is less, right is more.
- **Bass**: Bass frequency level. Noon is flat, left cuts, right boosts.
- **Mid Freq**: Sets which mid frequency range the Middle knob boosts or scoops (sweepable midrange).
- **Middle**: Mid frequency level at the range set by Mid Freq. Noon is flat, left scoops, right boosts.
- **Treble**: Treble frequency level. Noon is flat, left cuts, right boosts.
- **Volume**: Overall output level. Left is less, right is more.

## Notes for patch-building
- Modern high-gain distortion with real EQ shaping power (four-band-equivalent via Bass/Mid Freq/Middle/Treble) — capable of scooped-mid metal-adjacent voicings or boosted-mid cutting leads depending on Mid Freq placement.
- Typical placement: main distortion stage of the chain, into an amp set relatively clean/lower-gain since the Hard Drive is doing the heavy lifting itself.
- Silent soft-touch switching, buffered bypass. Mono in/out, standard 9V DC center-negative, 78mA draw.
