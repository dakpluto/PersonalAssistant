# JHS Coyote

Octave fuzz pedal based on what JHS describes as "a lost circuit that belongs to no known lineage" — a single multi-function control sweeps continuously between a gated swell effect, a Tone Bender-style fuzz, and an octave-up fuzz, with strong touch-sensitivity and volume-knob cleanup unusual for the octave-fuzz category.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level, minimum at full counterclockwise to maximum at full clockwise
- **Swell / Fuzz / Octave**: Single continuous sweep through three zones — full counterclockwise gives a gated, reversed-tape-style swell (notes bloom in from silence); noon/center gives a full Tone Bender-style fuzz (rich low end, aggressive mids, handles chords); clockwise gives an aggressive octave-up fuzz with snarling harmonics

## Notes for patch-building
- Effectively three fuzz/dynamics voicings from one knob — pick a fixed Swell/Fuzz/Octave position appropriate to the song when building a static patch (e.g. center for a straightforward fuzz tone, full CCW for ambient swells).
- Notably touch-sensitive/volume-cleanup-friendly for an octave fuzz, so guitar volume-knob rolloff can be part of the patch's playing technique rather than relying on CTL switching.
- Typical placement: fuzz/distortion stage of the chain.
- True bypass. Mono in/out, 9V DC center negative, 5mA — do not exceed 9V.
