# JHS Crayon

Preamp/distortion/fuzz pedal — a simplified, single-footswitch version of JHS's Colour Box preamp concept, going from gritty preamp-style drive up to gated, compressed fuzz depending on gain staging between its two volume-type controls.

## Controls
- **Master**: Overall output volume
- **Pre-Vol**: Gain staging control — moves the voice from gritty preamp drive toward over-the-top gated fuzz as it's pushed
- **Tilt**: Active tilt EQ — flat at 12 o'clock, clockwise boosts highs/cuts bass, counterclockwise boosts bass/cuts highs

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Hi-Pass toggle**: Engages a low-end cut, useful for cutting through a mix or for a thinner, lo-fi voicing
- **Hi-Pass dip switch**: Selects the Hi-Pass corner frequency, 200Hz or 750Hz
- **Footswitch**: On/off

## Notes for patch-building
- Pre-Vol is the key gain-shaping control — treat it like a gain knob more than the Master, since it's what pushes the circuit from clean-ish preamp coloration into fuzz territory.
- Tilt EQ is a fast way to brighten or darken the voice without hunting through separate bass/treble knobs.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V DC center-negative, 15mA current draw.
