# JHS 3 Series Overdrive

Compact overdrive in JHS's $99 3 Series line, offering mild-to-moderate gain and EQ shaping with a switchable clipping voicing.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level
- **Body**: EQ control shaping the pedal's midrange/low-end character
- **Drive**: Amount of gain/overdrive
- **Gain toggle**: Selects between a more saturated/compressed voicing and a more open/crunchy voicing

## Notes for patch-building
- Gain toggle is the main voicing fork — open/crunchy side for a transparent boost-into-amp feel, saturated/compressed side for a thicker, more self-contained drive
- Body knob stands in for a broader tone control than a typical single-knob overdrive, useful for tightening low end ahead of a high-gain AMP model
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (12mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
