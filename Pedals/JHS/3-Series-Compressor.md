# JHS 3 Series Compressor

Simple, straightforward compressor in JHS's $99 3 Series line, aimed at everything from subtle tone-smoothing to a fully squashed sound.

## Controls
- **Attack**: How quickly the compressor engages after picking
- **Sustain**: Amount of compression/sustain applied
- **Volume**: Output level, for makeup gain
- **Bright toggle**: Engages a brighter EQ voicing when up

## Notes for patch-building
- Low current draw (8mA) and always-on-friendly circuit — a natural pick for an always-on compressor ahead of drive/amp
- Bright toggle is handy for compensating for the treble loss compression can introduce, especially in front of darker amp models
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative, single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
