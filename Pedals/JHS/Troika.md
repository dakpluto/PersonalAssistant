# JHS Troika

Studio-grade delay device co-developed by JHS Pedals and Third Man Hardware (Jack White's company), built around vocal-delay needs but usable for guitar or any line/mic source — note this is a delay pedal, not a fuzz, despite "Troika" sounding like it could belong with JHS's multi-fuzz-style products. It's built around a Lundahl transformer mic preamp on its XLR input and offers separate wet/dry outputs for studio-style routing alongside standard delay controls.

## Controls
- **Repeats**: Feedback amount — down for fewer repeats, up for more
- **Volume**: Delay (wet) signal level — down quieter, up louder
- **Distance**: Delay time, roughly 80ms to 1 second — down shortest, up longest
- **Mic Gain**: Preamp gain for the XLR mic input, +20dB to +80dB, located on the right side of the unit

## Footswitches / Switches
- **MIC/INST internal dip switch**: Selects whether the input is treated as a balanced XLR mic signal (routed through the onboard Lundahl transformer preamp) or a 1/4" instrument-level signal
- **TRAILS/NO TRAILS internal dip switch**: Sets whether delay repeats continue to ring out after bypass (trails) or cut off immediately (no trails)
- Single footswitch, silent soft-touch switching with buffered bypass

## Notes for patch-building
- A collaboration piece (JHS x Third Man Hardware) built as much for studio/vocal use as for a guitar pedalboard — its XLR mic input, phantom power passthrough, and separate wet/dry outs are studio-routing features not relevant to a standard GP-5 chain, but the Repeats/Volume/Distance controls behave like a conventional analog-style delay when run 1/4" instrument-in.
- Set the MIC/INST dip switch to INST for guitar-pedalboard use.
- Typical placement: delay stage of the chain (equivalent to GP-5's DLY slot) if used as an external delay pedal rather than through the GP-5's own DLY module.
- Power/voltage not confirmed on the product page — check the unit or manual before powering from a shared daisy-chain supply.
