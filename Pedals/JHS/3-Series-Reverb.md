# JHS 3 Series Reverb

General-purpose reverb in JHS's $99 3 Series line, with EQ shaping and a pre-delay option on top of the standard verb/decay controls.

## Controls
- **Verb**: Mix of reverb blended with the dry signal
- **EQ**: Tone of the reverb, clockwise brightens/adds high-frequency content, counterclockwise darkens/warms it
- **Decay**: Length of the reverb tail
- **Pre-Delay toggle**: Adds a short delay before the reverb onset, separating the dry note from the reverb for more clarity or a larger sense of space

## Notes for patch-building
- The general-purpose, unspecialized reverb in the 3 Series lineup — use this where the patch wants a straightforward room/plate-style verb rather than the Hall, Octave, or other specialized 3 Series reverbs
- EQ knob is useful for taming reverb brightness ahead of high-gain AMP models where a bright verb tail can get harsh
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (74mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
