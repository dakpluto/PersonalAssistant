# JHS 3 Series Fuzz

Compact fuzz in JHS's $99 3 Series line, aiming for "the perfect balance of vintage and modern" with a Fuzz control usable across its full sweep for tones ranging from distortion into pushed-amp-style overdrive.

## Controls
- **Bias**: Gates/starves the fuzz circuit — turning up gives a more gated, choked sound
- **Fuzz**: Amount of fuzz saturation
- **Volume**: Output level
- **Fat toggle**: Engages a bass boost for a fatter, more powerful low end

## Notes for patch-building
- Bias knob is the character control — low settings are more sputtery/gated, higher settings smooth toward sustained fuzz
- Fat toggle helps compensate for the low-end loss fuzz circuits often have, especially useful on single-coil guitars or thinner amp models
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (9mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
