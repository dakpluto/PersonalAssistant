# JHS PG-14

Paul Gilbert signature distortion pedal — not a Two-Rock-style preamp (correcting an assumption before research: JHS's own product page describes it as a dedicated FET-based distortion circuit designed to Paul Gilbert's specifications, emulating the touch and response of a pushed tube amp at low/moderate volumes). Built around a FET distortion engine with an active mid-frequency preamp stage in front of it for extended tone-shaping range.

## Controls
- **Push**: Drives the input of a preamp stage in front of the FET distortion circuit, emulating an overloaded preamp hitting the front of a tube amp; interacts strongly with Drive and is sensitive to pick attack
- **Drive**: Gain/saturation amount of the FET distortion stage
- **Volume**: Master output level
- **Tone**: Overall high-end/brightness shaping
- **Mid**: Boosts or cuts the frequency selected by Mid Freq
- **Mid Freq**: Sweeps the frequency the Mid knob acts on, roughly 400Hz–7.5kHz

## Notes for patch-building
- Push and Drive work together rather than independently — low Push with moderate Drive gives smoother, amp-like overdrive; higher Push adds a distinct gain boost and more aggressive saturation, closer to stacking two gain stages.
- Mid/Mid Freq give this pedal unusually deep tone-shaping for a distortion pedal — useful for carving a lead voice that cuts without just adding more gain.
- Typical placement: main drive/distortion slot in the chain; pairs well as a single do-it-all gain stage given its wide clean-to-high-gain range.
- Single footswitch on/off. Mono in/out, standard 9V DC center-negative, 36mA current draw.
