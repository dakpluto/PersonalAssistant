# JHS Notaklön

Transparent, Klon Centaur-style overdrive in a compact solderless-kit-derived enclosure — three knobs plus a "Shamrock" mod switch that recreates the internal modification JHS applied to Klon KTR units for several years. Internally steps the 9V supply up to 18V for extra headroom, the same trick the original circuit relies on.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Overdrive amount. Fully left is clean (usable as up to +16dB of clean boost in combination with Output); fully right is maximum overdrive saturation. Turning clockwise also pushes more mid-frequency content, characteristic of the Klon circuit topology.
- **Treble**: Treble boost/cut. Noon is neutral.
- **Output**: Overall volume. Left is less, right is more.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Shamrock Mod switch**: Adds +4dB of gain with a further push in the mid frequencies and an extra stage of hard-edge clipping — turns the transparent low/mid-gain Klon voicing into something noticeably more saturated and aggressive.
- Single footswitch, on/off.

## Notes for patch-building
- Also sold in **Splatter** and **Pink** limited colorways — identical circuit and control layout to the standard black Notaklön, cosmetic difference only.
- Classic "clean boost that doesn't sound like a clean boost" — great as an always-on mid-push in front of an amp set for edge-of-breakup, or dial Gain up for its own transparent overdrive character. The Shamrock switch is the tool for pushing past transparent into a hotter, more compressed drive without changing knob positions.
- Typical placement: first or second drive stage, ahead of higher-gain distortion/fuzz — designed to push an amp rather than replace one.
- Mono in/out (1/4" jacks top-mounted), 9V DC center-negative, 30mA draw (internally boosted to 18V — do not exceed 9V input, it will void the warranty).
