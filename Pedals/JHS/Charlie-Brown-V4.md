# JHS Charlie Brown V4

Mid-gain overdrive modeled on a Marshall Bluesbreaker/JTM45-style circuit — JHS's own copy describes it as turning any amp into "a gritty JTM45 stack." The V4 revision added a full 3-band active tone stack on top of the original two-knob (Drive/Volume) design.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Gain amount; interacts with Volume similarly to how gain/master volume interact on a real amp head
- **Volume**: Output level, works together with Drive for the overall dynamic feel
- **Bass**: Active EQ, boosts/cuts low frequencies
- **Middle**: Active EQ, boosts/cuts midrange
- **Treble**: Active EQ, boosts/cuts high frequencies

## Notes for patch-building
- Voiced for sixties-style British crunch through to thicker, more liquid rock overdrive — lower-to-medium gain range compared to JHS's own Angry Charlie (JCM800-style, higher gain).
- Stacks well with other drives — commonly run ahead of a higher-gain distortion as the "always-on" grit stage, or alone for classic-rock breakup.
- Single footswitch on/off. Mono in/out, standard 9V DC center-negative, 9mA current draw.
