# JHS Colour Box V2

Studio-grade tube-flavored preamp / DI / tone-shaping utility modeled after console mic-preamp circuitry (a "Neve-style preamp in a pedal"), with a balanced XLR output for going straight to a mixer or interface alongside its 1/4" instrument path. V2 expands the original Colour Box's EQ and adds a Hi/Lo headroom switch and phantom-power pass-through for condenser mics.

## Controls
- **Master**: Overall output volume (affects the 1/4" output only — the XLR send stays at a consistent level for front-of-house).
- **Pre-Vol**: Gain between the two internal gain stages — functions as a drive/gain control, adding grit, distortion, or fuzz as it's increased.
- **Step** (5-position rotary): Sets how much gain each Pre-Vol increment adds — 1st position +18dB, 2nd +23dB, 3rd +28dB, 4th +33dB, 5th +39dB.
- **Treble**: ±15dB boost/cut, flat at noon.
- **Treble Shift**: Sets the center frequency the Treble knob acts on, sweepable roughly 2kHz–30kHz.
- **Middle**: ±10dB boost/cut, flat at noon.
- **Middle Shift**: Sets the center frequency the Middle knob acts on, sweepable roughly 150Hz–2.4kHz.
- **Bass**: ±15dB boost/cut, flat at noon.
- **Bass Shift**: Sets the center frequency the Bass knob acts on, sweepable roughly 20Hz–440Hz.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Hi/Lo toggle**: Sets clean headroom. Lo = ultra-clean, no breakup, good for pure tone-shaping without distortion. Hi = distorts much more readily, opening up a range of overdrive/distortion/fuzz textures from the preamp stage itself.
- **Hi-Pass Filter toggle**: Engages a 12dB/octave high-pass filter with an adjustable cutoff (roughly 160Hz–650Hz).
- **Input selector (INST/XLR)**: Switches the combo input jack between 1/4" instrument and XLR microphone input.
- **-20dB Pad switch**: Attenuates high-output microphones; only active in XLR input mode.
- **Ground lift switch**: Reduces hum/ground-loop noise on the XLR output.
- **Phase invert switch**: Flips signal polarity on the XLR output.
- Single footswitch — bypass behavior depends on input mode: in INST mode it fully bypasses the 1/4" path; in XLR mode the XLR output is disengaged when the pedal is switched off (buffered bypass, silent switching, not true bypass).

## Notes for patch-building
- Not an on-off effect in the usual GP-5-patch sense — it's a preamp/EQ/DI utility pedal that typically sits at the very front (as a drive/tone-shaping preamp) or the very end (as a DI/EQ stage before FOH) of a rig, and isn't part of the GP-5's own signal chain since it's an external XLR/1/4" hardware box.
- Useful reference for patch-building context when Michael is discussing his live rig's front-of-house signal path or studio DI'ing, separate from the GP-5's internal module chain.
- Passes 48V phantom power through to condenser mics plugged into its XLR input.
- Predecessor to the newer **Colour Box 10** (10th-anniversary model) — the V2 remains in JHS's catalog as the standard current version; the Colour Box 10 is a separate, more recent anniversary edition, not a direct replacement.
- Standard 9V DC center-negative (max 9V, do not exceed).
