# JHS 3 Series Octave Reverb

Shimmer-style reverb in JHS's $99 3 Series line, a modern take on the "Shimmer" effect Brian Eno invented in the early 1980s and popularized on U2's *The Unforgettable Fire* — combines octave-shifted signal with the reverb trail.

## Controls
- **Reverb (Verb)**: Amount of reverb blended in
- **Octave**: Blend amount of the octave layer within the reverb
- **Decay**: Length of the reverb trail
- **Octave toggle**: Selects whether the octave layer is pitched up or down

## Notes for patch-building
- Octave-up (shimmer) setting is the classic ambient/pad texture; octave-down gives a darker, more organ-like wash
- Octave knob controls how prominent the pitched layer is relative to plain reverb — low settings keep it subtle, high settings make it the dominant texture
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (65mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
