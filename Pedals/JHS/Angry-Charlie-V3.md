# JHS Angry Charlie V3

High-gain overdrive/distortion modeled on a Marshall JCM800-style circuit, with a 3-band active EQ giving it Marshall-head-like tone-stack control. Ranges from bluesy vintage crunch (Drive low) to aggressive high-gain distortion (Drive high) — JHS's own copy frames it as a high-gain-territory pedal.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Gain/distortion intensity — lower settings give bluesy, vintage-amp-like breakup, higher settings push into aggressive high-gain territory
- **Volume**: Output level, interacts with Drive similarly to a master volume on an amp head
- **Bass**: Active 3-band EQ, boosts/cuts low frequencies
- **Middle**: Active 3-band EQ, boosts/cuts midrange
- **Treble**: Active 3-band EQ, boosts/cuts high frequencies

## Notes for patch-building
- The highest-gain of JHS's Marshall-style overdrives (paired with the lower/mid-gain Charlie Brown) — good candidate for a dedicated lead/high-gain rhythm voice rather than an always-on grit stage.
- Active 3-band EQ gives real tone-stack-style shaping, so it can dial in anything from a JCM800-ish crunch to scooped modern high-gain.
- Single footswitch on/off. Mono in/out, 9V–18V DC center-negative (18V raises headroom).
