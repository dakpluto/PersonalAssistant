# JHS Flight Delay

Analog-voiced delay distilling three of JHS founder Josh Scott's favorite delay circuits — BBD-style analog, digital reverse, and clean digital repeats — into one selectable-mode pedal with built-in modulation and tap tempo, deliberately kept simple (no menus/presets) compared to more complex modern delays.

## Controls
- **Time**: Delay length, 50ms to 1 second. Left is shorter, right is longer.
- **Mix**: Balance of delay signal against dry. Fully left is dry, fully right is fully wet, noon is 50/50.
- **Repeats**: Feedback / number of repeats. Left is fewer, right is more, with fully right giving self-oscillating infinite repeats.
- **EQ**: Tone-shapes the delay repeats (not the dry signal). Left is darker/more subdued, right is brighter/crisper.
- **Mod Depth**: Modulation intensity applied to the repeats. Left is subtler, right is more pronounced.
- **Mod Rate**: Modulation speed. Left is slower, right is faster.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Mode toggle** (3-position): Analog (BBD-style, warmer/darker degrading repeats), Reverse (backwards-playback repeats), Digital (clean, crisp DSP-style repeats).
- **Subdivision toggle** (3-position): Sets repeat interval relative to tapped tempo — quarter note, eighth note, or dotted eighth note.
- **Modulation Type toggle** (3-position): Chorus, Off, Vibrato — sets the character of the Mod Depth/Rate modulation.
- **Left footswitch**: Bypass, effect on/off.
- **Right footswitch**: Tap tempo — tap two or more times to set delay Time.
- **Tap/Exp jack**: Accepts an external tap-tempo footswitch or an expression pedal.

## Notes for patch-building
- Because Mode, Subdivision, and Mod Type are toggle switches rather than footswitch-controlled, a GP-5 patch needs one fixed choice per patch for each — pick Analog for warmer/degrading repeats, Digital for clean rhythmic delay, or Reverse as a specialty texture.
- Trails bypass: when switched off, existing repeats continue to ring out and decay naturally rather than cutting off — worth noting in patch documentation since it affects how a bypass moment sounds live.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V DC center-negative (max 9V), 85mA draw.
