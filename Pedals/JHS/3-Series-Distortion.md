# JHS 3 Series Distortion

Compact high-gain distortion in JHS's $99 3 Series line, offering heavy gain and aggressive harmonic saturation with a switchable clipping voicing.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level
- **Filter**: Tone control, shapes overall brightness/darkness
- **Distort**: Gain/distortion amount
- **Gain toggle**: Selects between a more saturated/compressed voicing and a more open/crunchy voicing

## Notes for patch-building
- Gain toggle is the key voicing choice — crunchy/open side works well for classic rock rhythm, saturated/compressed side for thicker lead tones
- Low current draw (9mA) — easy to slot into a pedalboard's DST position ahead of an AMP model without power budget concerns
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative, single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
