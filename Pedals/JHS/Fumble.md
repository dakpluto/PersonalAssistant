# JHS Fumble

JFET clean-boost/buffer pedal derived from a 1970s Barcus-Berry acoustic preamp circuit — a simple, transparent tone-enhancing boost rather than an overdrive or Klon-style gain device, despite the "boost that Klon players will recognize the appeal of" territory it's often discussed in.

## Controls
- **Output**: Master volume — turn up for more overall level
- **Input**: Not a standard gain knob — simultaneously attenuates bass and input gain at the front of the circuit. Fully clockwise = no cut (effectively bypasses the control); rolling left progressively thins and tightens the response, rolling right fills out and louden it

## Notes for patch-building
- Transparent, JFET-based clean boost — good as an always-on buffer/tone-enhancer, a front-end booster placed ahead of an overdrive/distortion to push it harder, an amp-slammer, or a solo-volume boost.
- Not a gain/distortion source itself — pairs with GP-5 DST/AMP gain rather than replacing it.
- True bypass.
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center negative, 5mA.
