# JHS 3 Series Rotary Chorus

Rotary-speaker emulation in JHS's $99 3 Series line, recreating the Doppler-effect modulation of a Leslie speaker originally designed for Hammond organs.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Overall output level (left less, right more)
- **Intensity**: Modulation depth (left less, right more)
- **Speed**: Rate of modulation (left slower, right faster)
- **Room toggle**: Down is the standard rotary effect; up adds a roomy reverb on top

## Notes for patch-building
- A distinct modulation flavor from chorus/flanger/phaser — the Doppler-style pitch/amplitude wobble reads as more "swirling speaker" than typical pedal chorus
- Room toggle up is a quick way to get a rotary-plus-ambience texture without a separate reverb slot
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (65mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
