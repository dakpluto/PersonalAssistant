# JHS 3 Series Hall Reverb

Large-space reverb in JHS's $99 3 Series line, inspired by the sound of cathedrals and concert halls where natural reverb decay seems to go on forever.

## Controls
- **Verb**: Mix of reverb blended with the dry signal
- **Dampen**: Rolls off high frequency from the reverb as it's turned clockwise
- **Decay**: Length of the reverb tail — full clockwise approaches an almost infinite reverb space
- **Modulate toggle**: Up adds slow modulation/movement to the reverb decay for a detuned, 3D-feeling reverb tail

## Notes for patch-building
- Dampen is the tone-shaping control — roll it back for a brighter, more present hall; increase it for a darker, more distant wash
- Modulate toggle up turns this into a shimmer-adjacent ambient reverb, useful as a distinct RVB voicing from a plain spring/room reverb elsewhere on the board
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (70mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
