# JHS AT Mini

Compact, single-channel version of the JHS AT+ (Andy Timmons signature overdrive/distortion), voiced after "the punch, saturation, and unmistakable feeling of standing in front of a stack of loud amplifiers" — a British-amp-in-a-box drive built around the same circuit as the original JHS Angry Charlie that Andy Timmons adopted as his lead channel. The AT Mini uses the AT+'s original V1 circuit permanently set to the AT+'s "100-watt" mode (the most open, loudest, least-compressed of that pedal's three wattage voicings) and drops the AT+'s independent footswitchable boost channel and wattage toggle to fit a smaller, cheaper enclosure.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level
- **Drive**: Gain/saturation amount, ranges from high-gain blues breakup to heavy British-voiced distortion
- **EQ**: High-pass filter — clockwise brighter, counterclockwise darker
- **Air**: Amp-style presence control for top-end shimmer/sizzle — clockwise brighter, counterclockwise darker

## Notes for patch-building
- Mid-to-high-gain drive/distortion voiced like a cranked British stack (Marshall-ish) rather than a transparent overdrive — pairs well ahead of a clean-ish GP-5 AMP model when the pedal itself should supply the gain and distortion character.
- No wattage toggle or boost channel like the full-size AT+ — it's a single fixed voicing, so dial Drive/EQ/Air once for the patch rather than expecting a footswitchable second mode from this pedal.
- True bypass, no internal toggle switches.
- Mono in/out, 9–18V DC center-negative (10mA at 9V), single footswitch on/off with LED.
