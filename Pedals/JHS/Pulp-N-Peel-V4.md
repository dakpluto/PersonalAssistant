# JHS Pulp 'N' Peel V4

Compressor / preamp / DI box, the fourth revision of one of JHS's original flagship pedals. It's built as a transparent, studio-grade compressor that preserves an instrument's natural attack rather than squashing it, with a parallel blend control, an active EQ, and a balanced XLR DI output alongside the standard 1/4" jack — designed to double as a stage/studio DI box as much as a compressor.

## Controls
- **Sustain**: Amount of compression — clockwise rolls in more compression/sustain
- **Blend**: Parallel mix between the compressed signal and the dry (uncompressed) signal, mixing-console style
- **Tone**: Active EQ that cuts or boosts high end on the affected signal
- **Volume**: Side-mounted output level control with extra headroom on tap

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Dirt mini toggle** (side-mounted): Switches the compressor's character between clean studio compression and a warmer, grittier tonal coloration
- **Ground-Lift switch**: Eliminates hum/buzz caused by ground loops when using the XLR DI output
- **Footswitch**: Soft-touch, buffered-bypass on/off for the compressor

## Notes for patch-building
- Always-on buffer stage regardless of bypass state — signal is buffered even when the compressor effect itself is off, which is generally desirable ahead of long cable runs or pedal chains.
- Typical placement: first or near-first in the chain, right after any tuner — compression this transparent works best shaping dynamics before drive/gain stages color the signal.
- The XLR DI output runs simultaneously with the 1/4" output, so it can feed a mixing board/interface directly while the 1/4" continues to the rest of the pedalboard/amp.
- Mono 1/4" in/out plus balanced XLR DI out, standard 9V DC center-negative, 32mA current draw.
