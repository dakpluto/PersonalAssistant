# JHS Moonshine V2

Overdrive pedal built as a heavily modified take on "the most famous overdrive ever created" (JHS's own framing — a Tube Screamer-derived circuit), intentionally voiced to not be transparent: it adds its own mid-heavy character and attitude rather than just pushing an amp cleanly. An internal charge pump converts the 9V supply to 18V internally for extra headroom and low-end punch.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Gain/saturation amount
- **Tone**: Smooth high-end shaping without harsh peaks
- **Clean**: Blends in the unaffected dry signal alongside the overdriven tone
- **Volume**: Output level

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Proof toggle (2-position)**: Down = highest-gain setting with the most saturation and sustain; Up = a more open, forceful crunch tone with extra volume and headroom
- **Footswitch**: On/off

## Notes for patch-building
- Gain range spans low-gain amp-like breakup up to grinding, heavy overdrive at the edge of cranked-amp distortion — wider range than a stock Tube Screamer-style circuit.
- The Clean blend knob is useful for keeping pick/string clarity at higher Drive settings, similar in concept to a fuzz's clean-blend control.
- Proof toggle is a static, non-footswitchable voicing choice — set it for the song before building a patch.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V DC center-negative, 30mA current draw.
