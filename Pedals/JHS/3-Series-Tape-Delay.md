# JHS 3 Series Tape Delay

Tape-echo emulation in JHS's $99 3 Series line, inspired by classic tape delay units going back to the 1953 Echosonic (the first tape delay) through units like the Roland Space Echo RE-201.

## Controls
- **Mix**: Blend of delayed/clean signal (left dry, right wet)
- **Tape Speed**: Delay time, 15ms to 950ms
- **Repeats**: Number of repeats (left fewer, right more)
- **Flutter toggle**: Down is standard; up adds tape-style pitch modulation/warble to the repeats

## Notes for patch-building
- Flutter toggle up is what sells the "tape" character — wow-and-flutter pitch instability on the repeats versus a clean digital echo
- Wide delay-time range (15ms–950ms) covers slapback through long ambient tape-echo washes from one pedal
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (65mA draw) — do not exceed 9V, single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
