# JHS 3 Series Delay

Compact digital delay in JHS's $99 3 Series line, switchable between a clean digital voicing and a darker, bucket-brigade-style analog voicing, with 80ms–800ms of delay time.

## Controls
- **Time**: Delay time, 80ms to 800ms (slapback through long ambient washes)
- **Repeats**: Number/decay of echoes, up to near-endless self-oscillation
- **Mix**: Blend of delayed signal against the dry direct tone
- **Type toggle**: Down is a clearer digital-voiced delay; up is a darker analog voicing that runs away into "bucket brigade" oscillation when Repeats is maxed

## Notes for patch-building
- Digital mode for clean, articulate slapback/ambient repeats; analog mode for warmer, darker, more washed-out echoes
- Maxed Repeats in analog mode self-oscillates — usable as a controlled noise/texture effect, not just conventional delay
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (71mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
