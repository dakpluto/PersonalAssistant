# JHS 3 Series Screamer

Tube Screamer-style overdrive in JHS's $99 3 Series line, based on JHS's own "Tube Screamer Strong Mod" modification — built for more clean headroom, more usable range as a boost, and a more versatile drive/tone response than a stock TS9/TS808.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Overall output level
- **Tone**: Brightness — clockwise gets brighter/more biting, good for cutting leads
- **Drive**: Overdrive intensity, from subtle/creamy/warm to hot and snarling
- **Clipping toggle**: One position gives the asymmetrical "Strong Mod" clipping; the other gives symmetrical clipping for stock TS9/TS808-style sounds

## Notes for patch-building
- Toggle lets this cover both a classic transparent Tube Screamer voicing and JHS's punchier Strong Mod variant from one pedal
- Classic mid-hump overdrive — pairs well ahead of an AMP model as a lead boost, or as the sole drive source for a vintage rock tone
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (12mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
