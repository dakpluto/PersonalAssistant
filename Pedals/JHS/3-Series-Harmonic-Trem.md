# JHS 3 Series Harmonic Trem

Dual-mode tremolo in JHS's $99 3 Series line, drawing on Leo Fender's 1960 "harmonic tremolo" circuit found in Bandmaster and Vibrasonic amplifiers, alongside a standard amp-style tremolo voicing.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level of the pedal
- **Rate**: Speed of the tremolo (left slower, right faster)
- **Depth**: Depth/intensity of the tremolo effect
- **Type toggle**: Up is a standard amp-style tremolo; down is the harmonic tremolo circuit (splits the signal into high/low bands modulated out of phase)

## Notes for patch-building
- Harmonic trem mode (toggle down) has a more complex, swirling character than standard amp trem — good stand-in when a patch wants to reference vintage Fender blackface amps without a real amp trem circuit
- Standard trem mode (toggle up) is the simpler, more universally familiar pulsing volume effect
- Mono in/out, 9V DC center-negative (65mA draw), single footswitch on/off with status LED, true bypass
