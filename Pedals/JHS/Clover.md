# JHS Clover

Preamp / EQ boost pedal based on the 1984 Boss FA-1 FET preamp. Built to shape tone and push an amp or downstream dirt pedals harder, with a 3-band active EQ and a mode switch that can strip that EQ away entirely for a straight FET boost. Works across electric, acoustic, and bass.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level — sets unity gain or boosts to push an amp/dirt pedals harder
- **Bass**: Active shelving filter, ±15dB at 270Hz
- **Mids**: Active peaking filter, ±15dB at 1.3kHz
- **Treble**: Active shelving filter, ±15dB at 10kHz

## Footswitches / Switches
- **EQ Mode rotary (3-position)**: Full EQ (all three EQ knobs active) / No Mid (mids fixed, replicating the original Boss FA-1's fixed-mid voicing) / No EQ (EQ section removed entirely — straight FET boost)
- **Low Cut dip switch**: Trims low end to reduce mud and help the boosted signal cut through a mix
- **Ground-Lift switch**: Eliminates hum when using the XLR output
- **Footswitch**: On/off

## Notes for patch-building
- Best thought of as a boost-first tool: set Volume for the push amount, then use the EQ (or drop to No EQ mode) to shape or bypass tone-shaping depending on whether the goal is a clean push or an active EQ correction.
- Typical placement: front of the chain as an always-available boost into an amp or into other dirt pedals, or later in the chain as a clean EQ boost for solos.
- Includes both 1/4" and XLR outputs usable simultaneously, useful if also running a DI feed.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V DC.
