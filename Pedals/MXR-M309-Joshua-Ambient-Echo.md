# MXR M309 Joshua Ambient Echo

Digital delay/ambient echo pedal built around Edge-style dotted-eighth and multi-voice echo textures — a compact, all-in-one "soundscape" delay with octave-blended repeats, modulation, and tap tempo.

## Controls
- **Regen**: Sets the number/density of repeats (feedback amount).
- **Delay**: Sets the echo tempo (delay time); can also be set via tap tempo.
- **Division**: Sets the rhythmic ratio of the primary echoes relative to tempo (straight, dotted-eighth, triplet, etc.).
- **Voice**: Blends in a mix of octave-shifted repeats (one octave down, plus one and two octaves up) to thicken/texture the trail, up to a shimmery organ-pad-like effect.
- **Mod**: Intensity of modulation applied to the delay signal (chorus-like movement in the repeats).
- **Mix**: Dry/wet balance of the effect.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Main footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the effect; also doubles as tap-tempo entry (tap to set the Delay time) and holds/activates other functions per the manual.
- **Echo 2 button**: Adds a secondary set of echoes at a fixed 1/4-note tempo (adjustable in advanced settings) layered against the primary echoes, for a denser rhythmic pattern.
- **Trails button**: Toggles Trails mode — when on, existing echoes continue to ring out naturally after the pedal is bypassed instead of cutting off instantly.
- **CTR jack**: Accepts an expression pedal (for real-time parameter control) or an external tap-tempo switch.

## Notes for patch-building
- Functions like a self-contained ambient delay module — comparable in role to the GP-5's DLY module, but with built-in octave-voicing and modulation that the GP-5 delay algorithms don't offer in one block, making it a strong choice for shimmer/ambient textures without needing a separate MOD module.
- Bypass is **true bypass by default**, but switches to **buffered bypass automatically when Trails mode is engaged** (needed so the wet trail can continue after bypass) — worth knowing when placed in an all-true-bypass chain.
- Supports stereo output and expression pedal control via the CTR jack.
