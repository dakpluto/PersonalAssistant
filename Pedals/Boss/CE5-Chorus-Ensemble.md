# Boss CE-5 Chorus Ensemble

Stereo chorus pedal descended from the iconic 1976 CE-1 Chorus Ensemble. Adds two independent high/low filter controls on top of the standard rate/depth/level chorus controls, and outputs true stereo via dual A/B jacks.

## Controls
- **RATE**: Speed of the chorus modulation
- **DEPTH**: Intensity/depth of the pitch modulation
- **E.LEVEL**: Output level of the effect signal mixed with the dry signal
- **HIGH FILTER**: Cuts/shapes high-frequency content of the effect sound
- **LOW FILTER**: Cuts/shapes low-frequency content of the effect sound

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single pedal switch: engages/bypasses the chorus effect.
- CHECK indicator LED doubles as a battery-check light.

## Notes for patch-building
- True stereo output (OUTPUT A/B): for mono rigs use OUTPUT A only, which carries the mixed wet+dry signal; running both outputs into two amps or a stereo return gives the widest, most spacious chorus sound.
- Buffered bypass — safe anywhere in a chain, including long cable runs or in front of other buffered/analog pedals without tone-sucking concerns when off.
- Sits naturally in the modulation slot of a signal chain, typically after drive/distortion and before delay/reverb (post-gain, pre-ambience) — same conceptual spot as a GP-5 MOD block.
- The independent High/Low Filter controls make this a good pick when you need to tame a chorus that's too shimmery/harsh (roll off High Filter) or too thin/watery (roll off Low Filter), rather than just relying on Depth/Rate alone.
- A lower-cost, single-footswitch alternative to running the GP-5's own MOD chorus algorithm when a real analog stereo chorus pedal is preferred in the effects loop.
