# Boss GE-7 Equalizer

Compact 7-band graphic EQ pedal for guitar (and general instrument/mic use). A standard boost/cut tone-shaping tool rather than an effect — widely used as an always-on tone-sculpting stage or as a switchable boost for solos.

## Controls
- **7 frequency sliders** (100Hz, 200Hz, 400Hz, 800Hz, 1.6kHz, 3.2kHz, 6.4kHz): Each boosts or cuts its band by up to 15dB (slider up = boost, down = cut); the lower three bands shape the fundamental, the upper four shape harmonic/presence content
- **LEVEL**: Overall output volume of the EQ'd signal, used to match unity gain with the bypassed signal or to push it as a clean boost

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single pedal switch: engages/bypasses the EQ (buffered bypass)

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits well early in a drive-heavy chain (before distortion, to shape what hits the gain stage) or late in the chain (after drive/amp, to correct the pedalboard's overall tone or carve a mid-boost for solos).
- A classic use is as a switchable "solo boost": set a mid-frequency bump (around 800Hz–1.6kHz) with LEVEL pushed above unity, engaged only for lead sections — a clean gain/EQ lift rather than added distortion.
- Buffered bypass — safe anywhere in the chain, and its buffer can help long cable runs or long pedal chains retain high end.
- Complements or substitutes for the GP-5's own EQ module when using a real amp/pedal front end instead of the GP-5's AMP sim — same graphic-EQ concept (fixed bands, boost/cut) as most multi-effects EQ blocks.
