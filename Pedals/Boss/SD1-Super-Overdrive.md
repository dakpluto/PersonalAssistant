# Boss SD-1 Super Overdrive

Classic asymmetrical-clipping overdrive pedal, in continuous production since 1981. One of the most widely used "boost into an amp" overdrives — known for a smooth, mid-forward push rather than heavy distortion.

## Controls
- **DRIVE**: Overdrive amount, from a very soft/subtle push at low settings to a hard, sustained overdrive at high settings.
- **TONE**: Tone shaping, from soft/dark lows (counterclockwise) to crisp, sharper highs (clockwise).
- **LEVEL**: Output volume — can be set well above unity to drive an amp's front end harder.

## Notes for patch-building
- Classic use is as a boost/overdrive in front of an already-driven amp channel: Drive low-to-moderate, Level up past unity, letting it push the amp rather than supplying all the gain itself.
- Works well as an always-on "amp tightener"/EQ-shaping boost even with Drive very low, since the asymmetrical clipping and midrange bump color the tone even at minimal gain.
- Single footswitch, buffered bypass — no mode switches or alternate voicings (compare the SD-1W Waza Craft edition, which adds a Standard/Custom toggle).
- Sits naturally early in a drive chain, ahead of higher-gain distortion/amp-sim stages, functioning as a gain-stage/EQ push rather than a stand-alone heavy distortion voice.
