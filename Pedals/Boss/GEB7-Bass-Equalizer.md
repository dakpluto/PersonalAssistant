# Boss GEB-7 Bass Equalizer

Compact 7-band graphic EQ pedal voiced specifically for electric bass (including 5-string range), with bands and Q tuned lower/wider than the guitar-oriented GE-7. A tone-sculpting tool rather than an effect, commonly run always-on to shape a bass's core EQ or switched in for a boost.

## Controls
- **7 frequency sliders** (50Hz, 120Hz, 400Hz, 500Hz, 800Hz, 4.5kHz, 10kHz): Each boosts or cuts its band by up to 15dB (slider up = boost, down = cut); bands and Q are chosen to suit bass fundamentals and harmonics (note the tighter spacing around 400–800Hz versus the GE-7's octave spacing)
- **LEVEL**: Overall output volume of the EQ'd signal, used to match unity gain with the bypassed signal or push it as a clean boost

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single pedal switch: engages/bypasses the EQ (buffered bypass); the CHECK LED also doubles as a battery-check indicator

## Notes for patch-building
- Typically placed early in a bass chain (after compression, before drive/amp sim) to correct or shape the fundamental before it hits gain stages, or late in the chain to notch problem frequencies for the room/mix.
- A common use is a scoop/boost preset switched in for slap or pick passages (cut low-mids around 400–500Hz, boost 50Hz and the top end) versus a flatter setting for fingerstyle/pocket playing.
- Buffered bypass — safe anywhere in the chain.
- For a GP-5-based bass patch, this is the pedal-level equivalent of the GP-5's own EQ module — useful when the bass tone is being built around a real pedal/amp front end rather than the GP-5's internal EQ.
