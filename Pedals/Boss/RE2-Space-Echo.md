# Boss RE-2 Space Echo

Compact pedal recreation of the classic Roland RE-201 Space Echo tape-echo unit, combining tape-style delay with built-in spring reverb. Known for its warm, wavering, saturated repeats rather than the clean digital character of a pedal like the DD-3T.

## Controls
- **ECHO**: Sets the volume of the echo (delay) signal; turn fully counterclockwise to run reverb-only
- **REVERB**: Sets the volume of the built-in spring reverb signal
- **INTENSITY**: Sets the amount of feedback (echo regeneration/repeats); fully clockwise drives the echo into self-oscillation
- **TONE**: Tilts the tonal character of the echo repeats — clockwise emphasizes highs and cuts lows, counterclockwise emphasizes lows and cuts highs
- **REPEAT RATE**: Sets the simulated "tape speed," i.e. the base delay time — clockwise shortens the interval between repeats
- **WOW & FLUTTER**: Adds simulated tape-speed instability/wobble to the repeats; clockwise increases the wavering
- **MODE**: 11-position rotary selecting combinations of the three virtual playback heads (head 2 and head 3 delay times are 2x and 3x head 1's) with/without reverb, reproducing the RE-201's 11 head/reverb combinations

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single pedal switch: engages/bypasses the effect (buffered bypass); holding it also allows tap-tempo entry for the repeat rate
- **CARRYOVER** switch: when ON, echo/reverb trails already ringing out continue to decay naturally after the pedal is bypassed, instead of being cut off abruptly (OFF)

## Notes for patch-building
- True stereo in/out (A/B input and output jacks) — can be run mono (single input/output) or wired for a full stereo delay/reverb spread later in the chain.
- Because it bundles delay AND reverb in one box, it can substitute for both a delay module and a reverb module in a signal chain when only one effect slot is available — but note the two share the same MODE-selected head/reverb combination, they aren't independently routable.
- The tape-emulation character (WOW & FLUTTER, saturated regen via INTENSITY) gives a fundamentally different color than a clean digital delay — pick this over a DD-3T-style delay specifically when warble/warmth/vintage tape character is the goal, not a clean utility repeat.
- CTL/EXP jack accepts an expression pedal or external footswitch for real-time control of a parameter (e.g. sweeping intensity/repeat rate) or tap-tempo — useful for songs needing an expressive echo swell.
- Buffered bypass, so it's safe to place anywhere in a chain without signal loss when off.
