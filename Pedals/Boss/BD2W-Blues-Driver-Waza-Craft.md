# Boss BD-2W Blues Driver (Waza Craft)

Waza Craft reissue of the BD-2 Blues Driver, with a front-panel Standard/Custom mode switch added to the original three-knob circuit.

## Controls
- **GAIN**: Overdrive amount, from a light dry-ish push to a fuller overdrive/distortion.
- **TONE**: Tone shaping — clockwise gives a sharper sound with emphasized treble, counterclockwise gives a softer sound with cut treble.
- **LEVEL**: Output volume.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch: engage/bypass (buffered bypass).
- **Mode switch** (Waza Craft addition, front panel): Standard vs Custom.
  - **Standard**: the classic BD-2 warm, natural overdrive and gain range.
  - **Custom**: extends the gain range and reshapes the frequency response for a fuller tone with beefier low end and more sustain — geared toward heavier blues/rock/stoner-rock use than the original circuit.

## Notes for patch-building
- The circuit tracks guitar volume-knob changes naturally, staying clean and dynamic at low guitar volume and overdriving progressively as it's rolled up — a strong pick if the source material relies on volume-knob cleanup rather than a footswitch for dynamics. This behavior carries over in both modes.
- Standard mode is the closer match to a stock BD-2 for classic blues/rock tones; Custom mode's extra gain and low-end can push it into a standalone medium-gain distortion role rather than just an overdrive.
- Buffered bypass; no separate true-bypass option.
- Place early in the drive chain, ahead of higher-gain distortion/amp-sim stages, same general logic as other Boss OD-family pedals.
