# Boss CE-2W Chorus (Waza Craft)

Waza Craft-series reissue/expansion of the classic Boss CE-2 Chorus, with an analog BBD (bucket-brigade) circuit and an added mode switch that also unlocks the sounds of the legendary CE-1 Chorus Ensemble (chorus and vibrato voicings). Known for lush, warm, all-analog modulation rather than a digital chorus's cleaner/colder character.

## Controls
- **RATE**: Speed of the chorus modulation — clockwise increases rate
- **DEPTH**: Intensity/width of the modulation — clockwise increases depth
- **Mode switch (3-position)**: Selects the chorus circuit/voicing —
  - **Standard**: The original CE-2's chorus sound
  - **CE-1 (Chorus)**: Emulates the CE-1 Chorus Ensemble's chorus voicing
  - **CE-1 (Vibrato)**: Emulates the CE-1's vibrato mode (pitch modulation with no dry signal blended in, rather than a chorus mix)

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single pedal switch: engages/bypasses the effect (buffered bypass); CHECK LED indicates active state

## Notes for patch-building
- Standard modulation placement: after drive/distortion, before delay/reverb (or in an amp's effects loop) — analog chorus circuits like this one are typically run post-gain-stage for clarity.
- The Vibrato mode has no dry-signal blend (pure pitch modulation) — treat it as a distinct effect from the two chorus modes, not just a "more intense" chorus setting; useful for classic pitch-wobble/vibrato textures a modulation module elsewhere in the chain may not offer.
- All-analog BBD circuit — pairs naturally with tube-amp-style warmth; a good pick when a patch wants a vintage/warm chorus character rather than a wide, modern digital-chorus shimmer.
- Mono in/out only (unlike some modern chorus pedals) — no stereo spread from the pedal itself.
