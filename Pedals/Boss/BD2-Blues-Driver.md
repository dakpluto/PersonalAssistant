# Boss BD-2 Blues Driver

Overdrive pedal introduced in 1995, prized for a dynamic, amp-like overdrive character and for interacting naturally with the guitar's own volume knob — rolling back guitar volume cleans the BD-2 up the way it would a real tube amp, rather than just getting quieter.

## Controls
- **GAIN**: Overdrive amount, from a light dry-ish push to a fuller overdrive/distortion.
- **TONE**: Tone shaping — clockwise gives a sharper sound with emphasized treble, counterclockwise gives a softer sound with cut treble.
- **LEVEL**: Output volume.

## Notes for patch-building
- The circuit is specifically designed to track guitar volume-knob changes naturally, staying clean and dynamic at low guitar volume and overdriving progressively as it's rolled up — a strong pick if the source material relies on volume-knob cleanup rather than a footswitch for dynamics.
- Sits comfortably as either a standalone blues/classic-rock overdrive or a boost into an already-driven amp; its character is more "amp-like breakup" than the more compressed, mid-forward SD-1/OD-3 voicings.
- Single footswitch, buffered bypass, no mode switches (compare the BD-2W Waza Craft edition, which adds a Standard/Custom toggle).
- Place early in the drive chain, ahead of higher-gain distortion/amp-sim stages, same general logic as other Boss OD-family pedals.
