# Boss MT-2 Metal Zone

Boss's high-gain distortion pedal aimed at heavy rock/metal, known for its extensive active 3-band EQ with a semi-parametric midrange — far more tone-shaping range than most compact distortion pedals.

## Controls
- **Dist**: Amount/intensity of distortion
- **Level**: Overall output volume
- **High**: Shelving EQ, roughly ±15dB around 10kHz
- **Low**: Shelving EQ, roughly ±15dB around 100Hz
- **Middle**: Semi-parametric midrange level, ±15dB boost/cut
- **Mid Freq**: Sets the center frequency the Middle control acts on, sweepable roughly 200Hz–5kHz

## Notes for patch-building
- Highest-gain/most scooped-capable of the Boss distortion pedals in this library — built for heavy rhythm and lead metal tones, with enough EQ range to go from scooped thrash mids to boosted lead-cutting mids.
- Typical placement: drive section of the chain, generally the primary/only distortion source in metal-oriented patches rather than stacked with another distortion.
- Keeping Dist below roughly 12 o'clock is a common practical tip to avoid excess noise/mud at high gain; a noise gate/suppressor ahead of or after it is common in real-world use given how much gain and EQ boost is available.
- Buffered bypass (not true bypass). Mono in/out.
