# Boss OC-5 Octave

Polyphonic octave/pitch-down pedal, the modern successor to Boss's long-running OC-2/OC-3 octave line. Adds selectable guitar/bass tracking and a Vintage/Poly mode switch on top of the classic mix-of-octaves control set.

## Controls
- **DIRECT LEVEL**: Volume of the unaffected dry signal
- **+1OCT**: Level of the one-octave-up voice
- **-1OCT**: Level of the one-octave-down voice
- **-2OCT/RANGE**: In Vintage mode, sets the level of the two-octaves-down voice; in Poly mode, acts as a Range control that restricts the octave effect to only the lower notes played

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single pedal switch: engages/bypasses the effect.
- **VINTAGE/POLY mode switch**: Vintage mode emulates the classic mono-tracking OC-2-style octave circuit (single-note tracking, that character's slight pitch-glide/artifacts); Poly mode adds polyphonic tracking for chords and multi-note passages.
- **GUITAR/BASS switch** (side-mounted): Optimizes the pitch-tracking engine for guitar-range or bass-range input.
- CHECK indicator LED doubles as a battery-check light.

## Notes for patch-building
- Buffered bypass; includes a dedicated Direct Out jack (dry signal only) for splitting to a second amp/DI ahead of the octave effect.
- Placement: like most octave/pitch pedals, tracks best fed a clean, unprocessed signal — put it early in the chain, before drive/distortion/fuzz and before the GP-5's own DST/AMP stages, not after.
- Vintage mode is the closer analog to Boss's classic OC-2 octave-down tone (thicker, slightly synth-like on single notes); Poly mode is the practical choice whenever chords or polyphonic passages need octave doubling.
- The GUITAR/BASS tracking switch matters — leaving it mismatched to the instrument noticeably hurts tracking accuracy, especially on lower strings.
- Good substitute/complement for a GP-5 patch's low end when a synth-bass or 12-string-style octave-doubling texture is wanted without relying on the GP-5's own onboard octave/pitch algorithms.
