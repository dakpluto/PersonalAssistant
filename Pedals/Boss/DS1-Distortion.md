# Boss DS-1 Distortion

Boss's original compact distortion pedal (since 1978), one of the best-known and most widely used distortion stompboxes ever made — a classic, slightly gritty/mid-forward distortion voicing.

## Controls
- **Tone**: Clockwise cuts lower frequencies for a sharper/brighter sound; counterclockwise cuts higher frequencies for a softer, warmer sound
- **Dist (Distortion)**: Controls the depth of distortion and the length of sustained notes
- **Level**: Overall output volume of the effect

## Notes for patch-building
- General-purpose distortion — sits between overdrive and heavier distortion in intensity; works both as a rhythm crunch pedal at lower Dist settings and a more aggressive lead voicing at higher settings.
- Typical placement: in the drive section of the chain, generally after any overdrive/boost pedal and before modulation/time-based effects — a common "second gain stage" pedal or a standalone distortion source into a cleaner amp setting.
- JFET-buffered bypass, not true bypass — the input/output buffer stays in the path even when off.
- Mono in/out. Long-running, low-cost, widely available — a safe default pick when a patch calls for a generic mid-gain "classic distortion" pedal rather than a specific boutique voicing.
