# Boss OD-3 Overdrive

Overdrive pedal built around a "Dual-Stage Overdrive Circuit" — a warmer, thicker, more amp-like overdrive than the mid-forward SD-1, with more low-end body and a wider gain range.

## Controls
- **DRIVE**: Overdrive amount — clockwise increases the effect, counterclockwise reduces it.
- **TONE**: Tone shaping — counterclockwise is milder/darker, clockwise is sharper/brighter.
- **LEVEL**: Output volume.

## Notes for patch-building
- Dynamically responsive to picking attack and guitar volume-knob changes, more so than a typical clipping overdrive — suits playing styles that lean on volume-knob cleanup.
- Thicker and darker than the SD-1/OD-1 family; a reasonable choice when a patch wants an overdrive with more inherent low-mid body rather than a bright mid-push.
- Single footswitch, buffered bypass, no mode switches or alternate voicings.
- Works either as a moderate-gain standalone overdrive or, with Drive low and Level up, as a boost into a driven amp — same general placement logic as other Boss OD/SD pedals: early in the drive chain, ahead of higher-gain distortion/amp stages.
