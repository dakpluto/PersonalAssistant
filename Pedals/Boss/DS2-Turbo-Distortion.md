# Boss DS-2 Turbo Distortion

Boss's follow-up to the DS-1 (see `DS1-Distortion.md`), adding a "Turbo" boost mode switch and a remote-switching jack for footswitch-controlled mode changes; higher-gain and more flexible than the original DS-1.

## Controls
- **Dist**: Amount of distortion/gain
- **Tone**: Clockwise cuts lower frequencies (brighter); counterclockwise cuts higher frequencies (warmer)
- **Level**: Overall output volume

## Footswitches / Switches
- Main footswitch: effect on/off
- **Turbo mode switch** (Mode I / Mode II): Mode I gives a warmer, more mellow distortion with a flatter frequency response (rhythm-oriented); Mode II adds a mid-range boost for a more biting, cutting distortion (lead-oriented)
- Remote jack accepts an optional external footswitch (e.g. Boss FS-5L) for hands-free Turbo mode switching independent of the main on/off footswitch

## Notes for patch-building
- The Turbo mode switch is the key differentiator from the plain DS-1 — effectively gives two distortion voicings (rhythm-flat vs lead-boosted-mids) in one pedal, though switching it normally requires a second footswitch (via the remote jack) rather than the main footswitch alone.
- Typical placement: drive section of the chain, same role as the DS-1 — rhythm/lead distortion source, usable either as the sole distortion or stacked after a lower-gain overdrive.
- Buffered bypass (not true bypass). Mono in/out.
