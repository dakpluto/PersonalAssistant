# Boss SD-1W Super Overdrive (Waza Craft)

Waza Craft reissue of the classic SD-1 Super Overdrive, with a front-panel Standard/Custom mode switch added to the original three-knob circuit.

## Controls
- **DRIVE**: Overdrive amount, from a very soft/subtle push at low settings to a hard, sustained overdrive at high settings.
- **TONE**: Tone shaping, from soft/dark lows (counterclockwise) to crisp, sharper highs (clockwise).
- **LEVEL**: Output volume — can be set well above unity to drive an amp's front end harder.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch: engage/bypass (buffered bypass).
- **Mode switch** (Waza Craft addition, front panel): Standard vs Custom.
  - **Standard**: reproduces the classic SD-1 sound and gain range.
  - **Custom**: extends the gain range and adds more midrange, giving a fuller, slightly hotter voicing than the original circuit while keeping the same three-knob control set.

## Notes for patch-building
- Classic use is as a boost/overdrive in front of an already-driven amp channel: Drive low-to-moderate, Level up past unity, letting it push the amp rather than supplying all the gain itself. Standard mode is the closer match to a stock SD-1 for this role.
- Custom mode gives more usable gain on its own, so it can stand in as a light-to-medium standalone overdrive voice, not just a clean boost — useful when a patch wants two distinct overdrive characters without needing two pedals.
- Buffered bypass: safe for long cable runs, always slightly in the signal path when off.
- Sits naturally early in a drive chain, ahead of higher-gain distortion/amp-sim stages, functioning primarily as a gain-stage/EQ push.
