# Fender x Hello Kitty Fuzz

Novelty/signature-branded fuzz pedal from Fender's Hello Kitty collaboration line. Despite the theming, it's a genuine op-amp-based fuzz circuit covering a wide range from mild overdrive-like grit up to full fuzz.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level.
- **Tone**: Overall tone shaping.
- **Fuzz**: Drive/fuzz amount — covers a broad range from subtle overdrive-like grit to full-on ripping fuzz.
- **Internal trim pot**: Hidden high-frequency trim inside the enclosure, used to tame top end for bright single-coil pickups or bright amps (not a footswitch/panel control — set-and-forget).

## Notes for patch-building
- Front-end drive stage — same functional slot as any other fuzz/DST pedal ahead of the GP-5's own DST/AMP; the wide Fuzz range means it can cover both a light-overdrive role and a full-fuzz role depending on setting.
- True bypass switching, single footswitch.
- Op-amp fuzz circuit rather than a germanium/silicon transistor design — expect a more consistent, less touch-sensitive/temperature-sensitive fuzz behavior than vintage-style fuzz circuits.
- Powered by 9V battery or 9VDC center-negative adapter (88mA draw). Input impedance 500k ohm, output impedance 5k ohm max.
