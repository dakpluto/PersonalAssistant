# Summer School Electronics Class Reunion

Dual-circuit overdrive/fuzz pedal combining Summer School's take on a 90's Russian Muff (Black side) in parallel with their own Trash Panda circuit (Green side), blendable via a master Blend control. Two distinct drive characters running simultaneously rather than in series.

## Controls
- **Drive (Black/Muff side)**: Gain amount for the 90's Muff circuit
- **Tone (Black/Muff side)**: Frequency shaping for the Muff circuit
- **Diode toggle (Black/Muff side, Si/LED)**: Silicon setting gives "a more standard classic muff sound"; LED setting gives "a darker sound with more low end"
- **Drive (Green/Trash Panda side)**: Gain amount for the Trash Panda circuit
- **Tone (Green/Trash Panda side)**: Frequency shaping for the Trash Panda circuit
- **Diode toggle (Green/Trash Panda side, Si/LED)**: LED setting delivers "the classic amp-like overdrive"; silicon offers "a lower output, but provides a crispier fizzier drive"
- **Vol**: Master output volume
- **Blend**: Balances between the Muff side and Trash Panda side outputs

## Footswitches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the whole pedal (both circuits run together, blended by the Blend knob — there's no way to switch to only one side with the footswitch).
- Two LEDs (one per side) indicate the pedal is engaged.

## Notes for patch-building
- Because both circuits run in parallel and are only reachable via one footswitch, this is a single always-blended voice, not a two-channel switcher like the King of Kings — use the Blend knob to dial in a fixed ratio per patch rather than expecting to toggle between the two sides live.
- Sits in the drive section of the chain, wherever an overdrive/fuzz pedal would normally go — typically feeding a clean or edge-of-breakup amp so the pedal's own dirt is the main flavor.
- The Muff side (Black) leans fuzzier/vintage; the Trash Panda side (Green) leans toward a more amp-like high-gain overdrive — pick the Blend/diode combination based on which character (or mix) the song calls for.
