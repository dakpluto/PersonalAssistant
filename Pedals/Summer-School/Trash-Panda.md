# Summer School Electronics Trash Panda

High-gain soft-clipping overdrive/distortion built from spare available parts, versatile enough to cover country through hard-rock. Also the "Green side" circuit that appears alongside the 90's Muff in the Class Reunion.

## Controls
- **Vol**: Output level
- **Drive**: Gain/distortion amount
- **Tone**: Wide-range tone control — can roll off piercing high end or cut mud, per the manufacturer's description

## Footswitches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the pedal. Single LED indicates on/off state.

## Notes for patch-building
- Soft-clipping (unlike the hard-clipping Gus-Drive), so it sits at the more amp-like, less fizzy end of the high-gain spectrum — a good candidate wherever a patch wants aggressive gain without a harsh/brittle top end.
- The wide-range Tone control does double duty as both a high-end tamer and a mud-cutter — useful as the only tone-shaping stage for this pedal, since there's no separate bass/treble split.
- Placement: standard drive/distortion slot in the chain; gain range and soft-clipping character make it equally suited as the main distortion voice or as a second, higher-gain stage stacked after a lower-gain overdrive.
