# Summer School Electronics Half Day Compressor

Ross-style compressor with a couple of modern conveniences: an input-capacitance toggle for guitar vs. bass, and a Comp knob that adds a squashed, boosted character as it's pushed higher.

## Controls
- **Vol**: Output level
- **Comp**: Compression amount — levels out volume spikes and raises softer notes; pushing it higher yields a more "squashed" compressed tone useful for solos
- **Tone**: General tone-shaping control for dialing in the sound by genre
- **Guitar/Bass toggle**: Changes input capacitance. "Guitar" rolls off lower frequencies so the compressor sits cleanly in a band mix; "Bass" passes more low end, suited to bass, synths, baritone, and drop-tuned guitars

## Footswitches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the compressor. Single LED indicates on/off state.

## Notes for patch-building
- Standard compressor placement: early in the chain, before drive/gain stages, so it evens out pick/finger dynamics feeding into the dirt.
- The Guitar/Bass toggle makes this usable across instrument types on the same board — set it to match the instrument, not the desired tone.
- Especially useful for fingerstyle or string-to-string volume consistency, per the manufacturer's own framing; dialing Comp up toward max is the way to get a boosted, "squashed" lead/solo tone out of it rather than a separate control.
