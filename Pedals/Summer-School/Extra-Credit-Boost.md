# Summer School Electronics Extra Credit Boost

Simple two-mode clean/dirty boost pedal. A toggle switches between a straightforward op-amp boost and a transistor-based boost with an added brightness control.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level, active in both modes
- **Brightness**: Adds top-end sparkle and grit; only active when the toggle is set to the transistor-boost position
- **Mode toggle (Boost / Boost & Brightness)**: Left position ("Boost") is a one-knob op-amp boost; right position ("Boost & Brightness") switches to a transistor-based boost and brings the Brightness knob into the circuit

## Footswitches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the boost. Single LED indicates on/off state.

## Notes for patch-building
- Manufacturer-suggested uses: at the end of the chain for solo boosts, in front of an overdrive for added grit/push, or as an always-on preamp.
- In "Boost" mode it's a transparent clean volume bump — good as a solo-level lift placed after the drive/amp section. In "Boost & Brightness" mode the transistor stage adds its own coloration and high-end bite, so it doubles as a light gain/edge stage when placed earlier, ahead of an overdrive or the amp's front end.
- Standard 9V negative-center power.
