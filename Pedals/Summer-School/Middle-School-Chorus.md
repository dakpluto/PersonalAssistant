# Summer School Electronics Middle School Chorus

Analog bucket-brigade-device chorus, Summer School Electronics' first modulation pedal. Dual-mode design that functions as both a chorus and a vibrato. Made in Syracuse, NY.

## Controls
- **Depth**: Modulation depth, shared between chorus and vibrato modes
- **Rate**: Speed of modulation in chorus mode
- **LFO**: Separate LFO/rate-shaping control used in vibrato mode, independent from the chorus-mode Rate control

## Footswitches / Switches
- Primary footswitch: engages/bypasses the effect
- Second footswitch: switches the pedal between chorus mode and vibrato mode
- Because chorus and vibrato have independent rate controls, the pedal can be left set up with two different modulation speeds and switched between them live

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the MOD slot of the signal chain (post-drive/amp, pre-delay/reverb is the typical convention, though placement is a taste call).
- The two-footswitch layout lets one pedal cover both a subtle chorus (e.g. clean rhythm shimmer) and a more pronounced vibrato/warble (e.g. lead or ambient sections) without needing two separate modulation pedals or a MOD-model swap mid-song.
- Bypass type and mono/stereo are not specified on the manufacturer's product page.
