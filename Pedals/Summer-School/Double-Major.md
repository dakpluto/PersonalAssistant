# Summer School Electronics Double Major (Dual Overdrive)

Dual overdrive pedal built around two identical, independently-usable K-style (Klon-adjacent) transparent overdrive circuits stacked in one enclosure. Handbuilt in Syracuse, NY.

## Controls
- **Volume (Left/Red channel)**: Output level for the left circuit
- **Drive (Left/Red channel)**: Overdrive amount for the left circuit
- **Tone (Left/Red channel)**: Frequency shaping for the left circuit
- **Volume (Right/Black channel)**: Output level for the right circuit
- **Drive (Right/Black channel)**: Overdrive amount for the right circuit
- **Tone (Right/Black channel)**: Frequency shaping for the right circuit
- **Clipping toggle (per channel)**: Selects soft or hard germanium diode clipping for that circuit

## Notes for patch-building
- Both circuits are identical K-style transparent drives, so this is a "one drive voiced two ways" tool rather than two different pedal types — e.g. one side set as a low-gain always-on boost, the other as a hotter lead push, or both stacked in series for a single higher-gain drive stage.
- The two channels can be run independently or stacked (one feeding the other); when stacking, put the lower-gain/cleaner side first in the signal path and the higher-gain side second.
- Because it's a transparent overdrive design, it works well as a boost into an already-voiced amp/AMP-sim rather than as the primary gain/tone source — pairs naturally ahead of a distortion or amp block rather than replacing one.
- Footswitch count and independent per-channel bypass are not confirmed by the manufacturer's product page — verify on the physical unit before assuming both sides can be toggled separately.
