# Summer School Electronics Gus-Plus

Bass-friendly, blendable version of the Summer School Gus-Drive high-gain hard-clipping overdrive. Usable on guitar, bass, or any instrument where retaining low-end clarity through the drive matters.

## Controls
- **Vol**: Output level
- **Blend**: Rolls in clean (dry) signal alongside the driven signal; turned all the way down, the pedal behaves like a standard Gus-Drive (fully driven, no clean blended in)
- **Drive**: Overdrive/gain amount

## Footswitches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the pedal. Single LED indicates on/off state.

## Notes for patch-building
- When blending in clean signal, balance Vol and Blend together to find the right sweet spot — raising Blend without adjusting Vol can leave the overall level unbalanced against bypass.
- The blend knob is what makes this the bass/full-mix-friendly option versus the plain Gus-Drive: use Gus-Plus whenever the driven tone needs to retain low-end fundamental (bass, low-tuned guitar, or any part that can't afford to lose punch to hard clipping).
- High-gain hard-clipping circuit with low-frequency roll-off that increases as gain increases (inherited from Gus-Drive) — the Blend control is specifically there to counteract that roll-off by reintroducing the clean low end.
