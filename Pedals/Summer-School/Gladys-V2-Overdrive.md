# Summer School Electronics Gladys V2 Overdrive

Updated version of Summer School's Gladys low/mid-gain overdrive, with increased headroom, more available gain, a 2-band active EQ, and silent (click-free) switching.

## Controls
- **Vol**: Output level
- **Drive**: Overdrive/gain amount
- **Low**: Active bass EQ
- **High**: Active treble EQ
- **Comp toggle**: Up position runs Gladys as a hard-clipping boost/drive similar to a classic "breaker" circuit — low headroom, light drive. Down position adds more volume and headroom for a crisper, more direct, harsher/modern drive sound

## Footswitches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the pedal, with silent (non-popping) switching. Single LED indicates on/off state.

## Notes for patch-building
- The Comp toggle is effectively a voicing switch, not just a compression amount — treat it as choosing between two distinct overdrive personalities (vintage low-headroom breaker vs. modern crisper/harsher) rather than a subtle tweak.
- The 2-band active EQ (Low/High) gives more post-drive tone-shaping range than a single Tone knob, useful for cutting through a mix or taming harshness at higher Drive settings.
- Low/mid-gain voicing makes it a natural rhythm-drive or amp-push pedal rather than a lead/high-gain tool; pair with the amp's own gain stage for higher-gain needs.
