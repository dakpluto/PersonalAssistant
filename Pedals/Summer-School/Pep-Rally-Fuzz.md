# Summer School Electronics Pep Rally Fuzz

Classic 2-transistor silicon fuzz (a louder, higher-headroom follow-up to Summer School's limited Stone Thrower), built to cover 60's-style fuzz tones, clean up when the guitar volume is rolled back, and go into full saturation when the Fuzz knob is maxed.

## Controls
- **Vol**: Output level
- **Fuzz**: Fuzz/gain amount — from vintage-style breakup up to full "sonic destruction" when maxed
- **Tone**: A tilt-EQ control shaping the fuzz's frequency balance

## Footswitches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the fuzz. Product photos also show a small unlabeled toggle switch next to the footswitch; Summer School's product description mentions "an added boost stage at the end of the circuit" but doesn't document this switch directly, so its exact function (likely a boost engage/disengage) isn't confirmed here — verify in person before relying on it in a patch.

## Notes for patch-building
- Classic 2-transistor fuzz topology: cleans up well with the guitar's volume knob rolled down, so it can cover both a light-gain edge-of-breakup voice and a maxed-out fuzz voice from the same setting if a player rides the guitar volume.
- Placement: early in the chain, ahead of any overdrive/amp gain stage, as with most fuzzes — fuzzes are typically sensitive to what's in front of them (avoid stacking a buffered pedal or wah directly before it if a vintage-correct fuzz interaction matters).
- Silicon transistors (not germanium), so it's more temperature-stable and consistent than a vintage germanium fuzz, at the cost of some of that fuzz's softer/smoother character.
