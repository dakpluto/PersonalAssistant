# Summer School Electronics Gus-Drive

Summer School Electronics' first pedal: a high-gain hard-clipping overdrive built to push an amp into singing, howling saturation. Two knobs, dog-themed naming.

## Controls
- **Bark**: Volume (output level)
- **Howl**: Gain (drive amount) — as gain is increased, low frequencies are progressively rolled off so the guitar stays defined in a band mix rather than turning to mud

## Footswitches
- Single footswitch engages/bypasses the pedal. Single LED indicates on/off state.

## Notes for patch-building
- High-gain and hard-clipping means this reads more like a distortion than a transparent overdrive — treat it as a drive/distortion-stage pedal rather than a low-gain boost.
- The built-in low-frequency roll-off that scales with the Howl (gain) setting is a designed-in feature, not a side effect — useful for keeping a high-gain tone tight without needing a separate tone/EQ control, but it also means there's no way to keep full low end at high gain settings (that's what the blend-equipped Gus-Plus is for).
- Placement: standard drive-pedal position ahead of the amp's front end, or stacked after a lower-gain overdrive for an additional gain boost.
