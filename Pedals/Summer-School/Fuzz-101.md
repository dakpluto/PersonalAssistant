# Summer School Electronics Fuzz 101

Back-to-basics silicon fuzz built around two BC108 transistors with "legendary fuzz mods," plus a Rat-inspired tone stage. Made in Syracuse, NY.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level
- **Fuzz**: Amount of fuzz/gain
- **Bias**: External control that adjusts the voltage to the transistors, shifting the character from sputtery, gated "Velcro" fuzz to big, more sustained distortion-like fuzz
- **Tone**: Rat-inspired tone circuit — enough EQ range to cut through a mix or roll off treble to blend in

## Notes for patch-building
- Classic vintage-style silicon fuzz — place first in the signal chain (before other drives, EQ, or the AMP block) and, per general fuzz convention, ahead of any buffered pedals, since fuzz circuits are sensitive to the impedance a buffer presents before them.
- The Bias control is the character knob here: dial it toward instability for gated/broken-up "dying battery" fuzz textures, or toward normal bias for a more usable, sustaining fuzz/distortion tone.
- Works as either the sole gain source (bypass the AMP's own DST) or stacked before a clean-to-edge-of-breakup amp voicing for added texture.
- Bypass type and footswitch count are not confirmed by the manufacturer's product page.
