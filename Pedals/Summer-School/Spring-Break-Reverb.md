# Summer School Electronics Spring Break Reverb

Spring-style reverb pedal with an added delay circuit that feeds only the reverb tails, not the dry attack. Capable of self-oscillation for drone/ambient soundscapes. Handmade in Syracuse, NY.

## Controls
- **Reverb level (single main knob)**: With the Delay switch off, the pedal runs as a simple one-knob spring reverb; this knob sets the reverb amount (exact name not given on the manufacturer's product page)
- **Mix**: Active only when the Delay switch is on — sets the balance of the delayed reverb-tail repeats
- **Delay**: Active only when the Delay switch is on — sets delay time, up to 600ms
- **Feedback**: Active only when the Delay switch is on — sets number/decay of the delay repeats feeding back into the reverb tail

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Delay switch**: Turns the internal delay circuit on/off. Off = plain one-knob spring reverb. On = activates the Mix/Delay/Feedback controls, delaying only the reverb tails (dry pick attack stays undelayed) for up to 600ms, with self-oscillation possible at high feedback settings

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the RVB slot at the end of the chain; because the delay here only affects reverb tails (not the dry signal), it behaves more like a modulated/rhythmic reverb tail than a true parallel delay — don't treat the Delay/Feedback/Mix knobs as a substitute for a standard DLY module when a clean, undelayed slapback or rhythmic repeat on the dry signal is needed.
- The self-oscillation capability at high Feedback makes this a good pick for ambient/soundscape sections or song outros where a swelling, runaway reverb texture is wanted.
- Bypass type and mono/stereo are not specified on the manufacturer's product page.
