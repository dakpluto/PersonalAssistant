# Summer School Electronics Snow Day Delay

Digital delay built around two independent PT2399 chips, offering two separately-configurable delay settings that can be rotated between or run simultaneously. Summer School Electronics' first time-based effect; hand built in Syracuse, NY; lifetime warranty. Mono operation.

## Controls
- **Mix**: Volume of the repeats
- **Repeat**: How long/how many times the repeats trail off
- **Delay 1**: Delay time for setting 1 (active when the Blue light is on), up to 600ms in standard mode
- **Delay 2**: Delay time for setting 2 (active when the Yellow light is on), up to 600ms in standard mode

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Onboard footswitch**: Rotates between Delay 1 and Delay 2 (or, in Blizzard Mode, toggles both running together)
- **Blizzard Mode toggle**: Down = the two delay settings act individually/independently (only one active at a time, selected by the footswitch); Up = both delay settings run simultaneously
- **Selector button**: Switches between the Blizzard Mode settings

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the DLY slot. The two independently-timed delay settings make this a natural fit for a song needing two distinct delay voices (e.g. a shorter slapback for rhythm sections, a longer trailing repeat for leads) from a single pedal, switched via its own onboard footswitch rather than requiring a CTL-controlled parameter change.
- Blizzard Mode (both delays simultaneously) layers the two delay times together for a denser, more complex repeat pattern — useful for ambient or texture-heavy sections.
- Runs through PT2399-based digital delay chips that can be pushed into analog-style saturation/clipping at higher Repeat/Mix settings rather than staying pristine-clean.
- Bypass type is not specified on the manufacturer's product page.
