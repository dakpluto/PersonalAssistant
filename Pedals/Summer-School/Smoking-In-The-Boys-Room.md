# Summer School Electronics Smoking In The Boys Room

Dual-effect pedal combining a distortion voiced after a classic orange distortion pedal with a bucket-brigade-style chorus, built to capture 90s grunge tones. Designed and built in collaboration with Supercool Pedals; hand built in Syracuse, NY and Peterborough, ON.

## Controls
- **Distortion section**: Voiced after a classic orange distortion pedal, with a modified output buffer and a redesigned tone stack (individual knob names beyond the tone stack are not specified on the manufacturer's product page)
- **Chorus — Depth/Warble knob**: Replaces a standard depth control; dials in the amount of warble and pitch variation, from subtle chorus to heavily modulated
- **Order switch**: Reverses the internal signal order between the two effects — distortion into chorus, or chorus into distortion

## Notes for patch-building
- True bypass.
- The order switch is the key tonal decision: distortion-into-chorus gives a more standard modulated-drive tone, while chorus-into-distortion pushes the modulated signal into the drive stage for a more unstable, swirling grunge texture — pick based on which side of that classic 90s sound the patch needs.
- Functions as two module slots in one footprint (drive + modulation); useful when board space is tight and a patch calls for both a mid-gain distortion and a chorus without dedicating two separate pedals.
- Mono/stereo is not specified by the manufacturer; treat as mono unless confirmed otherwise.
