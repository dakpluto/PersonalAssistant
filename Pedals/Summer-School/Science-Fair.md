# Summer School Electronics Science Fair

Parallel-blend overdrive/distortion pedal combining two distinct clipping circuits — "Lab Rat" (hard-clipping, crispy distortion) and "Test Tube" (soft-clipping, mid-hump overdrive) — run side by side rather than in series. Hand built in Syracuse, NY; lifetime warranty.

## Controls
- **Drive (Lab Rat / black knobs, left side)**: Gain for the hard-clipping distortion circuit
- **Tone (Lab Rat / black knobs, left side)**: Tone shaping for the hard-clipping circuit
- **Drive (Test Tube / green knobs, right side)**: Gain for the soft-clipping, mid-hump overdrive circuit
- **Tone (Test Tube / green knobs, right side)**: Tone shaping for the soft-clipping circuit
- **Blend (center silver column)**: Crossfades between the two parallel circuits — full left = Lab Rat only, full right = Test Tube only, center = both combined
- **Volume (center silver column)**: Master output level

## Notes for patch-building
- Because the two circuits run in parallel (not stacked in series), the Blend knob is the primary voicing control — this pedal can act as either a single crisp hard-clip distortion, a single soft mid-forward overdrive, or a blended hybrid of both, all from one footswitch/slot.
- Good single-pedal substitute for needing two different drive characters (bright/aggressive vs. warm/mid-forward) without two separate DST-slot pedals — set Blend as the "voicing" decision per song rather than automating it, since there's no indication of an expression/CTL input.
- Bypass type and mono/stereo are not specified on the manufacturer's product page.
