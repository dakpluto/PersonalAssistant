# Summer School Electronics School Lunch Multi-Effect

Summer School Electronics' first multi-effect pedal, combining scaled-down versions of four of their standalone circuits — fuzz, distortion, delay, and boost — in one small-footprint enclosure. Runs on a single 9V negative-center supply.

## Controls
- **Fuzz section — Volume, Fuzz, Bias**: 2-transistor (BC108) fuzz circuit, same control set as Fuzz 101
- **Distortion section — Volume, Drive, Tone**: "Trash Panda" circuit, a high-gain all-purpose distortion
- **Delay section — Mix, Delay, Repeat**: Simplified version of the Snow Day Delay; Mix sets repeat volume, Delay sets delay time, Repeat sets how many/how long the repeats trail off
- **Boost section — single knob**: Transistor-based boost (based on the Extra Credit Boost), for volume/gain-stage boost

## Notes for patch-building
- Manufacturer recommends placing this pedal first in the signal chain, or ahead of any pedals with buffers, so the onboard fuzz sees a consistent input impedance.
- Internal order (fuzz → distortion → delay → boost, or otherwise) and the number/layout of footswitches are not specified on the manufacturer's product page — confirm on the physical unit before assuming independent per-effect bypass.
- Functions as a compact stand-in for up to four separate pedal-board slots (fuzz, drive, delay, boost) when board space is limited; each section is a scaled-down/simplified version of the corresponding standalone Summer School pedal, not necessarily identical in range or voicing.
- Mono/stereo is not specified; treat as mono unless confirmed otherwise.
