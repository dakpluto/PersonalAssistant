# Donner Stylish Fuzz

Classic-voiced fuzz pedal. True bypass.

## Knobs
- **Sustain**: The main fuzz/sustain knob — controls fuzz gain amount and how compressed/sustained the note is. This is the big center knob.
- **Treble**: Shapes tone — cuts or boosts highs in the fuzz signal
- **Bass**: Shapes tone - cuts or boosts lows in the fuzz signal
- **Volume**: Output level

## Notes for patch-building
- Only pull this into a build when the song actually calls for a fuzzy, gated/splattery texture — it's not a general-purpose gain stage the way the GP-5's own DST module is.
- Low Bustain gives a looser, more open fuzz; cranked Bustain gets compressed and saturated, closer to a classic Fuzz Face-style wall of fuzz.
- Runs before the King of Kings and the GP-5 in the board order, so anything it does gets shaped further by whatever drive/amp comes after it.
