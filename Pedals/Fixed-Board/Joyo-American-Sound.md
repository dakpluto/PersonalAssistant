# Joyo American Sound (JF-14)

Fender-style "amp-in-a-box" overdrive. Widely treated as a clone of the Tech21 SansAmp Blonde, and marketed by Joyo as a '57 Deluxe simulator, from clean to overdrive. Michael mostly uses it as a low-gain drive pedal. On both the guitar and bass Full Boards (added 2026-10-12). Optional since 2026-10-16: either/or with the Donner White Tape. Michael says which one a build uses.

## Knobs
- **Level** (labeled Volume on some listings): Output volume
- **Drive**: Overdrive amount, from clean amp tone to cranked-Fender breakup
- **Voice**: Tone character of the amp voicing (the Blonde's Character control)
- **Low**: Bass EQ
- **Mid**: Mid EQ
- **High**: Treble EQ

No toggle switches.

## Footswitch
- Engages/bypasses the pedal.

## Power
- 9V DC, center negative. **Don't use a 12V supply: it damages the pedal.**

## Notes for patch-building
- Board position (Michael, 2026-10-12): the last drive on both boards.
  - Guitar: King of Kings → **American Sound** → Narcissus → GP-5.
  - Bass: Tidal Wave → **American Sound** → Narcissus → GP-5.
- Because it comes after the King of Kings and Tidal Wave, it acts as the "amp" stage those drives push into, like a boost into a Fender. Chorus still comes after all the dirt.
- Primary use: low-gain, edge-of-breakup Fender grit. Think a Deluxe or Bassman just starting to break up. Low Drive with Level at or slightly above unity.
- Good fit when the patch's GP-5 amp or snaptone is clean, or only lightly broken up, and the song wants Fender-style hair: country, roots rock, blues, indie, classic rock rhythm.
- Into a clean snaptone it supplies the front-end grit a cranked small Fender would. Into an already driven snaptone it acts more like a tone-shaping boost.
- Avoid maxing Voice and Drive together. Retailer listings warn it gets noisy and can feed back at extreme settings.
- On bass there's no Blend control, so the whole signal goes through it. Keep Drive low and Low at noon or above so the fundamental stays intact. Use it for warm grit (Bassman-style breakup), not heavy distortion.
