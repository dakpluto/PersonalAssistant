# Friedman IR-D Dual Tube Preamp

Dual-channel, dual-tube (real 12AX7, high-voltage — not a simulation) preamp and DI pedal with onboard IR/cab simulation. Designed to function as a stand-alone, cab-less amp replacement for live and studio use as much as a front-end gain stage, with full MIDI programmability.

## Controls (per channel — Channel 1 and Channel 2 are independent)
- **Volume**: Channel output level.
- **Gain**: Channel drive/gain amount.
- **Bass / Middle / Treble**: Standard 3-band EQ for that channel.
- **Boost Volume / Boost Gain**: Separate level and gain controls for that channel's boost stage.
- **Bright switch (2-way)**: Adds top-end brightness to the channel.
- **Structure switch (3-way)**: Alters the channel's gain-stage voicing/character.
- **CAB/IR select switch (3-way)**: Selects between onboard IR/cab simulation options for that channel (also bypassable for use with an external IR loader).

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Channel 1 footswitch**: Short press activates Channel 1; long press bypasses the IR/cab simulation output.
- **Channel 2 footswitch**: Short press activates Channel 2; long press bypasses the effects loop.
- **Boost footswitch**: Short press activates the boost stage (per active channel's Boost Volume/Gain settings).
- MIDI (5-pin DIN and USB) allows full recall of channel, boost, FX loop, per-channel IR/cab, and per-channel Presence/Thump settings across up to 128 presets via Friedman's editor software.

## Notes for patch-building
- Functions as a real tube AMP+CAB stage in one box rather than a simple boost/OD — best modeled as a substitute for the GP-5's own AMP and CAB modules (similar role to a NAM capture, but running real analog/tube hardware with its own onboard IR loading rather than a captured profile).
- The two independent channels with independent EQ/gain/IR make it well suited to a "two amp voicings in one pedal" role — e.g. Channel 1 as a clean/low-gain platform, Channel 2 as a higher-gain lead voicing, footswitchable mid-song.
- Ships with 13 free Friedman IRs and supports loading custom third-party IRs per channel.
- Balanced TRS output and headphone output make it usable as a direct/cab-less recording or silent-practice solution, not just a pedalboard front-end.
- Compact but not a simple stompbox — genuinely a multi-function preamp/DI unit with an editor-software/MIDI layer behind the panel controls.
