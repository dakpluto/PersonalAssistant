# Joyo King of Kings (R-20)

Dual-channel overdrive pedal, clone of the Analogman King of Tone. Two fully independent gain channels (Left/Right) that can be used separately or stacked in series.

## Per-channel controls (Left and Right each have their own set)
- **Volume**: Output level for that channel
- **Gain**: Overdrive/drive amount for that channel
- **Tone**: Frequency shaping for that channel
- **Clipping toggle**: Switches the diode clipping character for that channel (affects how hard/soft the overdrive breaks up)
- **Feedback toggle**: Switches the feedback circuit for that channel (affects gain stage response/compression)

## Footswitches
- Left footswitch: engages/bypasses the left channel
- Right footswitch: engages/bypasses the right channel
- Channels can be run independently, or both engaged together to stack (left feeding right) for a hotter, more saturated lead boost.

## Notes for patch-building
- Because each channel is fully independent, this is the natural "two distinct drive voicings in one pedal" tool for full-board builds — e.g. left = low-gain rhythm push, right = higher-gain lead boost.
- Sits after the fuzz pedal and before the Narcissus/GP-5 in the board order — typically used as a boost/second gain stage into the GP-5's own DST+AMP, not as the only drive source.
- When only one channel is needed for a song, leave the other bypassed rather than setting it to unity/clean — it's true bypass per side, so an unused channel should just stay off.
