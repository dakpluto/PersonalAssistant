# Way Huge Swollen Pickle Jumbo Fuzz MkIIS

Jeorge Tripps' take on the Electro-Harmonix Big Muff, originally released by Way Huge in 1997 and revived (like the rest of the Way Huge line) as a Jim Dunlop-manufactured reissue. Known as a huge, burly, high-gain fuzz with an unusually wide tonal range for a Muff-style circuit thanks to added filter/scoop/compression controls beyond the classic four-knob Muff layout; the current WHE401S "MkIIS" designation is the standard full-size current-production Jumbo Fuzz (a smaller "Smalls" Swollen Pickle variant also exists as a separate compact model).

## Controls
- **Sustain**: Gain/fuzz amount — described by the manufacturer as ranging "from mild crunch to Armageddon."
- **Loudness**: Output volume, with enough headroom to overpower most amps' input stage.
- **Filter**: Sweeps a heavily band-pass-filtered tone — the Swollen Pickle's signature control, adding girth and sizzle rather than a simple treble/bass tilt.
- **Scoop**: Tone-stack control moving between the classic Swollen Pickle mid-scoop voicing and a flatter, less-scooped mid response.
- **Crunch**: Adjusts the compression intensity of the fuzz.

## Internal Trim Controls
- **Voice**: Sets how strongly the external Scoop control cuts mids, from a light to a heavy scoop.
- **Clip**: Switches between two sets of clipping diodes, for a smoother vs. a more "opened-up" fuzz sustain character.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: fuzz stage, early in the drive order — high-gain Muff-style fuzzes generally want the guitar signal directly, ahead of buffers, wah, and other drive pedals.
- The Filter/Scoop/Crunch trio gives this pedal a much wider EQ/voicing range than a stock Big Muff — useful when a patch needs a fuzz that can be tightened up or scooped rather than only the classic dark, wooly Muff voicing.
- True bypass switching, per manufacturer/reviewer sources describing the Swollen Pickle line generally (no buffer in the signal path when off).
- Power: 9V battery or Dunlop ECB003 adapter / DC Brick family supplies; exact current draw not confirmed in research.
- The two internal trims (Voice, Clip) are not footswitch- or knob-accessible — treat them as one-time setup choices to document in a patch write-up rather than something adjusted per-song.
