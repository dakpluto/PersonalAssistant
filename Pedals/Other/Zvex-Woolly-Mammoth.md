# ZVex Woolly Mammoth

Fuzz pedal from Z.Vex Effects (zvex.com) built primarily around bass-friendly low end but equally popular with guitarists, known for an unusual "pulse-width gating" control that produces an eerily smooth, near-silent decay between notes rather than an abrupt cutoff. Zvex currently sells two versions: the original 4-knob Woolly Mammoth (documented below, available in both silk-screened Vexter and hand-painted finishes) and the expanded Woolly Mammoth 7 (WM7), which adds a switchable 3-band Marshall-style tone stack in place of the single EQ knob. This file covers the original/standard Woolly Mammoth's controls.

## Controls
- **Wool**: Amount of fuzz/"fur" on the note — the core gain/fuzz-amount control
- **Pinch**: Adjusts the pulse width of the waveform, producing an unusually smooth gating effect — introduces a distinctive, eerie near-silence between notes as they decay rather than a hard noise-gate chop
- **EQ**: Tilt-style tone control — turning left adds lows and softens highs (moving through a midrange-forward zone), turning right progressively reduces lows and adds high end
- **Output**: Output level of the pedal, designed for smooth, repeatable level matching

## Notes for patch-building
- Distinctive low-end-forward fuzz that stays articulate and "sub"-heavy even at high Wool settings — a strong choice for bass or low-tuned guitar parts that need to retain low-end weight through a fuzz stage, unlike a Big Muff or RAT which tend to compress/thin the low end at high gain.
- The Pinch control is the pedal's signature feature and worth calling out explicitly in a patch write-up — it's not a simple noise gate, it reshapes the waveform's decay character and interacts with Wool/EQ, so document a specific setting rather than "gate: on."
- Typical placement: fuzz stage, first or early in the drive order like most fuzzes (wants to see the instrument's pickups fairly directly).
- Hand-painted units carry a lifetime warranty; Vexter-series units use identical internal parts in a lower-cost silk-screened enclosure — tonally identical, cosmetic difference only.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V center-negative power, single footswitch on/off with LED; Z.Vex circuits are known for very low standby current draw.
