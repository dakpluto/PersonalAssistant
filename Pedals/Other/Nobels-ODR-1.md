# Nobels ODR-1

Long-running "natural" overdrive from German maker Nobels, prized for a smooth, amp-like breakup and a distinctive two-band-in-one tone control (Spectrum) rather than a simple treble/bass or single tone knob. A budget-friendly pedal with an outsized reputation — Nobels' own knowledge base notes it's intentionally buffered rather than true bypass, aimed at players running long cable chains. Current production includes both the original 3-knob ODR-1 and an "ODR-1 30th Anniversary" edition adding a fourth control.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Overdrive/distortion amount, from slight crunch to full-on hard-rock saturation.
- **Spectrum**: Simultaneously adjusts deep-mid and treble content — fully counterclockwise gives a warm, mid-heavy blues tone; turning clockwise makes the sound progressively stronger and brighter, described by the manufacturer as "ideal for really good hard rock." Functions differently from a standard tone knob since it moves two frequency regions together rather than a single sweep.
- **Level**: Output volume.
- **Bass Cut** (30th Anniversary edition only): Cuts low frequencies that can muddy the tone with strong pickups — fully counterclockwise passes the full pickup signal; turning clockwise progressively attenuates the bass.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: drive stage — its "transparent, amp-flattering" reputation makes it a good pick as an always-on or boost-style overdrive ahead of an already-driven amp, similar in role to a Klon-type transparent OD, though voiced with more mid/treble emphasis via Spectrum rather than being tonally neutral.
- **Buffered bypass, not true bypass** — confirmed directly via Nobels' own documentation, which explains the choice as preserving high end over long cable runs.
- Jacks (standard ODR-1): IN, OUT, DC power, and a REMOTE jack accepting a momentary footswitch (or a compatible Nobels MS-8/MS-4/MF-2 switcher) for remote on/off control.
- Power: 9–18V DC, 2.1mm center-negative barrel, max 11mA current draw, ~1MΩ input impedance, ~2.2kΩ output impedance, +6dB max input level — confirmed from the official Nobels ODR-1 30th Anniversary owner's manual.
- The newer ODR-1X model (a different, more feature-laden pedal in the same family) adds a selectable true-bypass/buffered-bypass mini switch and a Remote TRS jack for gain-boost + on/off control — don't conflate its spec sheet with the standard ODR-1 covered here.
