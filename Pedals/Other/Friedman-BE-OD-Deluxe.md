# Friedman BE-OD Deluxe

Dual-channel overdrive/distortion pedal from Friedman Amplification modeled after the preamp of Friedman's BE-100 amp head, nicknamed "Brown Eye" (BE) — the origin of that nickname isn't formally documented by Friedman, who treats it as an inside joke. Dave Friedman built his reputation modding and building amp rigs for high-profile touring guitarists before launching Friedman Amplification, and the BE-100 became known for high-gain, Marshall-derived tones. The BE-OD Deluxe expands on the original single-channel BE-OD by adding a second, slightly-lower-gain channel plus a Mid control on each side, letting a player carry two distinct BE-100-style voicings (e.g. rhythm and lead) in one footprint. Reviewers describe its range as running from gritty, Plexi-style raunch at low settings to full modern high-gain, harmonically dense distortion at high settings — a compact stand-in for a modded Marshall-family amp's dirt channel(s).

## Controls
Each of the two channels has an identical set of six knobs:
- **Volume**: Channel output level.
- **Gain**: Overdrive/distortion amount for that channel; Channel 1 replicates the original BE-OD's gain range, Channel 2 is voiced with somewhat lower gain on tap, making a natural rhythm (Channel 2) / lead (Channel 1) pairing.
- **Bass**: Low-end response.
- **Middle (Mid)**: Midrange content — new versus the original single-channel BE-OD; used to keep the guitar present in a band mix rather than getting scooped out.
- **Treble**: High-end response, works together with Presence to shape overall brightness/cut.
- **Presence**: Upper-frequency shimmer/cut, interacts closely with Treble — reviewers note it can be a little fiddly to dial in precisely.

## Switches
- **Tight switch (3-position, one per channel)**: Replaces the single-pedal original's Tight knob. The two non-neutral positions progressively tighten low-end response, removing flub/looseness — particularly useful for drop tunings and higher-gain settings.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: gain stage, mid-chain — designed to emulate a modded Marshall-style amp's preamp/dirt channel(s), so treat it like a DST/PRE-stage pedal driving a comparatively clean amp rather than a subtle boost.
- True bypass switching; two footswitches — one for effect bypass, one for Channel 1/Channel 2 select — so a patch note should specify both which channel is active and whether it's engaged, not just "on."
- Powered by 9-18VDC (no battery option); needs 2 isolated 9V outputs from a multi-output power supply (or the included splitter cable) rather than a single standard 9V supply, since it runs at up to 18V internally.
- 1/4" (6.3mm) input/output jacks; current production, sold new directly from Friedman as of this writing.
- If a patch calls for two distinct gain/voicing settings within one song, this pedal's dual-channel footswitch naturally maps to that need — but remember the GP-5's own CTL footswitch can only toggle module on/off, not switch between two save states, so treat "Channel 1 vs Channel 2" as equivalent to needing two separate module slots if modeling this pedal's behavior inside the GP-5 rather than using the pedal itself.
