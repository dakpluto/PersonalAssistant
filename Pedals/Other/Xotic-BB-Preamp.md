# Xotic BB Preamp

Compact "amp-in-a-box" preamp/overdrive from Xotic Effects built around a Tube Screamer-derived gain stage (tone control internally hard-wired near 9:00) plus an added variable-gain stage and a 2-band active Baxandall EQ. Reputation is for exceptionally touch-sensitive, transparent drive that ranges from a clean boost through creamy overdrive to a thick, AC/DC-style crunch, all while staying dynamically responsive to pick attack and volume-knob changes.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Drive amount. Minimal distortion at minimum, creamy overdrive past noon, thick/saturated distortion when cranked — usable as a nearly-clean boost or a standalone lead-gain source depending on setting.
- **Volume**: Output level; capable of +30dB of clean boost on top of the gain stage, so it can push a clean amp channel hard or just balance level against other pedals.
- **Treble**: Active EQ, cuts/boosts high end by roughly ±15dB.
- **Bass**: Active EQ, cuts/boosts low end by roughly ±15dB. Together with Treble this is described as a 2-band active (Baxandall-style) EQ that reshapes tone without getting muddy or thin at the extremes.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: drive/preamp stage, early-to-mid chain ahead of modulation/delay — usable either as an always-on clean/tone-sweetening boost (low Gain) or as the primary overdrive source feeding a clean amp (Gain past noon).
- True bypass.
- With all four knobs at noon it produces a musical, mid-forward boost rather than obvious distortion — useful as a baseline before pushing Gain further for a patch that wants overdrive.
- Because Treble/Bass are active (not passive, cut-only) controls, they can add gain/edge as well as remove it — treat them as tone-shaping in both directions rather than assuming boost-only or cut-only behavior.
- The exact EQ corner frequencies (Hz center points for the Bass/Treble bands) were not confirmed from manufacturer sources during research — treat as unverified if a patch write-up needs to cite exact frequencies.
