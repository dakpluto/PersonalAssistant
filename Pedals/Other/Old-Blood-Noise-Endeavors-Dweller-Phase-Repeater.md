# Old Blood Noise Endeavors Dweller Phase Repeater

A modulation/delay hybrid from Old Blood Noise Endeavors (Tulsa, OK) built around a bank of allpass filters, with true delay lines running between each phasing stage — something OBNE branded a "phase repeater" rather than a conventional phaser. Depending on settings it can sit anywhere from a classic phase/vibe sound to a resonant random-step filter to warm, repeating delay-like textures, and is generally regarded as one of the more genuinely original modulation pedals of the 2010s boutique wave. As of this writing there is no separate "V2" model — the current production unit is simply the Dweller Phase Repeater.

## Controls
- **Rate**: Sets the speed of the LFO driving the phase stages.
- **Depth**: Sets the LFO's modulation depth (intensity of the sweep).
- **Regen**: Feeds phased signal back into the effect, increasing intensity/resonance of the phasing.
- **Stretch**: Sets the delay time inserted between each phase-repeating stage — this is what separates the Dweller from a standard phaser, letting the stages spread out in time rather than sweeping instantaneously.
- **Mix**: Blends dry and wet signal, 100% dry to 100% wet.

## Switches
- **Voice toggle**: Selects 4-stage or 8-stage phase repeating (more stages = denser, more phaser-like comb filtering).
- **Shape toggle**: Selects the LFO waveform — sine, triangle, or random sample-and-hold (step) — giving six total Voice x Shape combinations ("six modes").

## Jacks
- **Expression jack**: External control over the Stretch parameter (some sources describe this generally as control over "Time or Morph"; Stretch is the parameter documented on the current manufacturer product page).

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: modulation stage, in the MOD/DLY area of the chain — but because Stretch pushes it toward delay-like territory, treat it as a swing slot that can cover a phaser, vibe, or a short warbly delay/reverb-adjacent texture depending on setting, rather than assuming it always behaves like a plain phaser.
- Bypass: true bypass via a soft-touch footswitch, designed for minimal switch noise.
- Power: 9V DC center-negative, 2.1mm jack, 110mA (power supply not included).
- The five controls interact — Regen and Stretch especially reshape what Rate/Depth/Shape sound like — so a patch write-up should record the exact 5-knob + 2-toggle setting rather than a qualitative description.
- An internal trim-pot sets output level (boost, slight drop, or unity gain per manufacturer materials); this is a set-and-forget internal adjustment, not a per-patch control, and its default/current setting on any given unit is unconfirmed by this research.
