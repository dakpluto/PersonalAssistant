# Greer Amps Lightspeed Organic Overdrive

Low-gain, transparent-leaning overdrive from Greer Amps, built to replicate the warm, natural breakup of a tube amp being pushed rather than impose its own strong drive voicing. Highly touch-sensitive — reviewers note it responds closely to pick attack and guitar volume changes, functioning equally well as a clean boost or a mild-to-medium overdrive depending on how it's driven. Recognizable for its unpainted, raw-metal enclosure with an industrial, circa-1930s look.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Gain/saturation amount, from a nearly clean, transparent boost at low settings to mild-to-medium overdrive saturation at higher settings — never reaches high-gain distortion territory.
- **Loudness**: Overall output volume.
- **Freq**: A single-knob EQ sweep rather than a fixed tone control — centered around noon it gives a slight boost in mid-presence; turning clockwise emphasizes high mids, turning counterclockwise brings out warmer bass presence. The wide sweep is designed to flatter a variety of pickup types (single-coil or humbucker).

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: drive stage, well-suited as an always-on/boost-style pedal ahead of an amp's own breakup or another gain stage, given its low-to-medium gain ceiling and amp-like touch response — similar functional role to other "transparent overdrive" pedals in this library (Klon-style, Timmy, etc.) though voiced with its own Freq-knob character rather than a traditional tone stack.
- True bypass.
- Power: 9–18V DC, center-negative only (9V battery or standard 2.1mm supply); current draw 11mA @ 9V.
- The circuit board is fully epoxy-potted (a manufacturer anti-reverse-engineering measure), so there's no practical way to inspect or modify the internal circuit — relevant only if a patch write-up ever needs to note pedal modifiability, which it won't for a stock unit.
- Enclosure dimensions 4.77" x 2.6" x 1.39"; built to order with roughly 1–2 week lead times and a 5-year warranty per the manufacturer.
