# MXR M307 Layers

Stereo "harmonic sustain" pedal that builds up to three overlapping, independently-faded layers of sustained tone under your playing — closer to a real-time sustain/pad-builder than a conventional reverb, delay, or looper.

## Controls
- **Attack**: Fade-in time for each new layer.
- **Decay**: Fade-out time for each layer once released (higher frequencies within a layer decay faster, mimicking a real string's natural decay).
- **Mix**: Level of the wet (layered/sustained) signal blended with the dry signal.
- **Trig**: Sensitivity of the auto-trigger function, which creates a new layer automatically from pick-attack transients. Fully counterclockwise turns auto-trigger off, leaving the footswitch as the only way to create layers.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Footswitch**: Press to engage a new sustain layer. Hold to sustain that layer indefinitely until released, at which point it fades out per the Decay setting. Double-tap clears all active layers and puts the pedal in bypass.
- **Single button**: Limits the effect to one sustain layer at a time instead of the default three overlapping layers.
- **Sub Oct button**: Transposes newly created layers down an octave; previously created layers are unaffected, so layers of mixed octaves can be stacked.
- **CTR jack** (TRS): Two modes — TAP/EXP mode accepts an external footswitch, keyboard sustain pedal, or expression pedal for triggering/blending layers; AUDIO mode instead outputs a dry signal for running the pedal in a dry/wet split rig.

## Notes for patch-building
- Not a direct substitute for the GP-5's DLY or RVB modules — it's closer to an infinite-sustain/pad generator that sits in the same general "ambient/time-based tail" role, useful for swells, drones, and pad-under-solo textures rather than discrete echoes or room ambience.
- Best placed after drive/distortion and before (or blended in parallel with) the GP-5's DLY/RVB, similar to where an ambient reverb or freeze pedal would sit — layering a sustained pad, then letting the GP-5's own delay/reverb wash over the combined signal.
- The footswitch's hold-for-sustain / double-tap-to-clear behavior is a real-time performance tool, not a simple on/off — worth flagging in patch notes if it's meant to be a momentary swell effect rather than a toggled-on texture.
- True stereo pedal with a dry/wet-split-capable CTR jack; needs a 9V/300mA center-negative supply and explicitly cannot run on battery power.
