# MXR RR104 Randy Rhoads Distortion+

Randy Rhoads signature edition of the classic MXR Distortion+ overdrive/distortion circuit — EQD-simple two-knob layout, voiced/matched to recreate the raw, overdriven tone Rhoads got from his own Distortion+.

## Controls
- **Output**: Overall output volume of the effect; clockwise for more.
- **Distortion**: Amount of distortion/clipping; clockwise for more.

## Notes for patch-building
- Classic op-amp clipping distortion — a single, fairly aggressive gain stage rather than a subtle overdrive; functions as a direct substitute/complement for the GP-5's DST module, or as a boost/fuzz-adjacent push into an amp's front end for that vintage hard-rock crunch.
- Because it's only two controls (no separate tone knob), tonal shaping has to come from the amp/EQ stage after it — pair with the GP-5's EQ module or an amp model with real tone-stack range rather than expecting the pedal itself to shape brightness.
- True hardwire bypass, mono in/out, standard 9V DC (2.1mm center-negative via Dunlop ECB003 or MXR Brick supply, or 9V battery), 11mA draw. Straightforward stompbox with no additional switches or modes.
