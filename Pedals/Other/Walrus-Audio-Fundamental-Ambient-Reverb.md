# Walrus Audio Fundamental Series Ambient Reverb

Compact ambient/atmospheric reverb pedal from Walrus Audio's budget-friendly Fundamental Series. Three-knob, three-algorithm design aimed at dark, spacious, and lo-fi/textured reverb tones rather than short-decay "room" or "spring" voicings.

## Controls
- **Decay**: Reverb decay/tail length. Fully counterclockwise is the shortest decay, fully clockwise is the longest/most sustained.
- **Tone**: Frequency shaping of the reverb signal — acts as a lowpass filter in Deep and Lush modes (darkens the tail), and as a resonant bandpass tone control in Haze mode.
- **Mix**: Blend of dry signal and reverb. Fully counterclockwise is 100% dry (clean signal only), fully clockwise is 100% wet.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch: bypasses/engages the effect. Trails mode is on by default (reverb tail fades out naturally after bypass rather than cutting off) — trails can be disabled via a power-up footswitch-hold procedure.
- 3-position slide switch selects the reverb algorithm:
  - **Deep**: Dark, ambient reverb with an added low octave — cavernous, murky sustain.
  - **Lush**: Breathy, expansive reverb with extended decay, suited to pads and swelling textures.
  - **Haze**: Lo-fi, textured reverb combining distortion and sample-rate reduction with the resonant bandpass Tone control — vintage/degraded character.

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits at the very end of the chain as an ambient/time-based tail, the same functional slot as the GP-5's own RVB module — use this when a patch wants a reverb character (dark/octave-down, lush/pad-like, or lo-fi/distorted) the GP-5's built-in reverb algorithms don't cover.
- Buffered bypass with "Analog Dry-Through," not true bypass — dry signal path stays analog even when the digital reverb engine is bypassed.
- Mono in/out only — no stereo operation, unlike many higher-end ambient reverbs.
- The Haze mode's built-in distortion/bitcrush means this pedal can double as a texture/lo-fi effect, not just a spatial one — worth knowing if a patch wants "broken speaker" ambience rather than clean reverb.
