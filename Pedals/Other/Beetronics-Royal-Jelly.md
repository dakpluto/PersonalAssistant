# Beetronics Royal Jelly

Dual overdrive/fuzz blender from Beetronics, built around two footswitch-selectable circuit blends ("Queen" and "King") that each run overdrive and fuzz in parallel rather than as separate stacked stages. Distinctive bee-themed graphics and honeycomb-inspired internals match the rest of Beetronics' catalog; reviewers describe its range as everything from natural amp-like breakup to thick, Big-Muff-style wall-of-fuzz.

## Controls
- **Honey**: Central gain control for the overdrive/fuzz blend circuit — sweeps from clean boost through medium gain into fuzz territory.
- **Lo**: Bass EQ, up to ±10dB cut/boost.
- **Hi**: Treble EQ, up to ±10dB cut/boost.
- **Volume**: Overall output level.
- **Dry**: Blends in the clean, unaffected signal alongside the OD/fuzz blend, from fully dirty to fully dry.

## Switches
- **Queen / King (left footswitch)**: Selects between two distinct overdrive/fuzz blend voicings sharing the same knob set — effectively two different circuit characters accessible without repatching.
- **Buzz (center footswitch)**: Adds a high-end boost/snarl to the fuzz circuit, layered on top of whichever Queen/King voicing is active.
- **Bypass (right footswitch)**: Standard effect on/off; true bypass.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: drive stage, early in the chain like other overdrive/fuzz pedals — the Dry control makes it usable further down a wet/dry signal chain too since it can retain unprocessed signal even at high Honey settings.
- With three footswitches doing different jobs (channel select, Buzz toggle, bypass), a patch write-up needs to spell out the exact footswitch states, not just knob values, to be reproducible — this is not a simple single-toggle pedal.
- True bypass; power is 9VDC center-negative only, no battery option.
- Jack configuration (mono/stereo, any auxiliary jacks) was not confirmed by research — treat as standard mono 1/4" in/out unless a source is found confirming otherwise.
- Note the potential naming confusion in some third-party write-ups: some describe "Honey/Taste/Volume" as per-channel knob sets, but the pedal's own manufacturer/review sources consistently describe a single shared Honey/Lo/Hi/Volume/Dry knob set with Queen/King as a footswitch-selected voicing choice, not independent per-channel controls — this entry follows the latter, better-sourced description.
