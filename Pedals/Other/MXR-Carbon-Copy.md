# MXR Carbon Copy (M169)

100% analog bucket-brigade delay, one of the most widely used analog delays on modern pedalboards for its warm, dark repeats that degrade musically as they trail off. This entry documents the current standard-production M169 Carbon Copy (three knobs, internal trim-pot modulation) — MXR also currently sells a separate Carbon Copy Deluxe (M292, stereo, tap tempo, onboard modulation controls, up to 1200ms) and a Carbon Copy Bright (brighter-voiced analog delay), which are different current-production models from this one.

## Controls
- **Regen**: Sets the number of repeats/feedback — clockwise for more repeats.
- **Mix**: Blends wet (delayed) and dry signal. Fully counterclockwise = 100% dry, fully clockwise = 100% wet.
- **Delay**: Sets delay time, roughly 20ms to 600ms.

## Switches
- **Mod**: Push-switch that toggles the onboard pitch/warble modulation on the repeats on or off (blue LED indicates on). Modulation width and rate are adjustable, but only via two internal trim pots (screwdriver-adjustable inside the enclosure) — not external knobs.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: time-based stage, late in the chain after drive/distortion and modulation (chorus/phaser), same conceptual slot as the GP-5's DLY module.
- True hardwire bypass, mono in/out, standard 9V DC (2.1mm center-negative via Dunlop ECB003 or MXR Brick supply, or 9V battery), 26mA draw.
- Analog BBD repeats degrade (darken/lose fidelity) with each successive echo — this is the pedal's signature character, distinct from a clean digital delay; useful to note in a patch write-up if the intent is specifically this warm/lo-fi decay rather than pristine repeats.
- Because Mod width/rate are internal trim pots, a patch built around this pedal should treat the modulation depth/speed as a fixed, pre-set characteristic (documented once, physically set on the actual unit) rather than something adjustable per-patch from the pedal's front panel.
