# Xotic EP Booster

Discrete-FET clean boost pedal from Xotic Effects, modeled after the preamp stage of the classic Echoplex EP-3 tape delay unit. Prized as a transparent, always-on tone sweetener rather than a distortion device — up to +20dB of clean headroom-friendly boost with a reputation for adding "sparkle" and perceived low-end/high-end lift without changing the fundamental character of the guitar signal ahead of it.

## Controls
- **Boost (Gain)**: Single external knob, sets output boost level up to +20dB. Sources describe the dial as spanning 0-10, with players commonly landing in the 4-8 range depending on how much level push is wanted; at lower settings it behaves as a subtle "always-on" tone enhancer rather than a volume jump.

## Switches
- **Internal DIP switches (2, accessed by removing the bottom plate)**: Adjust EQ voicing to match a given amp/guitar. Sources are not fully consistent on the exact switch labeling across production runs — some describe them as **Bass Boost** and **Brightness** toggles, while Xotic's own manual describes 3 selectable tonal variations (**Default**, **Unity Gain**, **Vintage**, with Default adding +3dB gain and Vintage voiced after the original Echoplex preamp tone); some newer units reportedly label the pair as Unity/Boost and Treble. Confirm the exact switch legend against the physical unit or its date-specific manual before writing a DIP configuration into a patch — don't guess which labeling a given unit uses.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: clean boost stage, usually first in the chain (guitar into pedal) or placed ahead of a drive/amp stage as a level/EQ push into the front end — not typically used as a gain-stacking overdrive itself.
- True bypass.
- Because the only external control is the single Boost knob, most of this pedal's voicing comes from the internal DIP switches, which are effectively fixed per-board rather than per-patch (players don't usually re-open the case per song) — document the DIP state once for a given physical pedal rather than per patch.
- Powerable at 9V-18V DC; running at 18V is commonly cited as adding extra headroom/dynamic range compared to 9V.
- The exact DIP switch legend was not consistently confirmed across sources (see Switches above) — treat the specific switch function as unverified until checked against the physical unit.
