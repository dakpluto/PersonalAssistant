# Wampler Pinnacle Deluxe V2

Wampler's flagship classic-rock overdrive/distortion pedal, built around what Wampler calls "the Brown Sound" — an homage to the gained-up Marshall tones popularized by Eddie Van Halen. The Deluxe V2 is the current full-featured version of the Pinnacle line, expanding the standard Pinnacle's simpler control set with a 3-band active EQ and an independent, footswitchable mid-heavy boost circuit.

## Controls

- **Volume**: Total output level.
- **Gain**: Total amount of gain applied to the signal path; clockwise increases gain/distortion.
- **Bass / Mid / Treble**: Independent 3-band EQ — each band can be added or cut independently of the others.
- **Boost**: Sets the output volume of the pedal's independent mid-heavy overdrive circuit (see Boost footswitch below); usable simultaneously with the main drive circuit to add gain and tighten the sound, or on its own as a solo boost.

## Switches

- **Gain/Boost toggle**: Downward position runs a lower-gain setting; upward position restructures the internal gain stages for a higher-gain, more aggressive voicing.
- **Vintage/Modern toggle**: Changes high-frequency character — Modern emphasizes more high-frequency content, Vintage has less.

## Footswitches

- **Main bypass footswitch**: Standard true-bypass on/off for the primary overdrive/distortion circuit.
- **Boost footswitch**: Independently engages the mid-heavy Boost circuit (voiced like a classic Tube Screamer-style overdrive) on top of — or instead of — the main circuit; its level is set by the Boost knob.

## Notes for patch-building

- **Bypass**: Relay true bypass on the main circuit.
- Typical placement: DST slot position, in the classic rock/gained-amp-in-a-box role — pairs naturally ahead of a clean-to-crunchy AMP model that needs pushing into higher gain, or can stand in as the sole gain source ahead of a clean amp model.
- The two footswitches (main drive, independent Boost) give this pedal a rhythm/lead split similar to a channel-switching amp — useful for patches that want the CTL footswitch to add gain/volume for a lead section without changing the base drive tone; document which footswitch state(s) a patch's CTL mapping expects.
- The Gain/Boost toggle and Vintage/Modern toggle are internal voicing choices (not footswitchable) and should be treated as fixed per-patch settings, not something a CTL press can toggle mid-song.
- Power: rated for 9V or 18V DC (sounds best at 9V per Wampler's own manual); do not exceed 18V DC or use center-pin-positive adapters. Manual states ~20mA draw at 9VDC.
