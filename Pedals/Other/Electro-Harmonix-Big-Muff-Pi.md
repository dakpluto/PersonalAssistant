# Electro-Harmonix Big Muff Pi

Legendary fuzz/sustainer pedal, one of the most widely used and cloned fuzz circuits ever made. EHX has produced dozens of historic Big Muff Pi variants over the decades with different circuit topologies and reputations (Ram's Head, Green Russian, Op-Amp, Civil War, Triangle, etc.) — this file documents the current standard-production "Big Muff Pi" as sold on ehx.com today (based on the NYC-era circuit), the baseline 3-knob unit that anchors the whole product line.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level of the pedal
- **Tone**: Frequency contour — sweeps from dark/bassy to bright/trebly (a tilt-EQ, not a simple high-cut)
- **Sustain**: Amount of fuzz/distortion and sustain — higher settings compress harder and thicken the note's decay

## Notes for patch-building
- Thick, wooly, heavily-saturated fuzz with a strong midrange scoop at higher Sustain settings — reads as much bigger/denser than a Tube Screamer-style overdrive, closer to a wall-of-fuzz than an amp-push tool.
- Typical placement: fuzz stage, usually first in the drive order (fuzzes generally want to see the guitar's pickups directly, ahead of buffered pedals, though the Big Muff's own input impedance is more forgiving of pedal-chain placement than a Fuzz Face-style germanium circuit).
- Cleans up somewhat with the guitar's volume knob rolled back, but not as dramatically as a Fuzz Face — it stays fairly saturated across most of its Sustain range.
- True bypass (mechanical 3PDT footswitch) on the standard full-size unit; EHX also sells a Nano Big Muff Pi with the identical circuit/controls in a compact enclosure.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V (battery or center-negative adapter), single footswitch on/off with LED.
- Pairs well with a lower/medium-gain GP-5 AMP model or a clean amp platform — the Big Muff is doing essentially all of the gain/voicing work itself, so an already-hot AMP model tends to just mush the two together.
