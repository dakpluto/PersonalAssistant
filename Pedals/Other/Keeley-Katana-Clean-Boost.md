# Keeley Katana Clean Boost

Single-knob dual-FET Class A clean boost from Keeley Electronics, one of Robert Keeley's original pedal designs (originating from work on the Time Machine Boost before becoming its own product). Runs an internal voltage-doubling circuit — 9V in, doubled to roughly 18V internally — for extra headroom, and is known for staying transparent to the guitar's core tone while adding clean volume, with a second push/pull gain stage for pushing into light overdrive.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Sets output boost level. Manufacturer manual describes it as controlling "the output volume of the Katana Clean Boost." Pushed in, it behaves as a clean volume boost; various sources describe the boost range in this mode from a few dB up through +20dB depending on setting.

## Switches
- **Push/Pull Volume (Boost mode)**: Pulling the Volume knob out engages an additional gain stage, adding up to roughly +30dB with a mildly overdriven/tube-like grit rather than staying perfectly clean — pushed back in returns to the standard clean-boost mode. This is the pedal's only mode-select control; there is no separate boost footswitch or toggle.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: clean boost stage, most often first in the chain into the amp/drive stage, or as a solo-level boost placed after other drive pedals to push volume for a lead break.
- True bypass; mono in/out, single footswitch (on/off), internal 9V-to-~18V voltage doubling for extra headroom.
- Because Volume also functions as the pushed/pulled mode selector, a patch's write-up should record both the knob position (0-10/20 scale, sources vary) and whether the knob is pushed (clean) or pulled (boosted/overdriven) — the two states aren't the same circuit.
- Exact numeric boost ceiling for the pushed-in clean mode varies across source descriptions (some cite up to +5dB minimum ceiling on the clean side, others describe it going to "20" on the dial) — treat exact dB figures as approximate pending direct confirmation from the physical unit's manual insert.
