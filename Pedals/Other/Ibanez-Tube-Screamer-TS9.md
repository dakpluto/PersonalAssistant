# Ibanez Tube Screamer TS9

Mid-boosting overdrive pedal, the most widely used and iconic member of Ibanez's Tube Screamer line. Ibanez currently keeps two "vintage" Tube Screamers in constant production — the TS9 and the TS808 (plus a hand-wired TS808HW) — that share the same basic 3-knob layout and JRC4558-based circuit; the TS9 is the more common/available of the two and is used here as the representative model, but a TS808 or a Tube Screamer Mini would use the same controls described below.

## Controls
- **Overdrive (Drive)**: Amount of gain/distortion added to the signal
- **Tone**: High-frequency contour — clockwise adds bite/brightness, counterclockwise mellows the top end
- **Level**: Output volume of the pedal

## Notes for patch-building
- Classic "transparent-ish but mid-forward" overdrive — scoops very little, actually pushes upper-mids, which is what makes it a reliable amp-pushing boost/drive in front of tube amps.
- Typical placement: first drive pedal in the chain, right after any tuner/buffer, ahead of any fuzz or higher-gain distortion — works as a clean boost (Drive low, Level up) into an already-driven amp, or as the sole overdrive at higher Drive settings.
- Low-gain to medium-gain tool; pairs naturally with a GP-5 AMP model set for a lower-gain edge-of-breakup tone, since the TS9 is doing the pushing rather than generating its own heavy distortion.
- JFET-buffered bypass, not true bypass — signal passes through the pedal's buffer stage even when off, which is generally tone-neutral/desirable on longer pedal chains.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V (battery or center-negative adapter), single footswitch on/off with LED.
