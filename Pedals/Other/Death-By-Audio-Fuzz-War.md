# Death By Audio Fuzz War

Brooklyn-built high-gain fuzz from Death By Audio, the boutique brand founded by Oliver Ackermann (A Place to Bury Strangers) in 2002 and still hand-assembled in-house (originally in the Williamsburg DBA warehouse/venue, now Queens). Circuit lineage traces to the Supa Tone Bender/Colorsound-style Big Muff variants — a louder, nastier take on the "ram's head" Muff — and it's become an underground-scene staple (Ty Segall used it heavily on *Slaughterhouse* and *Twins*) prized for going well past normal fuzz territory into all-out gated, blown-out noise.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level of the pedal.
- **Fuzz**: Sweeps the gain from a clean-ish booster (fully counterclockwise, where the circuit barely fuzzes and Tone instead acts as a frequency/filter shifter) up through overdrive, thick distortion, and finally full saturated "war" fuzz at max.
- **Tone**: A wide-range, multi-curve filter reshaping timbre from deep, bass-heavy sludge to screaming top end. With Fuzz rolled all the way down, Tone effectively becomes a frequency-shifter/parked-filter control rather than a conventional tone knob.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: fuzz stage, early in the drive order like most fuzz circuits — wants the guitar's pickups directly, ahead of buffers, wah, and other gain stages.
- True bypass with a standard hard-click footswitch and status LED.
- Only three knobs but heavily interactive at the extremes (Fuzz near zero changes what Tone does entirely) — document the Fuzz/Tone pairing precisely rather than describing the sound qualitatively.
- Very low current draw (1 mA), runs on a 9V battery or 9-18VDC center-negative supply; mono 1/4" in/out.
- Distinct from the Death By Audio/EarthQuaker Devices collab pedal Time Shadows II (already documented separately in this library) — the Fuzz War is DBA's standalone flagship fuzz, not a joint release.
