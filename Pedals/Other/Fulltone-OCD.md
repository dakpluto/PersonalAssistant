# Fulltone OCD (Obsessive Compulsive Drive)

Mike Fuller's high-headroom, amp-like overdrive/light-distortion pedal, a long-running boutique staple known for cleaning up nicely with guitar volume-knob attenuation and for pushing into a surprisingly amp-like distortion at higher Drive settings rather than staying strictly "transparent." **Manufacturing status**: Fulltone's original California operation shut down around 2020 amid well-documented controversy. The brand relaunched in January 2024 under a licensing deal with Jackson Audio, which now manufactures, markets, and distributes Fulltone USA pedals (from a Texas facility) while Mike Fuller, now based in Nashville, focuses on circuit design rather than day-to-day production. Under this arrangement Fulltone currently sells a Standard OCD v2.0 (~$199) and a Custom Shop OCD v1.4 (~$249, described by Fulltone as a hand-wired, part-for-part recreation of the original circuit) — but as of this research the Custom Shop v1.4 is explicitly marketed as a final, limited (500-unit) production batch with no restock planned, so verify current availability before assuming either OCD variant is in ongoing production.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level of the pedal.
- **Drive**: Overdrive/gain amount — ramps from dirty-clean overtones up through saturated distortion.
- **Tone**: Active treble cut/boost; roughly neutral around 12–1 o'clock, counterclockwise smooths out high-gain lead tones, clockwise adds cut and a less-compressed feel.

## Switches
- **HP/LP (High Peak / Low Peak)**: Internal-circuit voicing toggle mounted on the pedal face between the Volume and Drive knobs. HP gives a higher distortion ceiling, louder output, and a slight mid bump that leans toward a British-amp character; LP is the more transparent setting, passing the guitar's natural tone with the least added coloration.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: drive stage, early-to-mid chain — works as a transparent-ish boost/edge-of-breakup drive at lower Drive settings (LP mode) or as a thicker, more colored distortion voice at higher Drive/HP settings, so the HP/LP choice is a meaningful character decision to document per patch, not just a minor tweak.
- Class-A 2N5457 JFET input stage with an enhanced output buffer; True Bypass or an "Enhanced Bypass" buffered mode, selectable via an internal switch — note in a patch write-up which bypass mode a given unit is set to, since it affects tone when off in a longer chain.
- Input impedance 1MΩ, output impedance 10kΩ; power via 9V battery or 9–18V DC (center-negative, 2.1x5.5mm barrel), 8mA draw — running it at 18V raises headroom, a worthwhile note for a patch aiming for the amp-like distortion end of its range.
- Manufacturing/availability is in flux post-relaunch (see status note above) — treat "currently in production" as accurate only as of this research date and re-verify before specifying this pedal for a build that assumes long-term purchasability.
