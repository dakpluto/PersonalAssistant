# Foxx Tone Machine

Cult 1970s octave fuzz designed by Steve Ridinger (later Danelectro's president) in 1971, originally sold for $49 in Foxx's signature velvet-covered enclosure. Built around NOS Fairchild 2N3565 germanium transistors and 1N34A germanium diodes, it's known for a thick, vocal, harmonically rich fuzz with a foot-inaccessible octave-up toggle. **The original Foxx Tone Machine is discontinued — not currently manufactured by Foxx.** It's included here as a tonal-character reference; for an actual current-production pick, the closest widely-available equivalent is the Warm Audio Foxy Tone Box (see below), a faithful reissue built with the same-spec NOS components.

## Controls (original / Warm Audio Foxy Tone Box reissue)
- **Volume**: Output level.
- **Sustain**: Fuzz sustain/gain, described as ranging from choppy bit-crushing textures to liquid, singing sustain.
- **Fuzz/Tone**: Tone shaping from mellow to bright, letting the circuit be dialed in differently for single-coil vs. humbucker pickups.

## Switches
- **Octave**: Engages an octave-up layer for solo/lead use. On the original 1970s unit and the Warm Audio reissue this is a panel-mounted toggle, not footswitchable — a real ergonomic limitation by modern standards. (Some modern reinterpretations, like the Danelectro 3699 Fuzz, make the octave function footswitchable — see below.)

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: fuzz stage, early in the drive order like most fuzzes.
- The original is a discontinued/vintage unit; treat any "Foxx Tone Machine" tonal reference as reference-only character, and use a current reissue for an actual patch/gear recommendation.
- Current alternatives, in order of how closely they replicate the original circuit: **Warm Audio Foxy Tone Box** (full-size vintage-accurate replica, NOS Fairchild 2N3565 transistors + NOS 1N34A germanium diodes, true bypass, velvet-covered enclosure); **Stomp Under Foot Silver Foxx** (handmade US-built clone with NOS diodes); **Danelectro 3699 Fuzz** (Steve Ridinger's own modern remake, adds a footswitchable octave and a mids-boost toggle not on the original).
- Bypass type for the original/Warm Audio reissue: true bypass (confirmed for the Warm Audio Foxy Tone Box).
- Jack configuration and power specs were not confirmed by research for either the vintage original or the reissue — don't guess at these if a patch needs them; note as unconfirmed.
