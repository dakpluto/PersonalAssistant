# MXR Micro Amp (M133)

Single-knob clean boost, in the MXR lineup since the 1970s and still one of the simplest, most reliable ways to add pure level (up to +26dB) without changing tone. A pedalboard utility staple for solo boosts, gain-staging into an amp's front end, or evening out volume between guitars with mismatched pickup output.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Overall amount of signal boost, spec'd from +0.5dB to +26dB. Clockwise for more; near-max settings can drive an amp's front end or a following dirt pedal into extra saturation, not just added volume.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: flexible — as a clean solo/level boost it usually sits either at the very front of the chain (to also push a following drive/distortion stage harder) or at the very end before the amp (pure output-level boost with no interaction with other effects); which position depends on whether the patch wants "louder" or "louder and dirtier."
- Hardwire bypass, mono in/out, standard 9V DC (2.1mm center-negative via Dunlop ECB003 or MXR Brick supply, or 9V battery), 2.5mA draw.
- Flat frequency response (20Hz–20kHz, +0/-1.4dB) — this is a level boost, not a tone-shaping EQ boost, so it won't brighten or darken the signal on its own.
- No mix or blend control — it's a single-stage boost, so its entire musical effect is "louder" (and, pushed hard into another gain stage, "louder and more saturated").
