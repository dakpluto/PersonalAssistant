# Wampler Plexi-Drive Deluxe V2

Wampler's "Marshall-in-a-box" overdrive, built to replicate the sound, feel, and responsiveness of a vintage JTM45-style Plexi amp in pedal form. The Deluxe V2 expands the original 3-knob Plexi-Drive (Volume/Gain/Tone plus a single Bass Boost toggle) into a more versatile pedal with a full active 3-band EQ and an independent, footswitchable mid-heavy pre-gain boost — while keeping the JFET-based circuitry the standard Plexi-Drive is known for.

## Controls

- **Volume**: Total output level; interacts with Gain and the EQ, so it's generally dialed in last.
- **Gain**: Total amount of gain applied to the signal path; clockwise increases gain/crunch. At noon and above gives a classic Plexi crunch tone; at lower settings behaves more like a general-purpose overdrive with the Plexi voicing coloring rather than defining the tone.
- **Bass / Mid / Treble**: Active 3-band EQ — each band can be added or cut independently.
- **Boost**: Sets the output volume of the pedal's independent mid-heavy pre-gain overdrive circuit (footswitchable — see below); voiced like a classic Tube Screamer-style overdrive with pronounced mids, intended to push leads forward.

## Switches

- **Bright Boost toggle**: Changes the high-frequency character of the sound — upward position adds more high-frequency content, downward has less.
- **Bass Boost toggle**: Boosts sub-bass frequencies below 100 Hz — top position engages the boost, bottom position is neutral. Strongly interactive with the Treble/EQ settings and worth dialing in together.

## Footswitches

- **Main bypass footswitch**: True-bypass on/off for the primary overdrive circuit.
- **Boost footswitch**: Independently (soft-click) engages the pre-gain Boost circuit on top of — or instead of — the main circuit; note per Premier Guitar's review, the Boost's pre-gain knob needs to be turned up toward 3 o'clock to reach unity volume when used on its own, as it has limited range in isolation.

## Notes for patch-building

- **Bypass**: Relay true bypass on the main circuit (the original single-knob Plexi-Drive uses a simpler mechanical true-bypass footswitch; the Deluxe V2 uses relay switching).
- Typical placement: DST slot position, British-voiced classic-rock/crunch role — pairs well ahead of a clean-to-edge-of-breakup AMP model that needs pushing into a Marshall-style crunch, or stands alone as the primary gain source ahead of a clean amp model.
- Like the Pinnacle Deluxe V2, the two footswitches (main drive, independent Boost) suit a rhythm/lead CTL mapping — document which footswitch state(s) a patch's CTL assignment expects.
- The Bass Boost and Bright Boost toggles are internal, non-footswitchable voicing choices — treat as fixed per-patch settings rather than something a CTL press can change mid-song.
- Power: rated for 9V or 18V DC (sounds best at 9V per Wampler's own manual); do not exceed 18V DC or use center-pin-positive adapters. Manual states ~25mA draw at 9VDC.
