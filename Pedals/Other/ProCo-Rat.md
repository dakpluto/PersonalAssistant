# ProCo RAT

Classic op-amp clipping distortion, one of the most cloned circuits in pedal history. ProCo has sold many RAT variants over the years (original RAT, RAT2, Turbo Rat, Fat Rat, You Dirty Rat mini, RAT Metal, LOL Fuzz, etc.); the RAT2 is ProCo's current standard-production compact unit and is used here as the representative model — the core 3-knob layout and clipping circuit described below carry over to the rest of the family.

## Controls
- **Distortion**: Gain of the op-amp clipping stage — low settings give a mild, grainy overdrive, high settings give a thick, harmonically dense distortion/fuzz
- **Filter**: Post-distortion low-pass filter, but wired backwards from what players expect — turning it clockwise rolls off high frequencies (darker), turning it counterclockwise lets the guitar's natural brightness through (brighter)
- **Volume**: Output level of the pedal

## Notes for patch-building
- Distinctive "raw," slightly harsh op-amp distortion character — thicker and more aggressive than a Tube Screamer-style overdrive, with a more scooped/cutting midrange than a Big Muff.
- Typical placement: drive/distortion stage of the chain; works as either a gritty rhythm crunch (Distortion low-to-mid, Filter open) or a saturated lead tone (Distortion high).
- True bypass, mono in/out, standard 9V center-negative power (battery or adapter), single footswitch on/off with LED; glow-in-the-dark control markings.
- 100% analog signal path — no digital modeling involved, just the classic LM308-based (RAT2 uses an op-amp variant of the original LM308 circuit) clipping stage.
- Because the Filter knob's rotation is inverted from a normal tone control, dial it by ear rather than assuming clockwise = brighter.
