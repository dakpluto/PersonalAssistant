# Suhr Riot

Suhr's flagship distortion pedal, originally released in 2009 and designed to behave like the overdrive channel of a modded high-gain amplifier rather than a typical stompbox distortion. Suhr released an updated "Riot Reloaded" version with roughly 30% more gain and a re-voiced tone stack; as of current production, Suhr has discontinued Riot Reloaded (now sold only as a "Legacy Edition" archive listing) and reverted the standard Riot to the original 2009 circuit — per Suhr's own product page, the current Riot is "Identical circuit and tone of the Original Riot Circuit (Riot Reloaded circuit is discontinued)." Confirm which circuit a specific used/secondhand unit has before assuming spec parity, since both versions have shipped under variations of the same product line.

## Controls
- **DIST**: Gain control, spanning classic crunch at low settings up to high-gain saturation at max.
- **LEVEL**: Output volume, designed to be able to push the front end of a tube amp into further breakup when run hot.
- **TONE**: Overall voicing, from smooth/warm to cutting, upper-mid-forward lead tones.

## Switches
- **Voicing switch (3-position)**: Left = natural/organic tone that stays dynamically responsive to the guitar's volume knob; Middle = high-gain voicing with solid midrange and tight low end; Right = vintage-flavored high-gain response with strong note definition.

## Jacks
- **FX Link**: 1/8" mono jack for external switching/remote control, letting the pedal's bypass be triggered from a loop switcher or other external controller.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: gain stage, mid-chain in the drive order — voiced to sit in front of an amp's own preamp much like an overdrive/distortion pedal pushing a tube front end, so pair it before AMP-stage modules rather than as a subtle boost.
- True bypass via relay switching (reduces switch-popping versus a mechanical relay-less true-bypass pedal); includes a battery-voltage monitor that automatically forces the pedal to true bypass if a 9V battery's voltage drops too low for reliable operation.
- Power: 9VDC center-negative (2.1mm x 5.5mm), operable from 4V-18V, max 20VDC; current draw approximately 8mA; roughly 75-100 hours of battery life.
- The voicing switch and TONE knob interact significantly — the switch sets the overall gain-structure/EQ character while TONE fine-tunes within that voicing, so document both together rather than TONE alone.
