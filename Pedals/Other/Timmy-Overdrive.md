# Paul Cochrane Timmy Overdrive

Hand-built, boutique low/medium-gain overdrive from Paul Cochrane (paulcochrane.com), designed as a smaller, single-footswitch counterpart to Cochrane's original "Tim" pedal (which adds an effects loop and separate boost section). Renowned for genuinely transparent, amp-like drive and an unusually interactive EQ section; still hand-assembled in small batches rather than mass-produced. Current version is V3.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Amount of clipping/overdrive — ranges from a subtle clean boost to a rich medium overdrive; the Timmy never gets into high-gain distortion territory
- **Bass**: Pre-clipping low-frequency **cut** control — fully counterclockwise lets all the bass through, turning clockwise progressively cuts bass before the signal hits the clipping stage
- **Treble**: Post-clipping high-frequency **cut** control — same "cut" behavior as Bass (fully counterclockwise = full treble, clockwise cuts treble), applied after clipping
- **Volume**: Output level of the pedal

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single main footswitch: effect on/off
- External toggle switch: on V3, this now selects an **extra gain/boost mode** rather than clipping type (a change from earlier V1/V2 units, where this external 3-way toggle selected clipping voicing directly)
- Internal dip switch (inside the enclosure): selects **symmetrical vs. asymmetrical clipping** — symmetrical gives a smoother, more even-order overdrive; asymmetrical gives a slightly more compressed, harmonically richer clipping character. On earlier V1/V2 units this same choice (plus a second symmetrical variant) was exposed on the external toggle instead of an internal switch.

## Notes for patch-building
- The defining trait is transparency: it's meant to sound like "your amp, but more of it," not like a pedal imposing its own voicing — closer in spirit to a Klon than to a Tube Screamer, but with genuinely reversible/independent bass and treble shaping rather than a single tone knob.
- Because Bass/Treble are cut controls, dial both fully counterclockwise as a flat/neutral starting point, then cut only what's needed — don't reach for them expecting a normal "more treble clockwise" boost pot.
- Typical placement: first or second drive stage, low-to-medium gain — pairs naturally with an edge-of-breakup GP-5 AMP model the same way a Klon or TS9 would, letting the Timmy add gain/dynamics while the amp model supplies the fundamental voicing.
- Internal clipping dip switch is a "set and forget" choice for a given patch (not something to plan a CTL toggle around) — pick symmetrical or asymmetrical based on the desired texture before finalizing the patch.
- Mono in/out, standard 9V; being hand-built in small runs, exact enclosure/switch placement can vary slightly between units — verify against the physical pedal when possible.
