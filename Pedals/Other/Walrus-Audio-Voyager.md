# Walrus Audio Voyager Preamp/Overdrive (MKII)

Klon-style transparent preamp/overdrive from Walrus Audio, in the Centaur-derived tradition of adding gain and perceived volume/clarity while staying close to the guitar's natural tone. The MKII revision (current version) significantly expands on that formula with a 5-way diode-clipping selector (spanning the classic germanium Klon voicing through more open silicon clipping options) and a footswitchable parametric mid-EQ stage — features well beyond a standard one-trick Klon clone.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Drive amount — lower settings brighten the signal and add clarity/sheen with minimal breakup; higher settings push into thick overdrive/saturation.
- **Volume**: Output level.
- **Tone**: Treble content introduced into the signal.
- **Mid**: Boosts or cuts the midrange by up to ±12dB, centered on whatever frequency the Freq knob is set to.
- **Freq**: Sets the center frequency the Mid control acts on, sweeping roughly 250Hz-2kHz — effectively a parametric mid EQ combined with the Mid knob rather than a fixed mid control.

## Switches
- **5-way diode/clipping rotary switch**: Selects the clipping-diode configuration used in the drive circuit:
  1. Classic 1N34A Germanium Diodes — most compressed, the original Voyager/Klon-style voicing.
  2. Classic 1N34A Germanium Diodes with bass boost.
  3. Symmetric Silicon Diodes — more open and dynamic than germanium.
  4. Asymmetric Silicon Diodes — least compressed, open and driven.
  5. Asymmetric Silicon Diodes with bass boost.
- **Mid-EQ footswitch**: A second footswitch toggles the parametric Mid/Freq EQ stage in and out independently of the main bypass footswitch, giving two selectable voicings (e.g. a flat/base tone vs. a mid-boosted lead voicing) underfoot on a single pedal.

## Notes for patch-building
- Typical placement: overdrive/preamp stage, standard Klon-style position — either as an always-on tone/volume booster feeding a clean amp, or as the primary drive source, placed early-to-mid chain ahead of modulation/delay.
- True bypass; top-mounted jacks; 9VDC center-negative, 100mA minimum.
- The second (Mid-EQ) footswitch is directly relevant to the GP-5 workflow's CTL toggle concept — since it's a genuine on/off footswitch for one specific EQ stage, it maps cleanly to a CTL-style "on/off only" toggle if a patch wants two voicings of this pedal within the fixed 3-module CTL constraint (unlike knob-based state changes, which CTL can't do).
- Because Mid and Freq interact (Mid's boost/cut amount is centered wherever Freq is set), document both values together rather than just "mid boosted" — the frequency matters as much as the amount.
- The 5-way diode switch is a static, hands-on rotary selection (not footswitchable) — treat it as a fixed per-patch setting alongside Gain/Volume/Tone, not something toggled mid-song.
