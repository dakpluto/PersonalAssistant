# Benson Amps Germanium Preamp

Low-to-medium-gain preamp/boost/overdrive pedal. A variant of Benson's flagship preamp circuit with the first FET gain stage swapped for a germanium transistor, giving a warmer, softer-breaking-up character than the standard FET version. Simple four-knob layout.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Overdrive/distortion amount, from a clean boost up to a mild-to-moderate overdrive.
- **Treble**: Low-pass filter — cuts high end as you turn it down.
- **Bass**: High-pass filter — cuts low end as you turn it down.
- **Volume**: Output level.

## Notes for patch-building
- Works as a clean boost or low/medium-gain overdrive stage ahead of an amp or a higher-gain drive pedal — a natural front-end gain stage rather than a lead/high-gain voice on its own.
- The germanium stage gives touch-sensitive, dynamic breakup that complements or can substitute for a GP-5 PRE module or a light boost/overdrive pedal placed ahead of DST/AMP in a full-board chain, especially for low/medium-gain amp voicings.
- Quirks: true bypass. Mono, standard 1/4" in/out. Powered by 9VDC (Boss-style, center-negative) or a 9V battery, drawing 50mA. Germanium transistors are heat-sensitive — the manufacturer notes performance can degrade around 120°F (e.g. a very hot stage under lighting), so avoid leaving it in direct heat.
