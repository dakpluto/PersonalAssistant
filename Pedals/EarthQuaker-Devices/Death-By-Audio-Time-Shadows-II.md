# EarthQuaker Devices x Death By Audio Time Shadows II

Official product name is "Time Shadows® Subharmonic Multi-Delay Resonator" — a collaboration pedal jointly branded EarthQuaker Devices x Death By Audio (billed on Death By Audio's own site as "Time Shadows II," the second-generation version of their original 2019 collab). A genre-bending multi-delay/filter unit that behaves as three very different pedals depending on its mode switch — not a conventional delay.

## Controls
- **Time**: Controls the delay time.
- **Span**: Controls the intensity/density of repeats.
- **Filter**: Behavior depends on Mode (see below) — controls an envelope + input gate to the delay in EQD mode, filter frequency and delay phasing in DBA mode, and acts as a wet/dry mix control in its middle position.
- **Preset rotary switch**: Selects among 6 preset slots for recalling saved settings (knob positions, Mode, and expression assignment are all part of a saved preset).
- **Save/Recall switch**: Toggles between Live mode (knobs directly control the sound, LED one color) and Preset mode (recalls a stored preset, LED the other color); holding it saves the current settings to the selected preset slot.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Mode switch (3-way)**: Selects the core algorithm/personality of the pedal:
  - **EQD**: Envelope-controlled sub-octave filter delay.
  - **!¡**: Dual delay that shifts repeats up and down in pitch.
  - **DBA**: Multi-delay with a regenerating filter.
- **Footswitch**: Flexi-Switch® — supports both latching (tap once to engage, tap again to bypass) and momentary (hold to engage only while held) operation from the same switch. True bypass via relay switching (requires power to pass signal even when bypassed).
- **Expression/CV jack (1/4" TRS)**: User-assignable to control Time, Span, or Filter in real time; also accepts 0–3.3V control voltage. Ships pre-mapped to Span.

## Notes for patch-building
- Not a simple delay — it's closer to a small multi-effects unit combining delay, filtering, and (mode-dependent) pitch/octave shifting in one box, so it can substitute for both the GP-5's DLY module and add textures (sub-octave, pitch-shifted repeats, resonant filtering) the GP-5 doesn't have a dedicated block for.
- Best suited as an experimental/ambient centerpiece rather than a "set it and forget it" utility delay — the three Mode settings behave differently enough that changing Mode is closer to changing pedals than changing a delay algorithm.
- True bypass (relay-based) and momentary/latching switching make it flexible for both always-on ambient use and stutter/hold effects triggered live via the footswitch or expression pedal.
