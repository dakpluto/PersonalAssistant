# EarthQuaker Devices Tone Job (EQ & Boost)

A clean 3-band EQ and boost pedal — no gain/drive circuit, just transparent tone shaping and up to 5x clean volume boost.

## Controls
- **Level**: Post-EQ output volume. Can boost the signal up to 5x the input; unity gain is around 10 o'clock.
- **Treble**: Boost/cut above roughly 2kHz. Center detent = flat; above the detent boosts, below cuts.
- **Bass**: Boost/cut below roughly 500Hz. Center detent = flat; above the detent boosts, below cuts.
- **Middle**: Resonant filter centered around 1kHz, up to 20dB cut/boost. Center detent = flat; above the detent boosts, below cuts.

## Notes for patch-building
- Purely a tone/volume tool, not a drive — sits well anywhere a transparent EQ correction or a clean solo/volume boost is needed, either before a drive/amp stage to shape what hits it, or after everything as a final output-level and tone trim.
- The Middle control's resonant (not just shelving) behavior makes it useful for scooping or pushing a specific midrange character that a GP-5 AMP model's stock tone stack can't isolate as precisely.
- Good candidate as an always-on EQ correction pedal to compensate for a guitar's pickups or an amp's voicing, independent of any drive/boost duties handled elsewhere on the board.
- True bypass, relay-based switching (Flexi-Switch-style momentary/latching not mentioned for this model — single standard footswitch).
