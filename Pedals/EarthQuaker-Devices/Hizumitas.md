# EarthQuaker Devices Hizumitas (Fuzz Sustainar)

Fuzz/distortion pedal built in collaboration with Wata of Boris, recreating her vintage Elk BM Sustainar (a Japanese-market Triangle Big Muff variant). Delivers heavily saturated, gritty low-end-forward fuzz that stays surprisingly clear and dynamic across pickup types and playing intensity.

## Controls
- **Volume**: Output level. Unity is around 9-10 o'clock — everything past that is boosted, designed to be pushed for maximum effect.
- **Sustain**: Distortion intensity and sustain amount; clockwise increases both. Wide range — small moves make a big tonal difference.
- **Tone**: Unconventional voicing — clockwise boosts bass/low-end thunder, counterclockwise boosts highs, ranging into piercing treble/aggressive mid grit at the extreme.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch, relay-based true bypass (requires power to pass signal — no passive bypass).

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the drive/fuzz position of the chain, ahead of the amp — a Big-Muff-family fuzz, so expect a thick, saturated, low-mid-heavy wall rather than a tight/articulate overdrive.
- Substitutes for or stacks ahead of the GP-5's own DST module; because it's a fixed 3-knob circuit (no clipping/voicing toggle), a patch using this should treat Tone as the primary character choice (bass-heavy doom/sludge vs. cutting treble-forward lead) rather than expecting a mid-position "flat" setting.
- Note: this is based on the Elk BM Sustainar/Big Muff lineage, not the Univox Super-Fuzz circuit — worth knowing if comparing it against other EQD fuzzes that do descend from the Super-Fuzz (e.g. Hoof/Tone Job family lineages differ).
- No separate footswitch-selectable voicing — if a song needs two distinct fuzz characters, that requires two module slots (this pedal plus GP-5 DST, or CTL-toggling the whole pedal on/off) rather than switching within the Hizumitas itself.
