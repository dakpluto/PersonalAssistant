# EarthQuaker Devices Gary

Dual-circuit pedal developed with Lee Kiernan (IDLES) combining a natural-sounding op-amp overdrive (based on the green channel of EQD's Gray Channel) with an independent, envelope-controlled pulse-width-modulated square-wave fuzz. The two sides are fully independent gain stages with their own footswitches, not a single shared circuit.

## Controls
Overdrive side (left):
- **Go**: Drive amount, from a clean boost up to smooth, full-bodied distortion.
- **That's It**: Output level for the overdrive side.

Fuzz side (right):
- **Yes!**: Sets the envelope sensitivity that controls the duty cycle of the square-wave fuzz — governs how the fuzz's pulse-width modulation reacts to playing dynamics.
- **Oosh**: Output level for the fuzz side.

## Footswitches
- **Left footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the overdrive side.
- **Right footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the fuzz side.
- The two sides can be used independently or together (stacked), each with its own Flexi-Switch relay-based true bypass supporting latching (tap) and momentary (hold) operation.

## Notes for patch-building
- Because the two sides are fully independent circuits with separate footswitches, this is a natural "two distinct drive voicings in one pedal" tool — e.g. overdrive side as a low/medium-gain push, fuzz side as a separate, more aggressive/dynamic voice — without needing two separate pedals.
- The fuzz side's envelope-controlled pulse-width modulation makes it touch-dynamic and synth-adjacent (think dying-battery/gated PWM textures that respond to pick attack) rather than a static fuzz — a good pick when a patch wants a fuzz that reacts to playing dynamics.
- Sits early in the chain as a front-end gain stage (guitar in, ahead of amp/AMP-module stages), overlapping functionally with the GP-5's DST module; the two sides can be stacked (overdrive into fuzz, or vice versa depending on physical routing) for a hotter combined drive.
- Mono in/out, relay-based true bypass per side, standard 9V DC power.
