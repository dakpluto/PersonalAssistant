# EarthQuaker Devices Data Corrupter

Monophonic analog harmonizing pitch fuzz built around a CMOS Phase Locked Loop (PLL) synthesizer circuit. Takes the input signal, converts it into a crushing square-wave fuzz tone, then generates multiple harmonized voices above and below the original pitch for guitar-synth-style textures.

## Controls
- **Master Oscillator Root switch** (3-position): Sets the master voice's relationship to the input octave — Unison, -1 octave, or -2 octaves.
- **Master Oscillator Octave/Interval selector** (8-position rotary): Chooses the synthesized interval/frequency for the master voice.
- **Voice Mixer — Oscillator**: Blend level of the Master Oscillator voice into the mix.
- **Frequency Modulator mode switch**: Glide (smooth portamento between notes) or Vibrato (pitch modulation for sci-fi/wobble effects).
- **Subharmonic Root switch** (3-position): Sets the subharmonic voice's octave relationship — Unison, Oscillator-linked, or a separate program setting.
- **Subharmonic Octave/Interval selector** (8-position rotary): Chooses the subharmonic interval, 1-3 octaves below the original.
- **Voice Mixer — Subharmonic**: Blend level of the lower-octave subharmonic voice.
- **Voice Mixer — Square Fuzz**: Blend level of the raw square-wave fuzz at the original (unshifted) octave.

## Footswitches
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the whole effect; Flexi-Switch relay-based true bypass supports both latching (tap) and momentary (hold) operation.

## Notes for patch-building
- Monophonic — best on single-note lines and simple melodic runs rather than full chords, which will produce dense, chaotic harmonization rather than clean harmony.
- Functions as both a fuzz and a pitch/harmony effect at once, so it typically replaces the DST slot outright rather than stacking with another fuzz — the Square Fuzz mixer control alone can serve as a straightforward fuzz if the pitch voices are dialed out.
- A deep sound-design/texture tool more than a conventional gain stage — best reached for on leads, solos, or ambient/experimental patches wanting synth-like harmonized fuzz rather than a standard drive tone.
- Mono in/out, relay-based true bypass, standard 9V DC power.
