# EarthQuaker Devices Disaster Transport Legacy Reissue (Delay Modulation Machine)

Analog-voiced digital delay (PT2399-based) with built-in modulation, reissued in a redesigned enclosure. Covers everything from a fairly straight, warm delay to wild pitch-stretching modulated repeats, with delay and modulation independently footswitchable.

## Controls
- **Time**: Delay time, roughly 30ms at minimum up to 600ms at maximum.
- **Repeats**: Delay feedback/regeneration, from no repeats at zero to self-oscillation at maximum.
- **Mix**: Blend of delayed (wet) signal into the dry signal.
- **Speed**: Modulation (LFO) rate; range is set by the Mod Speed toggle.
- **Intensity**: How strongly the LFO affects delay time — from subtle pitch wobble to extreme pitch-stretching effects.

## Switches
- **Mod Speed** (3-position toggle): Sets the LFO rate range — Slow, Medium, or Fast.
- **Mod Mode** (2-position toggle): Chooses the modulation character — "Bend" (traditional, chorus-like pitch modulation) or "Stretch" (wilder, more extreme pitch-stretching modulation).

## Footswitches
- **Activate**: Engages/bypasses the delay effect overall.
- **Modulate**: Engages/bypasses the modulation on the repeats independently of the delay itself, so modulation can be tapped in/out while the delay keeps running.
- Both use Flexi-Switch relay-based true bypass, supporting latching (tap) and momentary (hold) operation.

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the DLY slot conceptually — an analog/lo-fi-voiced delay alternative to the GP-5's own DLY models, with the modulation built in rather than needing a separate MOD module for wobbly, tape-like repeats.
- The independent Modulate footswitch is the standout feature: a patch can run clean, un-modulated delay repeats for most of a song and kick in wobbly/pitch-stretched repeats for a specific section without touching the Time/Repeats/Mix settings.
- "Stretch" mode is extreme enough to function as a texture/effect in its own right (pitch-bending, sci-fi swells on repeats) rather than a subtle chorus-on-delay — reserve it for intentional ambient or experimental moments rather than default use.
- Relay-based true bypass, mono in/out, standard 9V DC power. Uses a PT2399 delay chip; EQD notes exact delay times can vary slightly unit to unit. This is a limited Legacy Reissue run of an earlier EQD model.
