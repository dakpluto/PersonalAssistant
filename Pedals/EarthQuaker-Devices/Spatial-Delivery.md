# EarthQuaker Devices Spatial Delivery (Envelope Filter with Sample & Hold)

Voltage-controlled envelope filter with three switchable sweep modes — Upward Sweep, Downward Sweep, and Sample & Hold — plus a crossfading filter type control and six-slot preset storage.

## Controls
- **Mode toggle**: Selects Upward Sweep, Downward Sweep, or Sample & Hold.
- **Range**: In Up/Down sweep modes, adjusts envelope sensitivity (how hard the input signal has to hit to trigger the sweep). In Sample & Hold mode, it instead sets the sweep/sample speed.
- **Filter**: Crossfades continuously between high-pass (counterclockwise), band-pass (center), and low-pass (clockwise) filtering — a continuous blend rather than a discrete switch.
- **Resonance**: Filter feedback control; adds body and ring/"quack" to the filtered voice.
- **Preset switch**: Selects among six preset slots, each storing all control positions, the selected mode, and any expression-pedal assignment.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Live/Preset mode switch**: Toggles between Live mode (physical knobs control the sound) and Preset mode (the Preset switch recalls a saved setting).
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the effect using Flexi-Switch® technology — tap to latch on/off, hold for momentary. Relay-based true bypass.
- TRS expression jack: assignable to Range, Resonance, or Filter, stored per-preset.

## Notes for patch-building
- Envelope filters respond to picking dynamics, so they sit best early in the chain (before distortion/compression) where the input signal's natural dynamics are still intact — heavy upstream gain or compression flattens the envelope and weakens the sweep response.
- The Filter knob's continuous crossfade between high-pass/band-pass/low-pass makes this more flexible than a fixed auto-wah — useful for both classic "quack" funk tones (band-pass, moderate resonance) and synth-like sweeps (low-pass, high resonance).
- Sample & Hold mode gives random/stepped filter jumps rather than an envelope-tracking sweep — a distinct effect from the Up/Down sweep modes, closer to a random gate/step filter than a wah.
- True bypass (relay-based) — needs power to pass signal; six presets plus Live mode support quick recall of a "funk" vs. "synth" vs. "S&H random" setting via footswitch taps on the Preset switch.
