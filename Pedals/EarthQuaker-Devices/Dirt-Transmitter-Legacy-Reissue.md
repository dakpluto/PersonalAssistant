# EarthQuaker Devices Dirt Transmitter Fuzz Driver (Legacy Reissue)

Silicon-transistor fuzz driver, a limited-run reissue of EQD's original 2008-era Dirt Transmitter format. Ranges from gated "Velcro" fuzz to thick, natural-sounding fuzz/overdrive depending on the Bias setting.

## Controls
- **Level**: Output volume; clockwise for more.
- **Fuzz**: Amount of gain/distortion; clockwise for heavier fuzz, counter-clockwise for lighter overdrive-like tones.
- **Tone**: High-frequency shaping; clockwise brightens, counter-clockwise mellows/rolls off top end.
- **Bias**: The pedal's core voicing control — adjusts voltage to the silicon transistors. Counter-clockwise produces choppy, gated "Velcro" fuzz tones; clockwise produces thicker, more natural/sustained fuzz. EQD notes it's "properly biased" around the 5 o'clock position, but the whole sweep is usable as a distinct voice, not just a trim control.

## Notes for patch-building
- Front-end gain stage — sits early in the chain (guitar into Fuzz, before drive/amp stages), functionally overlapping with the GP-5's DST module but with a much wider and more extreme voicing range via Bias, from splatty gated fuzz to a dense wall of sustain.
- Good substitute or supplement when a patch wants a fuzz character the GP-5's own DST models don't cover, especially anything needing that gated/velcro texture (verse riffs, staccato fuzz stabs) alongside a smoother fuzz option from the same knob.
- Relay-based "Flexi-Switch" true bypass, supports both latching (tap on/off) and momentary (hold-to-engage) footswitch operation from the same switch. Mono in/out, standard 9V DC (2.1mm center-negative), 3mA draw. This is a limited edition (1000-unit run) of a pedal EQD has otherwise reissued/discontinued multiple times, so exact unit-to-unit trim can vary slightly between production runs per EQD's own notes.
