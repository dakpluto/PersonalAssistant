# EarthQuaker Devices Acapulco Gold (V2)

Power amp distortion pedal based on the power section of a cranked vintage Sunn Model T. Deliberately minimal control set — the idea is you shape gain and brightness with your guitar's own volume/tone knobs, not pedal-mounted controls, the same way you'd ride a dimed tube amp from the guitar.

## Controls
- **Volume**: The pedal's single large knob. Sets output level — gets loud fast, use with care. There is no separate gain/drive knob; rolling back the guitar's volume knob is how you clean up or reduce gain, and rolling back the guitar's tone knob darkens the rhythm tone.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch: engages/bypasses the effect. True bypass via electronic relay switching (needs power to pass audio, but doesn't color the signal when off).

## Notes for patch-building
- Functions as a power-amp-in-a-box: sits where a distortion/OD pedal normally would (ahead of the amp, or as the amp itself if running direct), but its gain character responds to the guitar's volume knob more like an overdriven tube power stage than a gain-knob distortion pedal — patch notes should call out that dynamics/cleanup come from the player's guitar volume, not a pedal setting.
- Because there's no gain control, it's an all-or-nothing voicing choice for a given guitar-volume setting; if a song needs two distinct drive levels, that has to come from riding the guitar volume or from a separate boost ahead of it, not from re-dialing the pedal.
- True bypass, mono in/out, 9V DC 2.1mm center-negative, 18 mA current draw.
