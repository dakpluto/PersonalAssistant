# EarthQuaker Devices Sea Machine (Super Chorus)

Six-knob chorus/vibrato pedal built around a short digital delay line, capable of everything from subtle shimmery chorus to wobbly, detuned, near-vibrato pitch-bending and edge-of-oscillation textures.

## Controls
- **Animate**: Controls the delay time driving the effect. Low settings give tight, traditional chorus; higher settings push into dramatic pitch-bent detuning, doubling, and near-slapback territory.
- **Dimension**: Feedback amount for the internal delay line. Low settings add slap-back, midrange settings add reverb-like ambience, and maximum pushes toward echo/resonance and self-oscillation.
- **Depth**: Sets how strongly the LFO modulates the delay time (the intensity of the pitch modulation).
- **Shape**: Shapes the LFO waveform from a soft triangle wave (counterclockwise) to a hard square wave (clockwise).
- **Rate**: Sets LFO speed; a small onboard LED blinks in time with the rate, visible even in bypass.
- **Intensity**: Blends the modulated (wet) and dry signal; unity is around noon, with settings above noon boosting the wet signal.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the effect using Flexi-Switch® technology — tap to latch on/off, or hold for momentary engagement. Relay-based true bypass.

## Notes for patch-building
- A single pedal that spans classic chorus through vibrato-like pitch wobble to ambient/near-oscillating textures depending on Animate/Dimension settings — useful as a stand-in for both a chorus and a vibrato slot on a board.
- Typically placed in the modulation position of a chain (after gain stages, before or alongside delay/reverb); with Dimension and Animate both pushed high it starts to behave like a modulated delay, so it can double as a texture/ambience source rather than a straight chorus.
- Rate LED is a handy tap-along visual reference for setting tempo-synced modulation depth even while the pedal is bypassed.
- True bypass (relay-based) — no buffering when off, so it doesn't help drive long cable runs; needs power to pass signal.
