# EarthQuaker Devices Fuzz Master General Legacy Reissue

Octave fuzz based on the vintage Ace Tone Fuzz Master FM-2, reissued with expanded tonal controls beyond the original. Delivers the classic swelling octave-up fuzz grind with more range dialed in via a 3-way voicing switch.

## Controls
- **Voice** (3-way toggle): Selects the clipping character — Germanium (loose, vintage-style fuzz), Silicon (tighter, more compressed fuzz), or Open/transistor drive (near-clean octave-up / ring-modulator-like tone with little added dirt).
- **Fuzz**: Amount of distortion/dirt — clockwise for heavier fuzz.
- **Tone**: Frequency shaping — clockwise scoops toward bass-heavy, counter-clockwise brightens toward treble.
- **Level**: Output volume.

## Footswitches
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the effect; Flexi-Switch relay-based true bypass, supports latching (tap) and momentary (hold) operation.

## Notes for patch-building
- Front-end gain stage — sits early in the chain, ahead of drive/amp stages, functionally overlapping with the GP-5's DST module but adding the vintage octave-up fuzz character DST models typically don't cover.
- Octave-fuzz circuits like this track best on single notes; full chords will produce a more chaotic, "destructive" texture (per EQD's own description) rather than clean harmony — best suited to single-note riffs, leads, or intentionally gnarly chordal chaos rather than clean rhythm work.
- The Voice switch gives three genuinely distinct fuzz personalities in one footprint (loose germanium, tight silicon, near-clean octave/ring-mod) — useful when a patch needs to pick one fuzz character without stacking multiple fuzz pedals.
- Relay-based true bypass, mono in/out, standard 9V DC power. Limited Legacy Reissue run of a pedal EQD has produced/discontinued in earlier eras.
