# EarthQuaker Devices Chelsea (Low End Fuzz Driver)

Fuzz pedal developed with LCD Soundsystem, recreating a heavily modified vintage 1989 fuzz circuit used extensively on their recordings. Covers a wide range from light overdrive-like grit up through full fuzz/distortion while keeping low end intact and articulate.

## Controls
- **Sustain**: Fuzz/distortion amount and sustain — clockwise for more gain and longer sustain.
- **Tone**: Frequency shaping; clockwise brightens toward treble, counter-clockwise favors bass.
- **Level**: Output volume.

## Switches
- **Tone On/Off**: Bypasses the Tone control entirely for a more mid-focused, uncolored response — a separate voicing option, not just a "tone at noon" setting.

## Notes for patch-building
- Front-end gain stage — sits early in the chain (guitar into fuzz, ahead of any drive/amp stages), overlapping functionally with the GP-5's DST module.
- Keeps low end fuller than many vintage fuzz circuits, so it's a good pick when a fuzz tone is wanted without the low-string flub or thinning some classic fuzzes introduce — useful for drop tunings or bass-adjacent guitar parts as well as bass itself.
- Relay-based "Flexi-Switch" true bypass, supports both latching (tap on/off) and momentary (hold-to-engage) footswitch operation from the same switch. Mono in/out, standard 9V DC (2.1mm center-negative), 10mA draw.
