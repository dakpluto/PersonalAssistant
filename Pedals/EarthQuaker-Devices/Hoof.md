# EarthQuaker Devices Hoof (Hybrid Fuzz, V2)

EarthQuaker's flagship fuzz — a hybrid Germanium/Silicon transistor design (Fuzz Face/Tone Bender derived) built for maximum tone with better temperature stability than an all-germanium circuit. Widely used from small clubs to major stages for its versatility across gain and EQ ranges.

## Controls
- **Fuzz**: Saturation intensity — clockwise for heavy fuzz, counterclockwise for light/lower-gain fuzz, down to near-overdrive territory.
- **Shift**: Midrange voicing — clockwise scoops the mids, counterclockwise boosts them. Effectively a second gain-stage EQ character control specific to this circuit.
- **Tone**: Standard bass/treble tilt — bass to the left, treble to the right.
- **Level**: Overall output volume.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch, true bypass.

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the drive/fuzz position ahead of the amp, same role as any dedicated fuzz pedal — pairs well as a first gain stage into a clean or lightly-driven GP-5 AMP model.
- The Shift control is the pedal's signature feature and the main tool for dialing between vintage Fuzz-Face-style boxy mids (counterclockwise) and a more scooped, modern-voiced fuzz (clockwise) — treat it as a distinct tonal axis from Tone, not a duplicate control.
- Germanium/silicon hybrid design means it's less touch/temperature-sensitive than a pure-germanium fuzz, so settings should translate consistently set to set.
- True bypass, mono — standard fuzz-pedal placement rules apply (avoid stacking after a buffered pedal if maximum vintage fuzz interaction with guitar volume/tone knobs is wanted).
