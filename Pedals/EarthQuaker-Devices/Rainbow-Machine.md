# EarthQuaker Devices Rainbow Machine (Polyphonic Pitch Mesmerizer)

Polyphonic pitch-shifting modulator that generates a pitch-shifted harmony voice alongside your dry signal, with a regeneration control that pushes it from subtle shimmer into self-oscillating synth/noise territory.

## Controls
- **Pitch**: Sets the pitch of the polyphonic harmony voice, ranging from a fourth below the input up to a third above, with every atonal interval in between; noon is the unison position. Can also be controlled via an expression pedal.
- **Primary**: Volume of the polyphonic harmony voice.
- **Secondary**: Blends in an accompanying octave voice or thickens the modulation; its effect depends on where Pitch is set relative to noon.
- **Tracking**: Sets the lag time between wet and dry signal. Turning it up tightens pitch tracking with a shorter delay; turning it down loosens tracking for longer delay and more ambience.
- **Tone**: Rolls off treble on the wet signal for a darker, vintage character — clockwise is brighter, counterclockwise is darker.
- **Magic**: Regeneration/feedback control that feeds the Primary and Secondary signals back on themselves. Increasing it moves through added ambience, resonant edge-of-oscillation pitch delays, chorus, metallic digital flanging, ascending/descending pitch trails, controllable self-oscillation, and synthetic noise/distortion at extremes.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Activate footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the main effect. Relay-based true bypass with Flexi-Switch® technology — tap to latch on/off, or hold for momentary engagement.
- **Magic footswitch**: Independently engages/bypasses the Magic regeneration circuit, so the feedback chaos can be switched in and out separately from the base harmony effect.

## Notes for patch-building
- Polyphonic — tracks full chords, not just single notes, which sets it apart from most monophonic pitch-shifter/harmonizer stompboxes.
- Best placed after any gain/distortion stage (post-DST/AMP), since pitch tracking degrades on a heavily saturated or highly compressed signal; it wants a relatively clean, dynamic input to track accurately.
- The two independent footswitches let a board use the harmony as a subtle always-on texture while reserving the Magic footswitch for a chorus/section-specific burst of self-oscillating chaos.
- True bypass (relay-based) — no tone-suck concerns when off, but like other relay-bypass EQD pedals it needs power to pass signal at all.
