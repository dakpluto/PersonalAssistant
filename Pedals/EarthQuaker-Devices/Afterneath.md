# EarthQuaker Devices Afterneath (Enhanced Otherworldly Reverberator)

All-analog-dry/digital-wet ambient reverb built around a network of modulated, cascading delay lines rather than a conventional algorithmic reverb — known for lush, swirling, semi-controllable "otherworldly" washes and self-oscillation rather than clean, natural room/hall sounds.

## Controls
- **Length**: Reverb decay length, from short-ish plate-like tails to long, sustained wash.
- **Diffuse**: Spread of the reflections — counterclockwise gives sharper, more identifiable/stuttered repeats, clockwise smooths into a dense, washy blur.
- **Dampen**: Tonal character of the reverb tail — counterclockwise brightens, clockwise darkens/mellows.
- **Drag**: Detunes/spreads the underlying delay lines relative to each other; clockwise pulls them together toward a more conventional reverb-like sound, counterclockwise scatters them into ping-pong/pitch-shifted textures. Can be controlled via expression pedal or CV.
- **Reflect**: Feedback/regeneration amount — how much of the output feeds back into the network. Can be pushed into self-oscillation.
- **Mix**: Balance of dry input to wet effect (does not go 100% wet even at max — input signal is always present, just attenuated as wet increases).
- **Mode**: Selects one of 9 tracking behaviors for the Drag control (Unquantized, Unquantized w/ Slew, Volt/Octave, Chromatic, Major, Minor, Pentatonic, Octaves & Fifths, Octaves) — mainly relevant when Drag is under expression/CV control.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch with Flexi-Switch technology: supports both latching (tap to engage/bypass) and momentary (hold to engage, release to bypass) operation from the same switch.

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the reverb slot at the end of the chain (post-drive, post-modulation), same functional role as the GP-5's RVB module — but voice it as a texture/ambience effect rather than a room simulator; it's the pedal to reach for when a patch wants shimmering, unpredictable, semi-chaotic reverb tails (ambient/shoegaze/post-rock use) rather than a natural-sounding space.
- Reflect can self-oscillate — useful for swells/drones as a deliberate effect, but a patch relying on a stable, controlled reverb tail should keep Reflect well short of that threshold.
- Electronic buffered bypass (requires power to pass signal even when bypassed). Mono in/out. 9V DC 2.1mm center-negative, 100 mA current draw.
