# EarthQuaker Devices Bellows Legacy Reissue (Fuzz Driver)

Two-knob fuzz driver pedal (reissue of an earlier EarthQuaker circuit) spanning amp-like breakup at low settings through full fuzz saturation at high settings.

## Controls
- **Drive**: Input gain, controlling the amount of dirt. Lower settings give amp-like breakup; higher settings push into full fuzz saturation.
- **Level**: Output level, set by adjusting the bias voltage of the transistor stage. Counterclockwise produces thinner, cleaner-leaning tones; clockwise adds thickness and growl (bias-driven tonal shift, not just a volume change).

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch: true bypass via electronic relay switching (needs power to pass audio, but doesn't color tone when off).

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the drive/front-end slot ahead of the amp — same functional role as the GP-5's DST module or a fuzz/overdrive pedal choice, spanning light amp-like breakup through full fuzz depending on Drive.
- Because Level is a bias control rather than a plain output pot, it changes tonal character (thin/clean vs. thick/growly) as well as volume — treat Level as part of the drive voicing, not just a level-matching knob, when documenting a patch's settings.
- Mono in/out. 9V DC 2.1mm center-negative, 13 mA current draw. EarthQuaker recommends isolated power and warns against exceeding 9V.
