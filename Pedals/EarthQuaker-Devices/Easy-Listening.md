# EarthQuaker Devices Easy Listening

Note: EQD lists this in their current lineup as an all-analog amp/cab simulator (not a chorus/vibrato pedal). It's a one-knob headphone/DI amp-sim voiced after a black-panel Fender Deluxe Reverb, meant for silent practice or going direct into a DAW/interface, with zero added digital latency or artifacts.

## Controls
- **Level**: Output volume — EQD/reviewers note roughly 9 o'clock is already plenty loud for headphone use; going into a DAW or audio interface will typically need more.

## Notes for patch-building
- Not a chain effect — it's an amp+cab simulator meant to sit at the very end of the signal path in place of a real amp, feeding headphones, an audio interface, or a mixer/PA direct, not feeding into the GP-5's own AMP/CAB modules.
- Output is stereo TRS 1/4" (tip = left, ring = right), so it needs a TRS-aware headphone/interface cable or splitter, not a standard mono TS cable, to get both channels.
- Useful as a quiet-practice or direct-to-interface alternative when the GP-5 (or a NAM capture) isn't the desired signal path — e.g., silent late-night practice — rather than as a pedalboard-chain tone-shaping tool.
- All-analog circuit, standard 9V DC power (2.1mm center-negative), 75mA draw. Bypass behavior isn't documented by EQD; treat it as always-on/inline like other single-purpose utility-style EQD pedals unless confirmed otherwise.
