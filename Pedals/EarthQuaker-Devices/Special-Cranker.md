# EarthQuaker Devices Special Cranker

All-discrete analog overdrive designed to add grit and boost without drastically recoloring the underlying tone, with a switchable diode clipping section.

## Controls
- **More**: Gain control, adjusting transistor bias from nearly clean up to a medium overdrive at maximum. Some minor noise while turning the knob is normal/expected.
- **Tone**: Treble control — counterclockwise rolls off highs, clockwise boosts them, with a roughly flat response around the 3 o'clock position.
- **Level**: Output volume; unity gain falls roughly between 10 and 12 o'clock, varying with which diode clipping option is selected.
- **Diode selector** (toggle): Two-position switch choosing Germanium clipping (softer, more transparent/vintage character) or Silicon clipping (brighter, more modern edge).

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the effect using Flexi-Switch® technology — tap to latch on/off, hold for momentary engagement. Relay-based true bypass.

## Notes for patch-building
- A low-to-medium-gain overdrive meant to push an already-good amp tone rather than define the tone itself — fits well as a boost/first-gain-stage pedal ahead of an amp or amp-sim, rather than as a stand-alone high-gain distortion source.
- The Germanium/Silicon diode switch is a genuinely different clipping character, not just a gain-level change — Germanium leans softer/vintage, Silicon leans brighter/harder, useful for matching a patch's drive to a specific era/style of tone.
- Runs safely from 9–18V; EQD recommends isolated/transformer-based supplies over daisy-chains for quietest operation, which matters on boards with digital pedals sharing the same power brick.
- True bypass (relay-based) — no signal coloration when off, but needs power to pass signal through the chain.
