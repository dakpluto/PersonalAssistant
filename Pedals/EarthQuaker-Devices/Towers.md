# EarthQuaker Devices Towers (Stereo Reverberant Filter)

A stereo reverb pedal built around a resonant low-pass filter and generative micro-delays layered into the reverb tail, with three distinct modes for how the filter is controlled. Not a conventional room/hall/plate reverb — closer to a reverb/filter hybrid instrument.

## Controls
- **Length**: Controls reverb decay length (short to very long); also sets the speed of the Stretch feature.
- **Mix**: Balances dry signal against the reverb/filter effect.
- **Frequency**: Controls the filter's center frequency; its behavior depends on the selected Mode.
- **Filter**: Controls how much the resonant low-pass filter is blended into the reverb signal path, setting the depth of harmonic resonance/coloration.
- **Preset**: Selects among 8 onboard presets (knob/mode settings recalled together).
- **Mode toggle (3-position)**: Selects how the Frequency control behaves —
  - **Manual**: Frequency knob directly sets a fixed filter cutoff.
  - **Envelope**: Playing dynamics drive the filter's cutoff/sensitivity (auto-wah-like response into the reverb).
  - **LFO**: A variable-speed low-frequency oscillator sweeps the filter, producing flange-like, oscillating textures across the reverb.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Activate**: Turns the effect on/off. LED color indicates bypass behavior: green = Tails (reverb trails off after bypass), red = Full Bypass (immediate cutoff).
- **Stretch**: Slows the entire internal digital processor while held/engaged — doubles the reverb length, shifts the frequency spectrum downward, and introduces a pitch-bending, randomized-delay smear effect.

## Notes for patch-building
- True stereo in/out (a first for EarthQuaker) — best placed at the end of the chain, after modulation/delay, to fully use its stereo field; can also run mono in/stereo out.
- Substitutes for or supplements the GP-5's RVB module when a more textured, filter-driven ambient reverb is wanted rather than a straight hall/spring/plate — Envelope and LFO modes give it a semi-modulation/filter character that overlaps with what a MOD block would otherwise provide.
- The Stretch footswitch is a performance/momentary-style effect (pitch/spectrum shift plus doubled decay) — best assigned to a moment in a song (bridge, outro swell) rather than left engaged throughout.
- Expression jack (TRS) is assignable to Length, Mix, Frequency, or Filter for real-time control.
