# EarthQuaker Devices Bit Commander (V2) (Analog Octave Synth)

Monophonic analog octave/synth pedal that tracks the input pitch and generates square-wave synth tones at multiple fixed octave intervals, blended with the (squared) fundamental — built for old-school synth/octave textures rather than tracking chords or polyphonic playing.

## Controls
- **Level**: Master output volume.
- **Filter**: Overall tone control — clockwise brightens/adds treble, counterclockwise warms/darkens.
- **Base**: Level of the squared (waveshaped) fundamental input signal.
- **Sub**: Level of the two-octaves-down voice.
- **Down 1**: Level of the one-octave-down voice.
- **Up 1**: Level of the one-octave-up voice (transformer-based).

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch: true bypass via electronic relay switching (needs power to pass audio, but doesn't color tone when off).

## Notes for patch-building
- Best suited to single-note lines (monophonic tracking) — chords and dense playing will cause mistracking/glitching, so use it on lead lines, riffs, or single-note bass parts rather than full chordal rhythm parts.
- Sits early in the chain, before drive/distortion (tracks a clean, un-clipped input best) — pair the octave voices (Sub/Down 1/Up 1) sparingly and blend to taste against Base; can substitute for or stack with the GP-5's own octave/pitch effects when a patch wants a more overtly synth-like square-wave character rather than a clean tracking octaver.
- Mono in/out. 9V DC 2.1mm center-negative, 25 mA current draw. EarthQuaker recommends transformer-isolated power and warns against exceeding 9V.
