# EarthQuaker Devices Blumes (Low Signal Shredder)

All-analog overdrive built on a Tube Screamer-derived soft-clipping circuit, voiced with extra gain and deeper bass response than EQD's own Plumes (Small Signal Shredder). Marketed primarily as a bass overdrive but works on guitar as well.

## Controls
- **Gain**: Amount of overdrive drive.
- **Tone**: Sweeps from bass-heavy (counter-clockwise) through midrange (center) to treble-forward (clockwise).
- **Level**: Output volume.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **3-way clipping Mode switch**: Selects the clipping stage character.
  - Symmetrical LED clipping — crunchy, open, uncompressed, high output.
  - No clipping (clean OpAmp boost) — full-frequency clean boost with no added grit.
  - Asymmetrical silicon diode clipping — warmer, looser, more compressed, some fuzz-like character.
- **Footswitch**: Relay-based "Flexi-Switch" true bypass; supports both latching (tap on/off) and momentary (hold-to-engage) operation simultaneously.

## Notes for patch-building
- Front-end drive/boost stage, the same functional role as the GP-5's DST or a boost-into-AMP: use the clean-boost (no-clipping) mode to push an amp's front end, or the LED/silicon modes as the primary drive voice.
- Because it's designed around low-signal (bass-friendly) headroom with deep bass retention, it's a strong overdrive choice ahead of a bass-amp patch, or for guitar patches wanting an OD that doesn't thin out the low end the way a typical Tube Screamer clone does.
- Internal bipolar power supply gives extra headroom/dynamics beyond standard 9V pedals; still runs from standard 9V DC (2.1mm center-negative). Mono in/out.
