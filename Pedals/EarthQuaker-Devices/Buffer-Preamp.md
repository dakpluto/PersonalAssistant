# EarthQuaker Devices Buffer/Preamp

Passive-feel utility pedal (Utility Pedal Series) that restores tone lost to long cable runs and chains of true-bypass pedals. High 1MΩ input impedance and low 100Ω output impedance re-buffer the signal, with a slight low-end lift for added dimension.

## Controls
- None. This is a "set it and forget it" always-on inline buffer — no knobs to dial in.

## Notes for patch-building
- No footswitch, no bypass — it's always in the signal path wherever it's placed, so it isn't something a patch "engages" or "disengages" like an effect.
- Typical placement is at the very front of a long pedal chain (to buffer the guitar's signal before it degrades through several true-bypass pedals) or at the very end/output (right before a long cable run to the amp or GP-5 input) — EQD notes it works well in either position.
- Functional role is pure signal-integrity insurance, not tone-shaping: reach for it when a board has several true-bypass drive/fuzz pedals in series ahead of the GP-5 and high end is getting lost, rather than as a voice in the patch itself.
- The mild bass boost is a side effect of the buffering circuit, not an adjustable EQ control — don't treat it as a tone-shaping tool.
