# EarthQuaker Devices Dispatch Master (Digital Delay & Reverb)

Combines an independent digital delay and digital reverb in one compact unit — hi-fi voiced, not analog/lo-fi — usable together or separately since they share a single Mix knob but the reverb can be run alone via the Repeats knob's dual function.

## Controls
- **Mix**: Overall wet/dry blend of the combined delay+reverb signal against the dry signal.
- **Time**: Delay time, roughly 0ms up to 1500ms (1.5 seconds).
- **Reverb**: Reverb amount — counter-clockwise for less, clockwise for more; at zero the reverb is effectively off and the pedal behaves as a delay-only unit.
- **Repeats**: Delay feedback/regeneration amount, up to infinite self-sustaining repeats at maximum with no runaway feedback squeal. With Time turned all the way down (delay effectively off), Repeats instead governs the reverb's decay length, depth, and tonal character, letting the pedal work as a reverb-only unit.

## Footswitches
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the whole effect (delay+reverb together); Flexi-Switch relay-based true bypass, supports latching (tap) and momentary (hold) operation.

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the DLY→RVB portion of the chain — a single pedal covering both roles, useful when a board wants delay and reverb from one footprint rather than two separate pedals.
- Because Time and Reverb can each be dialed to zero independently, it can function purely as a delay, purely as a reverb, or as both blended together — flexible enough to substitute for either the GP-5's DLY or RVB module alone, not just both at once.
- Digital/hi-fi voiced (not a warm/dark analog delay), so it complements amp or NAM tones that want clean, clear repeats and ambience rather than degraded/lo-fi character.
- Relay-based true bypass, mono in/out, standard 9V DC power, 78mA draw (a power-hungrier pedal — worth checking power supply headroom on a crowded board).
