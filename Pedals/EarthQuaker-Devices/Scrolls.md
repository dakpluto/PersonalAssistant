# EarthQuaker Devices Scrolls (Bass Odyssey)

Two-channel bass preamp/overdrive pedal combining a tube-amp-style Drive channel with an active multi-band EQ channel, plus a balanced XLR DI out — built to function as a compact all-in-one bass rig rather than a simple stompbox effect.

**Note:** EarthQuaker's own product page describes Scrolls as a bass preamp/overdrive/EQ device ("Bass Odyssey"), not a vibrato/tremolo/pitch modulator — see report notes.

## Controls

### Drive channel
- **Drive**: Overdrive/gain intensity.
- **Blend**: Mixes clean and overdriven signal; fully clockwise maximizes the overdriven/crunch content.
- **Bandwidth**: Sets the clipping stage's frequency range — counterclockwise emphasizes low-end warmth, clockwise opens up high-end crunch.
- **Tone**: Low-pass filter on the drive signal; counterclockwise rolls off highs.
- **Level**: Output volume for the Drive channel.

### EQ channel
- **Bass**: Center-detent bass boost/cut.
- **Middle**: Center-detent midrange boost/cut.
- **Treble**: Center-detent treble boost/cut.
- **Vari**: Variable-frequency boost/cut control, center-detented at unity.
- **Vari-Freq**: Sets the target frequency for the Vari control, sweepable roughly 20 Hz–10 kHz.
- **Level**: Output volume when the EQ channel is engaged; noon is unity gain.
- **Deep** (push switch): Adds an 80 Hz low-end boost.
- **Process** (push switch): Scoops the midrange for a modern, punchy character.
- **Bright** (push switch): Adds a 5 kHz boost for extra attack/definition.

### Output section
- **Parallel Out**: Always-on buffered clean output for splitting to a second amp or rig.
- **Direct Out**: Balanced XLR output with onboard cabinet-simulation-style filtering, for going direct to a PA/interface.
- **Ground Lift**: Push-button on the XLR output to cut hum/ground loops.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Drive footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the Drive channel (Flexi-Switch® technology — tap to latch, hold for momentary).
- **EQ footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the EQ channel (standard on/off switching).
- Signal flows Drive → (always-active effects loop) → EQ; the effects loop sits hard-wired between the two channels and is always in the path regardless of channel bypass state.
- Bypass is relay-based true bypass on both channels; the unit requires power to pass signal.

## Notes for patch-building
- Built specifically for bass, but the Drive channel's overdrive and the EQ channel's boost/scoop controls are usable as a general two-stage tone-shaping/gain block for any low-end-focused instrument.
- The always-on effects loop between Drive and EQ is a real routing quirk — an external pedal patched into that loop stays in the chain even when both channels are bypassed.
- Because EQ and Drive are independently footswitchable, it can serve as "clean boost/EQ correction" (EQ channel alone) or "grit" (Drive channel alone) without committing to both at once.
- The XLR Direct Out with ground lift makes this a plausible end-of-chain DI solution for a bass board, separate from its front-of-amp tone-shaping role.
