# EarthQuaker Devices Sunn O))) Life Pedal (Octave Distortion + Booster)

Signature collaboration pedal with Sunn O))), combining a blendable analog octave-up, a heavy distortion with switchable clipping, and a clean booster in one unit — the full-size sibling of the more compact HalfLife.

## Controls
- **Octave**: Blends in the analog octave-up effect as it's turned clockwise; works best on single notes played above roughly the 12th fret.
- **Distortion**: Gain control for the distortion stage, offering roughly +60dB of gain; clockwise increases distortion.
- **Filter**: Low-pass filter, roughly 500 Hz–30 kHz; counterclockwise brightens, clockwise darkens.
- **Amplitude**: Output volume for the octave/distortion section.
- **Magnitude**: Output volume for the separate booster section; unity gain around noon.
- **Clipping switch**: Three-position toggle — OpAmp (no diodes, grinding/open tone), Asymm (asymmetrical diode clipping, smooth crunch), or Symm (stock dual-silicon-diode clipping).

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Amplitude footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the distortion section. Flexi-Switch® technology — tap to latch, hold for momentary.
- **Magnitude footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the clean booster section, independently of the distortion side.
- **Octave footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the octave effect — but the octave does not work independently; the Distortion (Amplitude) section must be active for the octave to be audible.
- **Octave EXP jack**: Accepts a TRS expression pedal to control the analog octave blend hands-free, e.g. for octave swells mid-riff.
- Relay-based true bypass.

## Notes for patch-building
- Three independent footswitches give more live flexibility than the HalfLife: distortion, booster, and octave (dependent on distortion) can each be toggled separately mid-song.
- Remember the dependency — the Octave footswitch does nothing unless Amplitude (distortion) is already engaged; don't plan a patch/CTL assignment that expects a clean octave-only sound from this pedal.
- The booster (Magnitude) is a genuinely separate, transparent clean-boost circuit and can be used on its own — e.g. as a solo/volume push — without engaging any octave or distortion.
- Same three-way clipping options as the HalfLife (OpAmp/Asymm/Symm) — a real circuit-level tonal choice worth recording per patch, not just a gain setting.
- Larger enclosure than the HalfLife but functionally the same core circuit with an added dedicated octave footswitch and expression control — pick based on board space vs. wanting that extra octave switch.
