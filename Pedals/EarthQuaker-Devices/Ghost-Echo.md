# EarthQuaker Devices Ghost Echo (V3)

Analog/digital spring reverb emulation ("vintage voiced reverb") modeled on the character of old amp-top spring tanks. Known for a haunted, slightly dark spring-reverb voice with a controllable pre-delay, plus a self-oscillating drone mode.

## Controls
- **Attack**: Sets pre-delay length, roughly 30ms at minimum up to about 150ms at maximum. When Dwell is pushed into self-oscillation, Attack instead controls the pitch of the oscillation.
- **Dwell**: Sets decay length; pushed clockwise toward around the 3 o'clock position the circuit starts self-oscillating into a sustained drone/tone.
- **Depth**: Wet/dry mix. Unity is around noon; everything past that boosts the wet signal further.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch, relay-based true bypass — engages/bypasses the effect. V3 uses updated op-amps/preamp tuning for a warmer, quieter, higher-headroom version of the same three-knob circuit.

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits last in the chain (reverb slot) — after any modulation/delay, into the amp.
- The self-oscillation behavior (Dwell near max) is a real, usable drone/swell effect, not just a design limit — worth flagging if a patch wants ambient/noise textures rather than a conventional reverb tail.
- Mono in/out, true bypass. A straightforward analog-voiced reverb substitute or complement to the GP-5's own RVB module — pick it over the onboard reverb when a darker, more "amp spring tank" character is wanted rather than a clean hall/plate/room algorithm.
- Only three controls and no tone-shaping knob — if the patch needs a brighter or more digital-sounding reverb, this isn't the tool; it's voiced specifically dark/vintage.
