# EarthQuaker Devices Plumes (Small Signal Shredder)

All-analog transparent overdrive in the Tube Screamer/TS808 tradition, built for low noise, high headroom, and three distinct clipping voices, designed to push a tube amp into responsive breakup rather than mask it with heavy coloration.

## Controls
- **Level**: Output volume.
- **Gain**: Drive/saturation amount.
- **Tone**: Three-position control (not a sweepable knob) — bass emphasis, midrange focus (center), or treble emphasis.
- **Clipping mode toggle (3-way)**:
  - **LED**: Symmetrical LED clipping — the "crunchiest," most compressed voice.
  - **Clean Boost**: No clipping stage engaged — pure op-amp gain for a clean boost.
  - **Diode**: Asymmetrical silicon diode clipping — more transparent, warmer breakup.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch, true bypass, Flexi-Switch technology (latching or momentary/hold operation).

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the drive slot ahead of the amp, same role as the GP-5's own PRE/DST modules or any low/medium-gain overdrive pedal — a natural low-gain "amp push" tool rather than a high-gain distortion source.
- The Clean Boost clipping-toggle position makes this pedal double as a clean boost without needing a separate boost pedal — useful for a solo-volume-bump role in a signal chain.
- Because Tone is a 3-position switch rather than a sweeping knob, don't expect fine EQ shaping from it — treat it as a coarse voicing choice (bass/mid/treble emphasis) rather than a parametric control.
- True bypass, mono, low current draw (~25mA) — straightforward drop-in overdrive with no quirks around bypass or signal path.
