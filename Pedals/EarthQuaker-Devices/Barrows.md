# EarthQuaker Devices Barrows (Fuzz Attacker)

Two-knob germanium fuzz pedal built around three matched germanium transistors. Note: EarthQuaker's own product page and current lineup list this pedal as "Barrows Fuzz Attacker," not as a boost/preamp — it is a dirt/fuzz pedal (its Level knob does have generous boost headroom, which may be the source of any boost/preamp characterization, but the circuit itself is a fuzz).

## Controls
- **Level**: Output volume; unity gain sits around noon, with plenty of extra boost available above that to push a downstream amp harder.
- **Attack**: Gain/character control. At low settings, gives a singing, amp-like overdrive with strong touch sensitivity; turning clockwise pushes it into an increasingly aggressive, sustain-drenched fuzz.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Single footswitch: mechanical true bypass on/off.

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits in the drive/front-end slot, ahead of the amp (or ahead of the GP-5's own DST/AMP modules) — same functional role as the GP-5's DST module or a boost pedal, but with germanium fuzz character rather than clipping-diode distortion.
- Attack's low-gain end behaves more like a touch-sensitive overdrive than a full fuzz, so it can cover both "boost into a clean amp" and "full fuzz" duty depending on where Attack is set — useful when a patch wants one drive pedal to span both roles rather than carrying two.
- Phase-corrected, buffered output — plays nicely stacked with other pedals/buffers on a board despite the true-bypass switching. Mono in/out. 9V DC 2.1mm center-negative, 10 mA current draw. EarthQuaker recommends isolated, transformer-based power (not a daisy chain) to minimize noise.
