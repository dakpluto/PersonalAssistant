# EarthQuaker Devices Ledges (Tri-Dimensional Reverberation Machine)

Compact digital reverb pedal offering three reverb algorithms (Room, Hall, Plate) with six saveable/recallable presets and expression control — built for quick access to a range of reverb types without deep menu diving.

## Controls
- **Length**: Reverb decay time, short to long.
- **Mix**: Wet/dry balance of the reverb signal.
- **Damping**: Rolls off high-frequency content in the reverb reflections/tail.
- **Mode switch (3-position: R/H/P)**: Selects the active reverb algorithm — Room, Hall, or Plate.
- **Preset selector (rotary)**: Chooses among six saved preset slots.

## Footswitches / Switches
- Main footswitch: engages/bypasses the effect. Buffered bypass with trails — reverb tails continue to ring out naturally after bypass rather than cutting off. Uses EarthQuaker's relay-based Flexi-Switch technology (latching and momentary/hold operation).
- Save/Recall footswitch: toggles between Live mode (green LED, knobs control the sound directly) and Preset mode (red LED, recalls one of the six saved presets).
- Expression jack (1/4" stereo/TRS): user-assignable expression control, defaults to mapping onto Damping.

## Notes for patch-building
- Sits last in the chain (reverb slot), same role as the GP-5's own RVB module — pick this over the onboard reverb when Room/Hall/Plate algorithm choice and instant preset recall matter more than deep parameter editing.
- Buffered bypass with trails is a real behavioral difference from a true-bypass reverb: the tail keeps decaying naturally after the pedal is switched off, which matters for CTL choreography around reverb on/off timing.
- Mono in/out (6.3mm mono jacks) — not stereo despite offering three algorithms.
- Six onboard presets plus a Live mode give this pedal effectively its own internal preset-switching independent of the GP-5's own CTL/patch system — useful if a song needs more than one reverb character without burning a second GP-5 patch slot.
