# EarthQuaker Devices Silos (Multi-Generational Time Reflection Device)

Compact delay pedal combining three delay types — Digital, Analog, and Tape — in one unit, with tap tempo, six-slot preset storage, and expression control.

**Note:** EarthQuaker's own product page describes Silos as a delay pedal, not an overdrive — see report notes.

## Controls
- **Mode toggle**: Selects the delay engine — Digital, Analog, or Tape — each with its own character.
- **Time**: Sets delay time from 0–1 second; character varies by mode (shorter settings for staccato slap/doubling, longer for atmospheric ambience).
- **Repeats**: Controls delay regeneration. Does not self-oscillate in Digital mode, but begins to self-oscillate from roughly the 3 o'clock position in Analog and Tape modes.
- **Mix**: Balances delay (wet) level against the dry signal.
- **Preset knob**: Selects among six preset slots, each storing all three knob positions, the selected mode, and any expression-pedal assignment.

## Footswitches / Switches
- **Footswitch**: Engages/bypasses the effect using Flexi-Switch® technology — tap to latch on/off, or hold for momentary engagement.
- **Live/Preset mode**: The pedal operates in one of two modes — in Live mode the physical knobs control the sound directly; in Preset mode, the Preset knob recalls a saved setting (including expression assignment) and the physical knob positions are ignored until you return to Live mode.
- Bypass is buffered with trails/tails — the pedal always needs power to pass signal, and existing delay repeats continue decaying naturally after bypass rather than cutting off.
- TRS expression jack: assignable to Time, Repeats, or Mix for hands-free control, and stored per-preset.

## Notes for patch-building
- Designed to take dirt/gain well — EQD specifically notes it was voiced to respond cleanly to overdrive and saturation upstream, so it's a reasonable choice for a delay slot placed after drive/distortion pedals.
- Buffered bypass with tails means it's safe to place anywhere in a chain without a true-bypass tone-suck concern, and delay trails won't get cut off if it's switched off mid-decay.
- No modulation on the delay repeats (a noted limitation vs. more feature-dense EQD delays), so pair with a separate chorus/modulation pedal if wobbly, modulated tape-style repeats are wanted rather than clean tape-mode slap.
- Six onboard presets plus Live mode make it workable either as a simple always-set-it-and-forget-it delay or as a small delay "looper" of saved tones recalled via footswitch taps on the Preset knob.
