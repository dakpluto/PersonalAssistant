Amp & Cab Handling

If you're using your own NAM capture or IR instead of the GP-5's built-in AMP/CAB.

I'll handle my own amp (NAM or other) Skips the GP-5's AMP block in the build. Pick the closest built-in model below just so the build knows what it's working around.
I'll handle my own cab (IR or other) Skips the GP-5's CAB block in the build.

Note: if your amp capture is amp-only (no cab baked in) and you haven't also got an external cab solution, you'll get a harsh/unfiltered tone — this build won't add a GP-5 cab behind it for you.
