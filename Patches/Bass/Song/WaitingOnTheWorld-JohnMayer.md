# Waiting on the World to Change — John Mayer

From *Continuum* (2006). About 88 BPM, est.
Bass: Harley Benton P/J, 5-string, passive. GP-5 only.
A deep, muted soul pocket. As far as I know, Pino Palladino played on much of *Continuum*, and his signature is a short, thumpy, flatwound-style note behind the beat.
Your P/J has roundwounds, so the patch and your hands fake the flatwound thump: heavy palm-muting near the bridge, tone rolled back.
Fingers, palm-muted. P pickup soloed. Tone knob around 25%.

## AMP: Classic Bass (Ampeg SVT) + CAB: Apg115 IR (User IR 1)

A B-15 with a dark top is the classic soul thump. It's the studio amp behind decades of this sound.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 10.
Low.

**PRE — COMP (Ross)**, always on.
Sustain: 38, VOL: 58.
Even, thumpy notes.

**DST — off.** No grit.

**AMP — Classic Bass**, always on.
Gain: 20, Bass: 60, Middle: 52, MidFreq: 220Hz, Treble: 28, VOL: 66.
Dark and thumpy. Treble 28 plus the rolled-back tone knob gives the flatwound impression.

**CAB — User IR 1 (Apg115)**, always on.
VOL: 60.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: 0, 120Hz: +3, 400Hz: +1, 800Hz: -1, 4.5kHz: -6, VOL: 52.
120Hz thump and 400Hz wood. -6 at 4.5kHz kills roundwound zing.

**MOD — off.**

**DLY — off.**

**RVB — Room**, on CTL.
Mix: 10, Decay: 25, Trail: on.
A tiny room for the choruses.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: RVB (Room).

- **CTL off** — Verses. A dead, deep, muted soul pocket. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Choruses. A small room so the thump blooms slightly.

Engage at the choruses. Mostly this patch just lives in CTL off.
