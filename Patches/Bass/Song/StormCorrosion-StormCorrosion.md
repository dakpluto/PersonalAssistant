# Storm Corrosion — Storm Corrosion

Storm Corrosion, the Steven Wilson and Mikael Akerfeldt project. BPM est., slow. Dark, sparse, folk-tinged ambient. The bass is warm and deep, working as a low anchor, not a lead voice. Built from the project's general sound; exact bass rig and who played it not verified.
GP-5 only, P/J bass, no pedalboard.

## AMP: Classic Bass + CAB: Apg115410 IR (User IR 2)

Apg115410 — flat, thunderous, deep. Anchors a sparse arrangement.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 8.
Very quiet tone. 8 barely touches it.

**PRE — COMP**, always on.
Sustain: 35, VOL: 58.
Very light. Preserves dynamics.

**DST — Bass OD**, on CTL.
Gain: 20, Blend: 30, VOL: 60, Bass: 50, Treble: 35.
Light push for the climax. Dark treble, Blend 30.

**AMP — Classic Bass**, always on.
Gain: 15, Bass: 62, Middle: 45, MidFreq: 220Hz, Treble: 30, VOL: 64.
Very clean SVT. Gain 15, Treble 30, MidFreq 220Hz for a woolly low-mid.

**CAB — User IR 2 (Apg115410)**, always on.
VOL: 60.
Apg115410 IR.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: +3, 120Hz: +3, 400Hz: +1, 800Hz: -3, 4.5kHz: -5, VOL: 50.
Dark. Low end up, top rolled off.

**MOD — off.** No modulation.

**DLY — off.** No delay.

**RVB — Deepsea**, on CTL.
Mix: 25, Decay: 55, Trail: off.
Huge deep decay for the climax. Mix 25 is obvious on purpose here.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: DST (Bass OD), RVB (Deepsea).

- **CTL off** — Main body. Dark, deep, clean, dry. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Climactic sections. Light drive and a large Deepsea reverb.

Engage CTL for the climax moments. Back off for the sparse parts.
