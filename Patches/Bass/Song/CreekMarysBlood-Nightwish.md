# Creek Mary's Blood — Nightwish

Nightwish, symphonic metal. BPM est. Slow, tribal, heavy build with an epic arc. Bass is fat, growly, and driven — Marko Hietala is known for a thick pick-driven tone, though the exact rig on this recording is not verified.
GP-5 only, P/J bass, no pedalboard.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 20.
Driven tone. 20 tidies the rests.

**PRE — COMP**, always on.
Sustain: 50, VOL: 58.
Firm compression for pick attack.

**DST — Bass OD**, on CTL.
Gain: 42, Blend: 55, VOL: 60, Bass: 55, Treble: 50.
Growl for the heavy payoff.

**AMP/CAB — NAM SnapTone, slot 55: BrightSVT** (always on)
- Built from the `SVT SANS BRIGHT DRIVE (SVT-CL)` NAM and the Hartke410 IR, combined into one snaptone.
- Real Ampeg SVT-CL with the bright drive setting, into the aluminum-cone Hartke410 IR.
- Symphonic metal: bright, driven bass so the notes survive the orchestra.
- Gain: 46, VOL: 50, Bass: 58, Middle: 55, Treble: 45
- Gain 46: a little under default. This part wants less push than the other BrightSVT patches.
- Bass 58: more low end.
- Middle 55: a touch more midrange.
- Treble 45: top end pulled back a little.
- VOL 50: the default. Trim here if the patch jumps in level against your others.
- Same in both CTL states. AMP and CAB are off in the `.prst`, so nothing stacks on the capture. The N->S block calls slot 55 directly.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: +2, 120Hz: +2, 400Hz: 0, 800Hz: +2, 4.5kHz: +1, VOL: 50.
Mostly flat with a low lift and a little 800Hz for pick definition.

**MOD — off.** No modulation.

**DLY — off.** No delay.

**RVB — Hall**, on CTL.
Mix: 15, Decay: 35, Trail: off.
Adds size for the epic payoff.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: DST (Bass OD), RVB (Hall).

- **CTL off** — Verses and the tribal sections. SVT at moderate gain, thick and warm. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Heavy sections. Bass OD adds growl and hall adds size.

Engage CTL for the heavy sections. Back off for quiet passages.
