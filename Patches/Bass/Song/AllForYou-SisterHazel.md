# All for You — Sister Hazel

Bass: Harley Benton P/J, 5-string, passive. GP-5 only.
From *...Somewhere More Familiar* (1997), ~100 BPM.
Warm, sunny, feel-good 90s alt-rock — jangly acoustic-driven guitars, an easy, welcoming groove. Jett Beres's bass job here is to stay warm and supportive, holding the song's relaxed energy together. It's a pretty consistent, upbeat tune throughout, with a small lift into the "all for you" chorus hook.

CTL off = the main verse groove. CTL on = a gentle lift for the chorus hook.

## GP-5 settings

Module order: NR → PRE → DST → AMP → CAB → EQ → MOD → DLY → RVB.

**NR — Gate**
- THRE: 12
- Always on. Very low threshold — clean, warm tone throughout, nothing to clean up.

**PRE — Micro Boost — On CTL**
- Gain: 45
- CTL off: bypassed (verse). CTL on: engaged (chorus hook).
- A gentle push for the hook — this song stays warm and easygoing throughout, so the lift stays subtle rather than dramatic.

**DST — Off**
- No drive. Clean and warm from top to bottom.

**AMP/CAB — NAM SnapTone, slot 53: CleanSVT** (always on)
- Built from the `SVT CLEAN (SVT-CL)` NAM and the Apg810 IR, combined into one snaptone.
- Real Ampeg SVT-CL preamp on its clean setting, into the Apg810 8x10 IR.
- 90s radio rock. A clean SVT is the default rig for that sound.
- Gain: 50, VOL: 50, Bass: 56, Middle: 54, Treble: 54
- Gain 50: the capture as built.
- Bass 56: more low end.
- Middle 54: a touch more midrange.
- Treble 54: a touch more top end.
- VOL 50: the default. Trim here if the patch jumps in level against your others.
- Same in both CTL states. AMP and CAB are off in the `.prst`, so nothing stacks on the capture. The N->S block calls slot 53 directly.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**
- 50Hz: +3, 120Hz: +1, 400Hz: 0, 800Hz: +2, 4.5kHz: +2, VOL: 55
- Always on, same for both CTL states.
- A gentle, mostly-flat curve — warmth and a touch of presence in equal measure, nothing carved out hard. This part just needs to sit comfortably under the jangly guitars.

**MOD — Off**
- No modulation. Direct and warm — this song's character comes from the amp and the song itself, not an added effect.

**DLY — Off**
- Not used.

**RVB — Room — On CTL**
- Mix: 18, Decay: 28, Trail: On
- CTL off: bypassed (verse — clean and direct). CTL on: engaged (chorus hook).
- A light touch of room opens things up for the sing-along hook, matching the song's easy, warm lift there.

## CTL summary

- **CTL Off — Verse.** Warm, relaxed, sitting comfortably under the acoustic-driven groove.
- **CTL On — Chorus hook.** A gentle boost and a touch of room lift the "all for you" hook just slightly above the rest.
- Engage CTL for the hook line, back off returning to the main verse feel.

## Note on GP-5-only

This patch is GP-5 only — no pedalboard beyond the Valeton GP-5 itself. All the tone comes from the GP-5's own modules above.
