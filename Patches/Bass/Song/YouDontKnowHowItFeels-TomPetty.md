# You Don't Know How It Feels — Tom Petty

From *Wildflowers* (1994). About 92 BPM, est.
Bass: Harley Benton P/J, 5-string, passive. GP-5 only.
A deep, relaxed pocket under a dry groove. The bass is round, warm, and sits behind the beat. Nothing flashy.
Built from the song's overall sound. I haven't verified the exact bass rig on the record.
Fingers over the neck. P pickup soloed. Tone knob around 40%.

## AMP: Classic Bass (Ampeg SVT) + CAB: Apg115 IR (User IR 1)

The B-15's round, woody thump is the classic organic-record bass sound. It suits Rubin's natural production.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 10.
Barely on.

**PRE — COMP (Ross)**, always on.
Sustain: 35, VOL: 58.
Light leveling.

**DST — off.** No grit.

**AMP — Classic Bass**, always on.
Gain: 22, Bass: 60, Middle: 50, MidFreq: 450Hz, Treble: 38, VOL: 65.
Warm, clean, round.

**CAB — User IR 1 (Apg115)**, always on.
VOL: 60.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: +1, 120Hz: +2, 400Hz: 0, 800Hz: 0, 4.5kHz: -4, VOL: 52.
Warm lows, dark top.

**MOD — off.**

**DLY — off.**

**RVB — Room**, on CTL.
Mix: 12, Decay: 25, Trail: on.
A small room for the choruses.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: RVB (Room).

- **CTL off** — Verses. Dry, deep pocket. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Choruses. A small room so the bass opens up.

Engage at the choruses. Off for the verses.
