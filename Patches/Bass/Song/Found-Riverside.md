# Found (The Unexpected Flaw of Searching) — Riverside

Riverside, from Love, Fear and the Time Machine. BPM est. Lighter, more open, art-rock feel. The bass is clear and melodic with some air. Built from the band's general sound; exact rig on the recording not verified.
GP-5 only, P/J bass, no pedalboard.

## AMP: Classic Bass + CAB: EBS410 IR (User IR 4)

EBS410 — accentuated 2-3kHz hi-mids, clear and clean. Fits a melodic, open part.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 10.
Clean tone. 10 is barely there.

**PRE — COMP**, always on.
Sustain: 40, VOL: 58.
Light leveling. Keeps the melodic lines even.

**DST — Bass OD**, on CTL.
Gain: 25, Blend: 38, VOL: 60, Bass: 48, Treble: 50.
Chorus push. Blend 38 keeps clarity.

**AMP — Classic Bass**, always on.
Gain: 22, Bass: 55, Middle: 52, MidFreq: 800Hz, Treble: 48, VOL: 64.
SVT with the EBS410 hi-mid clarity. Gain 22 is clean. MidFreq 800Hz adds note definition for the melodic lines.

**CAB — User IR 4 (EBS410)**, always on.
VOL: 60.
EBS410 IR.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: +2, 120Hz: +2, 400Hz: 0, 800Hz: -1, 4.5kHz: +1, VOL: 50.
Near flat. Small low lift, tiny top lift for air.

**MOD — B-Chorus**, always on.
Depth: 14, Rate: 0.7, VOL: 50.
Light hand. Depth 14, Rate 0.7Hz. Adds width, stays under the note.

**DLY — off.** No delay.

**RVB — Hall**, on CTL.
Mix: 16, Decay: 38, Trail: off.
Adds size for the chorus lifts.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: DST (Bass OD), RVB (Hall).

- **CTL off** — Verses. Clean, clear, slightly chorused, dry. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Chorus and build sections. Light Bass OD and a hall lift.

Engage CTL for the chorus lifts. Back off for verses.
