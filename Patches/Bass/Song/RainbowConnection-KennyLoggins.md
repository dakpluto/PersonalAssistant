# Rainbow Connection — Kenny Loggins

Kenny Loggins' version, from *Return to Pooh Corner* (1994). About 72 BPM, est.
Bass: Harley Benton P/J, 5-string, passive. GP-5 only.
Soft lullaby arrangement. The bass should feel like a gentle acoustic or upright underpinning: round, short, warm. Roots and gentle walk-ups.
Thumb or soft fingers over the neck. P pickup soloed. Tone knob around 30%.

## AMP: Classic Bass (Ampeg SVT) + CAB: Apg115 IR (User IR 1)

A B-15 with the top rolled way back is the softest, woodiest bass this set of loaded cabs can do.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 8.
Minimal.

**PRE — COMP (Ross)**, always on.
Sustain: 32, VOL: 58.
Gentle leveling.

**DST — off.** No grit.

**AMP — Classic Bass**, always on.
Gain: 18, Bass: 58, Middle: 50, MidFreq: 220Hz, Treble: 30, VOL: 66.
Very round and woody. MidFreq 220Hz for warmth.

**CAB — User IR 1 (Apg115)**, always on.
VOL: 60.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: 0, 120Hz: +2, 400Hz: +1, 800Hz: -1, 4.5kHz: -5, VOL: 52.
Woody 400Hz, dark top. It sounds acoustic, not electric.

**MOD — off.**

**DLY — off.**

**RVB — Hall**, on CTL.
Mix: 14, Decay: 40, Trail: on.
A touch of hall for the dreamy choruses.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: RVB (Hall).

- **CTL off** — Verses. Soft, dry, woody. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Choruses. The hall joins the bass to the dreamy wash.

Engage at the choruses.
