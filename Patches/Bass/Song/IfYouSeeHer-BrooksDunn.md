# If You See Her — Brooks & Dunn

"If You See Him/If You See Her" (1998), the duet with Reba McEntire. About 70 BPM, est.
Bass: Harley Benton P/J, 5-string, passive. GP-5 only.
A polished power ballad. Smooth whole and half notes, warm but clearer than a vintage thump, lifting with the chorus.
Built from the song's overall sound. I haven't verified the session bassist's exact rig.
Fingers. P forward with a little J. Tone knob around 50%.

## AMP: Classic Bass (Ampeg SVT) + CAB: TC410 IR (User IR 9)

A clean SVT into the neutral TC410. It's polished and modern-for-1998, a bit clearer than the B-15 thump on Neon Moon.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 10.
Barely there.

**PRE — COMP (Ross)**, always on.
Sustain: 38, VOL: 58.
Smooth, even sustain.

**DST — off.** No grit.

**AMP — Classic Bass**, always on.
Gain: 20, Bass: 58, Middle: 50, MidFreq: 450Hz, Treble: 44, VOL: 65.
Clean and warm with a touch more clarity.

**CAB — User IR 9 (TC410)**, always on.
VOL: 60.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: +1, 120Hz: +2, 400Hz: -1, 800Hz: +1, 4.5kHz: -3, VOL: 52.
Warm lows. 800Hz so moving lines read. The top is trimmed.

**MOD — off.**

**DLY — off.**

**RVB — Plate**, on CTL.
Mix: 18, Decay: 42, Damp: 55, Trail: on.
A smooth plate for the choruses. Damp 55 keeps the tail dark.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: RVB (Plate).

- **CTL off** — Verses. Close and warm. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Choruses. The plate blooms the bass into the big ballad chorus.

Engage at the choruses. Off for the verses.
