# Unstoppable God — Elevation Worship

Bass: Harley Benton P/J, 5-string, passive. Full board.
Part of a 5-song worship set. The board ahead of the GP-5 is fixed across all 5 songs — see "Full Pedalboard" below. Only this GP-5 patch changes song to song.

Elevation Worship writes big, driving arena-worship rock. Unstoppable God sits at 132 BPM in B — a fast, anthemic tune that needs the bass to lock a tight low end under the verses and then push harder under the chorus/bridge without losing the pocket. Two CTL states handle that split: verse stays controlled and supportive, chorus/bridge adds real push and top-end bite.

## GP-5 Settings

Module order: NR → PRE → DST → AMP → CAB → EQ → MOD → DLY → RVB

**NR — Gate**
- THRE: 25
- Always on. Enough to clean up noise from the two gain stages below without chopping sustain on held notes.

**PRE — Boost** (Xotic EP Booster)
- Gain: 55, +3dB: On, Bright: Off
- **CTL switch.** Off = bypassed, On = engaged.
- Off state (verse) stays clean and out of the way. On state (chorus/bridge) is the first half of the push — a clean, +3dB-boosted gain nudge ahead of the OD stage, not a tone-coloring stage. Bright off keeps it from getting harsh stacked with the OD after it.

**DST — Bass OD**
- Gain: 50, Blend: 45, VOL: 58, Bass: 55, Treble: 60
- **CTL switch.** Off = bypassed, On = engaged.
- Blend at 45 keeps the fundamental intact — this needs to still sound like a bass, not a fuzz pedal, even at full push. Treble at 60 gives it the edge to cut through a big arena-rock mix in the chorus. Off = clean verse. On = driven chorus/bridge, stacked with the Boost above for a real dynamic lift.

**AMP — Classic Bass** (Ampeg SVT)
- Gain: 55, Bass: 58, Middle: 55, MidFreq: 800Hz, Treble: 60, VOL: 65
- Always on, both CTL states. SVT is the standard "big worship rock" bass voicing — present and punchy, not scooped. MidFreq at 800Hz pushes the upper-mid growl that cuts through electric guitars and pads instead of hiding under them.

**CAB — AMPG 4x10** (Ampeg SVT-410HE)
- VOL: 60
- Always on. Only real bass cab in the catalog — gives the SVT sim proper low-end weight.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**
- 50Hz: +6, 120Hz: +2, 400Hz: -4, 800Hz: +4, 4.5kHz: +6, VOL: 52
- Always on, both states. 50Hz anchors the low end, 400Hz gets cut to keep it from clashing with guitars/pads in the low-mids, and 800Hz/4.5kHz get pushed for the presence and finger-attack clarity a 132 BPM anthem needs to stay articulate.

**MOD — Off**
Not used. This is a driving, rhythmic part — modulation would just blur the attack.

**DLY — Off**
Not used. Bass delay would muddy a fast tempo like this.

**RVB — Off**
Not used, both CTL states. Any room/ambience in the mix comes from the rest of the band — the bass itself stays dry and upfront.

### CTL Summary (GP-5)
- **CTL Off — Verse (default load state):** Boost and Bass OD both bypassed. Clean, controlled, tight low end — locks the groove without competing for space.
- **CTL On — Chorus / Bridge:** Boost and Bass OD both engaged. Adds real push and top-end bite for the big moments, while EQ and the SVT/cab stay constant underneath so the tone doesn't lurch, just gets bigger.

## Full Pedalboard — Fixed For This Set

This patch is one of 5 built for one worship set (Unstoppable God, Giving It All to You, Your Love Changes Everything, The Bread Has Been Broken, Thrive). Every pedal ahead of the GP-5 is dialed in once and left untouched for the whole set — only the GP-5 patch changes between songs. Don't touch these knobs between songs; that's the point.

**1. Flamma FS-08 Octave — Bypassed, entire set.**
All four octave knobs at 0, Dry at 100, so nothing surprises you if the footswitch gets bumped. None of these five songs call for octave layering.

**2. Donner Ultimate Comp — Engaged, entire set.**
- COMP: 42
- TONE: 50
- LEVEL: 55
- Mode: NORMAL
First in the chain — evens out finger-attack dynamics before anything else touches the signal. Moderate COMP glues the sound together across a set that spans near-silence (Bread) to full push (Unstoppable God, Thrive) without squashing quiet sections. NORMAL mode stays neutral — voicing is handled per-song downstream.

**3. Donner Stylish Fuzz — Bypassed, entire set.**
No fuzz texture anywhere in this set. True bypass throughout.

**4. Joyo Tidal Wave — Engaged, entire set.**
- Drive: 25
- Blend: 35
- Presence: 55
- Level: 55
- Treble: 55, Middle: 58, Bass: 55
- Mid-Frequency toggle: 500Hz
- Bass-Shift toggle: 80Hz (tight, keeps a full band mix from turning to mud)
- Cab-Sim (DI out): On
- Ground Lift: Off (only flip on if a specific room throws hum)
The constant foundation stage for the whole set. Drive is low and Blend under half — this adds harmonic warmth and preamp glue, not an audible overdrive. Also feeds a consistent DI signal to FOH all night, cab-sim engaged. Actual tone character per song comes from the GP-5 patch downstream, not from this pedal.

**5. Joyo Narcissus — Bypassed, entire set.**
Modulation is handled per-song by each GP-5 patch's own MOD module instead (this song: off). Stacking this pedal on top would fight with that. True bypass throughout.

**6. Valeton GP-5 — as detailed above.**

## Footswitch Choreography

- **Verses:** GP-5 CTL off. Comp and Tidal Wave running underneath the whole time, unchanged.
- **Chorus / Bridge:** GP-5 CTL on. That's the only switch this song needs — everything else on the board stays put for the whole set.
