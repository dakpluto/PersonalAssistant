# Neon Moon — Brooks & Dunn

From *Brand New Man* (1991). About 100 BPM, est.
Bass: Harley Benton P/J, 5-string, passive. GP-5 only.
A ballad-tempo two-step feel. Warm root-fifth with soft walk-ups. Round and dark, never pokey.
Built from the song's overall sound. I haven't verified the session bassist's exact rig.
Fingers over the neck. P pickup soloed or dominant. Tone knob around 40%.

## AMP: Classic Bass (Ampeg SVT) + CAB: Apg115 IR (User IR 1)

The B-15 is the classic Nashville studio bass sound: round, woody, and warm.
Apg115's 100Hz peak gives girth. Classic Bass stays clean at low gain.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 10.
Barely there.

**PRE — COMP (Ross)**, always on.
Sustain: 35, VOL: 58.
Evens out the sustained notes lightly.

**DST — off.** No grit.

**AMP — Classic Bass**, always on.
Gain: 20, Bass: 60, Middle: 48, MidFreq: 450Hz, Treble: 38, VOL: 65.
Clean and warm. Treble 38 rounds off finger noise.

**CAB — User IR 1 (Apg115)**, always on.
VOL: 60.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: +1, 120Hz: +2, 400Hz: 0, 800Hz: 0, 4.5kHz: -4, VOL: 52.
Warm lows, top rolled off.

**MOD — off.**

**DLY — off.**

**RVB — Room**, on CTL.
Mix: 15, Decay: 30, Trail: on.
A small room bloom for the choruses.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: RVB (Room).

- **CTL off** — Verses. Dry and warm. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Choruses. The room opens the bass up with the steel and the band.

Engage at the choruses. Off for the verses.
