# We Got Used to Us — Riverside

Riverside. BPM est. Moody, restrained, atmospheric. The bass is warm and round with a bit of width, sitting under the melody instead of pushing. Built from the band's general sound; exact rig on the recording not verified.
GP-5 only, P/J bass, no pedalboard.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 12.
Low gain and low noise. 12 just tidies the rests.

**PRE — COMP**, always on.
Sustain: 42, VOL: 58.
Gentle leveling. 42 keeps dynamics for a moody part.

**DST — Bass OD**, on CTL.
Gain: 25, Blend: 32, VOL: 60, Bass: 50, Treble: 42.
Light push for the build. Blend 32 adds weight, not aggression.

**AMP/CAB — NAM SnapTone, slot 54: FullB15** (always on)
- Built from the `Ampeg B18 - Head DI - Bass Chan - Vol 5 (B-18N)` NAM and the Apg115410 IR, combined into one snaptone.
- Real Ampeg B-18N at volume 5, warmer and fuller than the clean capture, into the Apg115410 (1x15 + 4x10) IR.
- Warm, melodic prog ballad bass. The fuller B-15 gives body and bloom.
- Gain: 49, VOL: 50, Bass: 60, Middle: 48, Treble: 38
- Gain 49: a little under default. This part wants less push than the other FullB15 patches.
- Bass 60: more low end.
- Middle 48: midrange pulled back a little.
- Treble 38: top end pulled back noticeably.
- VOL 50: the default. Trim here if the patch jumps in level against your others.
- Same in both CTL states. AMP and CAB are off in the `.prst`, so nothing stacks on the capture. The N->S block calls slot 54 directly.

**EQ — Bass EQ 2**, always on.
50Hz: +3, 120Hz: +2, 400Hz: +1, 800Hz: -2, 4.5kHz: -3, VOL: 50.
Warm low end, rolled-off top. Keeps the bass under the vocal.

**MOD — B-Chorus**, always on.
Depth: 12, Rate: 0.5, VOL: 50.
Light hand. Depth 12 and a slow 0.5Hz rate add width you feel more than hear.

**DLY — off.** No delay. Reverb handles space.

**RVB — Plate**, on CTL.
Mix: 14, Decay: 30, Damp: 50, Trail: off.
Small plate for the build. Mix 14 keeps the low end clear.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: DST (Bass OD), RVB (Plate).

- **CTL off** — Verses and quiet passages. Warm B-15 tone with a faint chorus, dry. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Bigger sections. Light Bass OD adds weight and a small plate reverb opens the space.

Engage CTL when the band builds. Back off for verses and quiet breaks.
