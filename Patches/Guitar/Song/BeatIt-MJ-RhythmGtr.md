# Beat It (Guitar) — Michael Jackson

Revised 2026-10-16 following the approved library audit.
Type: Song. Instrument: HSS Stratocaster.
Full board. Board Option: American Sound (bypassed; retained for legacy full-board builds).
Patch screen name: G-BeatItMJ. Patch VOL: 50.
Preset tempo: 139 BPM.

Beat It on HSS Strat: Deluxe-family rhythm crunch and a more saturated solo adaptation. The Super OD is the sole GP-5 solo gain addition. One fixed capture is a rhythm-first compromise; it does not switch to an EVH amp on CTL. Exact historical amp and preamp claims remain unverified.

## Revision intent

Keep the musical role of the original patch while reducing redundant gain, excessive space and unclear switching where identified in audit entry 141.
The module settings and section map below are the active revision.
Audited capture volumes and Michael’s explicit patch-level adjustments are preserved.
Use Super OD alone for the solo push; keep the rhythm-first Deluxe compromise.
No hardware audition or measured level match has been performed.

## Switching and section map

CTL blocks: DST, DLY.
- CTL off / loaded state: Lukather's riff and rhythm. GP-5 active: NR, EQ, RVB, N->S.
- First CTL press: Lead. GP-5 active: NR, DST, EQ, DLY, RVB, N->S.
- Press CTL again to return to the loaded block pattern, provided no shared MIDI block has been changed.
