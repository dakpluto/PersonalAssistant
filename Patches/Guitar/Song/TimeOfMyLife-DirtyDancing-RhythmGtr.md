# (I've Had) The Time of My Life (Rhythm Guitar) — Bill Medley & Jennifer Warnes

From the *Dirty Dancing* soundtrack (1987). About 108 BPM once the groove kicks in, est.
A slow, free-time intro, then a bright late-80s pop-soul groove that builds to the famous key-change finale.
The record is keys, sax, and vocals first. The guitar job is clean, chorused, funky 80s rhythm, plus a driven lead voice for the fills and the big ending.
Built from the song's overall sound. I haven't verified the exact guitars and amps on the record.
Instrument: Stratocaster (HSS). Position 2 or 4 for the funky rhythm. Bridge humbucker for the lead.
GP-5 only, Stratocaster (HSS), no pedalboard.

## AMP: J-120 CL (Jazz Chorus) + CAB: EVM112 IR (User IR 5)

A Roland JC is the definitive 80s studio clean: pristine, glassy, and built to pair with chorus.
EVM112 is the loaded guitar cab with a flat, hi-fi response. It keeps Nashville clean tones honest and doesn't add V30 honk.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 16.
Keeps the 16th-note muted scratching clean.

**PRE — COMP (Ross)**, always on.
Sustain: 40, VOL: 55.
80s funk rhythm is squashed. Sustain 40 gives even, percussive chucks.

**DST — Super OD (SD-1)**, on CTL.
Gain: 45, Tone: 58, VOL: 70.
The lead voice. An SD-1 into a clean JC gives a smooth, slightly compressed 80s lead. VOL 70 lifts it over the sax and keys.

**AMP — J-120 CL**, always on.
VOL: 60, Bass: 45, Middle: 50, Treble: 58, Bright: off.
Pristine clean. Treble 58 for sparkle, Bright off to avoid ice.

**CAB — User IR 5 (EVM112)**, always on.
VOL: 60.

**EQ — Guitar EQ 2**, always on.
100Hz: -3, 500Hz: -1, 1kHz: 0, 3kHz: +2, 6kHz: 0, VOL: 50.
The low cut and 500Hz dip clear space for the keys. +2 at 3kHz for chuck.

**MOD — A-Chorus**, always on.
Depth: 30, Rate: 0.8, Tone: 55.
Depth 30 is heavier than my usual light hand. This is one of the songs where an obvious 80s chorus is part of the sound.

**DLY — Analog**, on CTL.
Mix: 18, Time: 420ms, F.Back: 25, Trail: on.
About three-sixteenths at 108 BPM. It fattens the lead.

**RVB — Plate**, always on.
Mix: 18, Decay: 40, Damp: 45, Trail: on.
An 80s-style plate for polish.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: DST (Super OD), DLY (Analog).

- **CTL off** — Rhythm. Chorused, compressed JC clean for the funky chucking and the intro swells. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Lead. SD-1 plus analog delay for the fills and the key-change finale.

Engage CTL for fills and the final chorus lift. Stay off for the rhythm grooves.
