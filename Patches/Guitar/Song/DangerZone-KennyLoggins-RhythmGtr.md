# Danger Zone (Rhythm Guitar) — Kenny Loggins

From the *Top Gun* soundtrack (1986). About 158 BPM, est.
A Giorgio Moroder production. A driving synth pulse sits under big 80s hard-rock guitar: chunky power chords, then a screaming, delay-soaked lead.
Built from the song's overall sound. I haven't verified the exact guitars and amps on the record.
Instrument: Stratocaster (HSS). Bridge humbucker for both.
GP-5 only, Stratocaster (HSS), no pedalboard.

## AMP: Solo100 OD (Soldano SLO crunch) + CAB: V30112 IR (User IR 10)

The SLO crunch channel is the polished 80s LA hard-rock tone: tight, saturated, mid-forward. It's the right era of hot-rodded Marshall-style gain.
V30112 is the loaded guitar cab with enough upper-mid bark for a crunch tone. EVM112 would thin out once the amp breaks up.

## Module chain

**NR — Gate**, always on.
THRE: 38.
Tight stops for the chugged power chords.

**PRE — Boost (EP Booster)**, on CTL.
Gain: 50, +3dB: on, Bright: off.
Pushes the SLO into lead saturation and adds level.

**DST — off.** No pedal. The SLO handles the gain.

**AMP — Solo100 OD**, always on.
Gain: 58, PRES: 55, VOL: 60, Bass: 50, Middle: 58, Treble: 58.
Gain 58 is saturated crunch, not metal. Middle 58 for 80s mid push.

**CAB — User IR 10 (V30112)**, always on.
VOL: 60.

**EQ — Guitar EQ 2**, always on.
100Hz: -2, 500Hz: 0, 1kHz: +1, 3kHz: +1, 6kHz: -2, VOL: 50.
Tight lows, a slight presence lift, top trimmed.

**MOD — A-Chorus**, always on.
Depth: 15, Rate: 0.6, Tone: 50.
A light chorus for 80s width. It stays under the gain.

**DLY — Analog**, on CTL.
Mix: 22, Time: 380ms, F.Back: 30, Trail: on.
Lead delay. Mix 22 and F.Back 30 give the soaring 80s solo trail.

**RVB — Plate**, always on.
Mix: 14, Decay: 35, Damp: 50, Trail: on.
An 80s plate.

## CTL footswitch

On CTL: PRE (Boost), DLY (Analog).

- **CTL off** — Rhythm. Chunky SLO power chords with a touch of chorus. This is the resting state the patch loads into.
- **CTL on** — Lead. Boosted SLO plus a big analog trail for the screaming solo and melody lines.

Engage CTL for the solo and the lead hooks. Off for the power-chord rhythm.
